### Three Rootless Functional States of Consciousness

In the classification of mental states, consciousness is categorized not only by its moral qualities but also by its functional role in the cognitive process. Within this structure, there are **three rootless functional states of consciousness**. 

These states are designated as "rootless" because they are entirely devoid of the six primary psychological roots—the three unwholesome roots (greed, aversion, delusion) and the three wholesome roots (non-greed, non-aversion, wisdom). They are termed "functional" because, unlike moral or immoral actions, they possess no causal potency to produce future results, nor are they the passive results of prior actions. They arise merely to perform a specific, momentary operational function within a cognitive series and cease immediately once that function is fulfilled.

These three states are:
1. **Neutral-Feeling Sensory-Adverting Consciousness** (Direction of awareness at the five physical sense doors)
2. **Neutral-Feeling Mental-Adverting Consciousness** (Direction of awareness and determination at the mind door)
3. **Joyful Smile-Producing Consciousness** (The functional arising of a subtle smile in fully liberated beings)

---

### 1. Neutral-Feeling Sensory-Adverting Consciousness
The human organism interacts with the external environment through five physical sensory portals: the eye, ear, nose, tongue, and physical body. External sensory data—namely visible forms, sounds, odors, tastes, and tangible pressures—continually strike these respective doors. 

When sensory data strikes one of these portals, the continuous baseline state of passive consciousness, which depends upon the physical heart-base, is interrupted and halted. At that immediate moment, before the specific sensory cognition (such as visual or auditory awareness) can arise, this sensory-adverting consciousness arises dependent on the heart-base. Its sole function is to turn the stream of consciousness toward the sensory object that has presented itself. 

This state does not possess the capacity to discern, recognize, or evaluate what the object actually is; that recognition is performed by the subsequent stages of sensory cognition. Because it is an initial, structurally weak functional occurrence, it is accompanied by a **neutral feeling** (neither pleasant nor painful).

---

### 2. Neutral-Feeling Mental-Adverting Consciousness
The ongoing continuum of baseline consciousness located at the heart-base constitutes the mental portal. This consciousness performs two distinct operations depending on the pathway of the cognitive process:

* **In purely mental cognitive processes:** When an object presents itself directly to the mind without entering through the five physical senses, the baseline continuum is interrupted, and this state arises to perform the initial task of adverting (turning attention toward) that mental object, after which active cognitive phases follow.
* **In five-sense cognitive processes:** When an object has entered through the five physical senses, been perceived, and undergone intermediate investigation, this same consciousness arises to perform the function of **determination**. It decides and ascertains the nature of the object ("this object is of such a nature"), enabling the subsequent active response phase to take place. In this context, it is referred to as the determining consciousness.

Because this state arises at the preliminary stage of a cognitive process and lacks the capacity to become morally wholesome or unwholesome, it is a weak functional state accompanied by a **neutral feeling**.

---

### 3. Joyful Smile-Producing Consciousness
The term *hasita* refers specifically to a subtle or gentle smile. This consciousness arises solely to generate a gentle smile. Because the arising of a smile involves gladness, this state is accompanied by a **feeling of mental joy**. Although it operates within the active response phase of consciousness, it is completely free from wholesome or unwholesome roots and leaves no causal residue.

This state **arises exclusively in fully perfected, liberated beings**. When such an individual observes a condition of cyclic worldly existence in another person or experiences joy reflecting upon their own complete liberation from such states, this consciousness arises to produce a gentle smile.

#### The Six Types of Laughter and Smiling
Laughter and smiling manifest in six progressive degrees:
1. **Subtle Smile (*Sita*):** The eyes open slightly, and a serene joy is visible on the countenance.
2. **Gentle Smile (*Hasita*):** The tips of the teeth are slightly visible, without any vocal sound.
*(Fully enlightened teachers, solitary enlightened beings, and perfected beings smile exclusively through these first two quiet forms. The smile-producing functional consciousness generates only these two).*
3. **Audible Smile (*Vihasita*):** A gentle smile accompanied by a soft, pleasant sound.
4. **Moderate Laugh (*Upahasita*):** Laughter accompanied by slight movement or shaking of the head and shoulders.
*(Virtuous unliberated persons and noble disciples undergoing training smile and laugh in these two moderate ways).*
5. **Laugh with Tears (*Apahasita*):** Excessive laughter accompanied by the shedding of tears.
6. **Boisterous Laugh (*Atihasita*):** Unrestrained laughter accompanied by loud noise and the shaking of the entire body.
*(Individuals with undeveloped minds and unrefined dispositions laugh in these last two excessive ways).*

#### The States of Consciousness Generating a Smile Across Beings
* **Ordinary, unliberated persons** smile through 8 possible states: 4 joyful states rooted in attachment and 4 joyful, morally wholesome states.
* **Noble disciples undergoing training** smile through 6 possible states: 2 joyful states rooted in attachment (dissociated from erroneous views) and 4 joyful wholesome states.
* **Perfected, fully liberated beings** smile through 5 possible states: the 1 rootless smile-producing functional state and 4 higher joyful functional states.
* **Supreme and solitary enlightened beings** smile exclusively through 2 higher joyful functional states associated with wisdom.

### Structural Context within Consciousness Classification
Within the complete classification of mental states, there are **20 functional states** in total (these 3 rootless functional states and 17 higher functional states). Except for the two adverting states (sensory-adverting and mental-adverting), **all remaining 18 functional states occur exclusively in fully liberated beings.**

In total, there are **18 rootless states of consciousness**:
* 7 unwholesome resultants (passive outcomes of negative actions)
* 8 wholesome resultants (passive outcomes of positive actions)
* 3 rootless functional states

Because all 18 rootless states are structurally weak, they operate directly without internal or external prompting. In terms of feeling, these 18 states comprise:
* 14 states accompanied by neutral feeling
* 2 states accompanied by mental joy
* 1 state accompanied by physical pleasure
* 1 state accompanied by physical pain

In the broader division of consciousness, the 12 unwholesome states together with the 18 rootless states constitute the **30 non-sublime states of consciousness**. The remaining states (counted as 59, or 91 by detailed calculation) are classified as the **sublime or beautiful states of consciousness**.

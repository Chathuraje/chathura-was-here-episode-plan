### 1. Root-Endowed Resultant States of Consciousness

In the analysis of human experience within the sensory sphere, intentional wholesome actions generate corresponding experiential consequences. These resultant states of consciousness are known as **root-endowed resultant states** (or great resultants). Just as intentional wholesome actions are structured by their affective tone, their degree of cognitive understanding, and whether they occur spontaneously or through prompting, their resulting fruitions manifest in eight exact variations:

1. A joyful state accompanied by understanding, arising spontaneously
2. A joyful state accompanied by understanding, arising through prompting
3. A joyful state devoid of understanding, arising spontaneously
4. A joyful state devoid of understanding, arising through prompting
5. A neutral state accompanied by understanding, arising spontaneously
6. A neutral state accompanied by understanding, arising through prompting
7. A neutral state devoid of understanding, arising spontaneously
8. A neutral state devoid of understanding, arising through prompting

The consequences of wholesome sensory actions are not limited to these eight. The eight baseline, rootless resultant states (such as primary seeing and hearing) also stem from these same wholesome actions, yielding a total of sixteen wholesome resultant states.

#### Rebirth-Linking and Life-Continuum Functions
These resultant states operate in two distinct modes: the **rebirth-linking function** (establishing the initial consciousness of a new life) and the **life-continuum function** (the passive experiencing of results throughout an ongoing lifetime).

The nature of the initial consciousness at birth is determined by the strength and cognitive quality of the prior wholesome action, classified into four levels:

* **Superior Three-Rooted Action:** Wholesome actions performed with strong non-attachment, non-aversion, and mature cognitive understanding (wisdom). These generate an initial rebirth consciousness accompanied by understanding (one of the four understanding-endowed resultants). Individuals born of such actions possess the capacity to cultivate wisdom, practice meditation, benefit from noble companionship, and attain meditative absorptions and stages of liberation. When individuals lacking these foundational root-endowments attempt intensive contemplative practices without success, their minds may become corrupted and fall into mental derangement.
* **Inferior Three-Rooted Action:** Wholesome actions performed with weak, rudimentary understanding. Lacking the strength to produce a three-rooted rebirth, they yield an initial consciousness devoid of understanding (one of the four two-rooted resultants). Such individuals obtain only modest wellbeing in the human realm and must exert strenuous effort to secure it.
* **Superior Two-Rooted Action:** Wholesome actions driven purely by strong non-attachment and non-aversion, but without explicit cognitive understanding. These likewise produce an initial consciousness devoid of understanding, yielding a condition functionally similar to that of the inferior three-rooted action.
* **Inferior Two-Rooted Action:** Actions undertaken without understanding, motivated by social rivalry, imitation, or the pursuit of praise, supported only by weak positive dispositions. Because these actions lack the potency to generate a root-endowed resultant, they produce an unrooted neutral resultant as the initial consciousness. This accounts for individuals born with physical impairments, poverty, and dull intellects, who are incapable of fully appreciating favorable experiences or realizing the higher fruits of human life.

**Experiential Fruition During an Ongoing Lifetime:**
Throughout life, when desirable sensory objects are encountered, basic sensory awareness (such as visual or auditory registration) arises first as an unrooted wholesome resultant. The subsequent experience of joy and satisfaction is then processed through the root-endowed resultant states. Because every individual inherits a mixed legacy of wholesome and unwholesome past actions, all twenty-three sensory resultant states (seven unwholesome unrooted, eight wholesome unrooted, and eight root-endowed resultants) are experienced across the lifespan by ordinary and fully liberated persons alike.

### 2. Root-Endowed Functional States of Consciousness

Liberated persons continue to perform benevolent deeds recognized as virtuous—such as teaching, honoring the worthy, and listening to profound discourse. However, because they have entirely eradicated craving and the roots of future rebirth, their actions produce no future karmic consequences. Because these conscious states merely carry out an act without leaving causal residue, they are designated as **functional states of consciousness**.

These functional states correspond directly to the eight wholesome states:

1. A joyful state accompanied by understanding, arising spontaneously
2. A joyful state accompanied by understanding, arising through prompting
3. A joyful state devoid of understanding, arising spontaneously
4. A joyful state devoid of understanding, arising through prompting
5. A neutral state accompanied by understanding, arising spontaneously
6. A neutral state accompanied by understanding, arising through prompting
7. A neutral state devoid of understanding, arising spontaneously
8. A neutral state devoid of understanding, arising through prompting

In the active cognitive phases of a liberated person's sensory and mental processes, one of these eight states arises. Actions performed with deliberate mindfulness and understanding operate through the four understanding-accompanied functional states, whereas actions performed out of habit or routine operate through the four states devoid of explicit understanding.

*(Regarding the supreme teacher, one tradition holds that only understanding-accompanied functional states arise, while other teachers maintain that the spontaneous smile-producing consciousness and the four functional states devoid of explicit understanding may also occur).*

### 3. Distribution of the 54 Sensory-Sphere States Across Stages of Spiritual Attainment

The fifty-four states of sensory-sphere consciousness occur across different stages of attainment as follows:

1. **Unawakened Persons (45 states):** All 12 unwholesome states, 17 unrooted states (excluding the unique smile-producing state of the liberated), 8 wholesome states, and 8 root-endowed resultant states.
2. **First and Second Stages of Awakening (40 states):** The 4 unwholesome states bound to erroneous views and the 1 state of skeptical doubt are eradicated, leaving 7 unwholesome states, 17 unrooted states, 8 wholesome states, and 8 root-endowed resultant states.
3. **Third Stage of Awakening (38 states):** The 4 view-bound unwholesome states, the state of doubt, and the 2 states of aversive distress are eliminated, leaving 5 unwholesome states, 17 unrooted states, 8 wholesome states, and 8 root-endowed resultant states.
4. **Fully Liberated Persons (34 states):** With all 12 unwholesome states eradicated and all 8 wholesome states transformed, there remain 18 unrooted states (including the smile-producing state), 8 root-endowed functional states, and 8 root-endowed resultant states.
5. **The Supreme Teacher (29 states):** 17 unrooted states (excluding the smile-producing state), 8 root-endowed resultant states, and the 4 functional states accompanied by understanding. *(Under the alternative view, the smile-producing state and the 4 functional states devoid of understanding are also present).*

### 4. Classification of the 54 Sensory-Sphere States

The fifty-four sensory-sphere conscious states comprise:
* **Unwholesome States:** 12
* **Unrooted States:** 18
* **Elevated/Beautiful States:** 24 (8 wholesome, 8 resultant, and 8 functional)

Across the full set of fifty-four states, the distributions resolve into exact categories:
* **By Causal Type:** 8 wholesome, 12 unwholesome, 23 resultant, and 11 functional states.
* **By Affective Tone:** 18 joyful, 32 neutral, 2 displeased/aversive, 1 bodily pleasurable, and 1 bodily painful state.
* **By Mode of Origination:** 37 spontaneous (unprompted) and 17 prompted states.
* **Within the 24 Elevated States:** Exactly 12 are joyful and 12 neutral; 12 accompanied by understanding and 12 devoid of understanding; 12 spontaneous and 12 prompted.

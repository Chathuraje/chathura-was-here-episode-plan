### The Resultant States of Immaterial Absorption and Non-Physical Existence

When an individual cultivates the four immaterial meditative absorptions—the dimension of infinite space, the dimension of infinite consciousness, the dimension of nothingness, and the dimension of neither-perception-nor-non-perception—and passes away without losing these mental attainments, the causal force of that meditative cultivation produces a corresponding rebirth in an immaterial realm. 

In this existence, the initial moment of rebirth-linking, the continuous passive stream of life-continuity, and the final moment of death all occur through the four corresponding **resultant states of immaterial consciousness**.

In ordinary sensory and physical realms, consciousness depends upon and operates through a physical body. However, because the practitioner developed these absorptions by viewing physical form as flawed and repulsive, existence in the immaterial realm arises entirely without a material body. There is only a continuum of mind and mental factors. 

While ordinary sensory beings experience continuous physical suffering due to bodily afflictions, hunger, and physical upkeep, non-physical beings are entirely free from physical distress. Their state of peacefulness closely resembles the ultimate peace of liberation. 

The pleasure of sensory beings depends upon external stimulation through the senses, much like plain cooked rice that requires additional foods to have flavor. In contrast, the state of immaterial existence is intrinsically peaceful and satisfying within itself, comparable to a slice of cake that requires nothing added to be delicious; the life itself is a state of ease.

The lifespan of beings in these non-physical realms is exceptionally long:
* In the dimension of infinite space, the lifespan is 20,000 great cosmic cycles.
* In the dimension of infinite consciousness, the lifespan is 40,000 great cosmic cycles.
* In the dimension of nothingness, the lifespan is 60,000 great cosmic cycles.
* In the dimension of neither-perception-nor-non-perception, the lifespan is 84,000 great cosmic cycles.

### Inoperative Immaterial Consciousness and the Attainment of Cessation

Fully liberated individuals also generate these four immaterial states of absorption. However, because they have eradicated all mental defilements, their states of consciousness carry no potential to produce future rebirth or karmic results. Therefore, these states are designated as the four **inoperative (functional) states of immaterial consciousness**.

There is a unique meditative state known as the **attainment of cessation**, which is accessible only to non-returning and fully liberated individuals who have mastered all levels of material and immaterial absorption. This attainment is a temporary state of existing entirely without consciousness or mental factors. Fully liberated individuals cultivate the four inoperative immaterial absorptions both as a necessary step to enter this total cessation of mind and as a way to dwell in peaceful well-being within the present life.

### Prerequisites and the Cognitive Sequence of Meditative Absorption

Deep meditative absorptions can be attained only by individuals who are endowed at birth with the three positive root-conditions: non-greed, non-hatred, and wisdom (non-delusion). Individuals born without these three foundational roots cannot attain these states.

The cognitive sequence through which consciousness transitions from ordinary awareness into meditative absorption differs according to the sharpness of the practitioner's intellect:
1. **The practitioner of gradual intellect:** Prior to absorption, four active mental moments occur in sequence: a preparatory moment, an access moment, a conformity moment, and a transitional moment that shifts consciousness beyond the sensory domain. The state of meditative absorption immediately follows.
2. **The practitioner of sharp intellect:** This practitioner does not require the initial preparatory moment; only three active mental moments (access, conformity, and transition) occur before absorption arises.

When gathered together, the four material-realm absorptions and four immaterial-realm absorptions are termed the "eight meditative attainments." When the material-realm absorptions are analyzed under the fivefold system alongside the four immaterial absorptions, they are termed the "nine successive meditative attainments."

### Structural Factors of Immaterial Consciousness

All twelve immaterial states of consciousness—comprising the 4 wholesome, 4 resultant, and 4 inoperative states—contain only two meditative structural factors: **equanimity (neutral feeling)** and **one-pointedness (mental unification)**. 

Because of this two-factor composition, all twelve immaterial states structurally belong to the **fifth level of meditative absorption**. In their specific characteristics, they are accompanied by equanimity, conjoined with knowledge, and are induced (requiring intentional cultivation). 

Although all immaterial states share the structural classification of the fifth absorption, each higher absorption is progressively more refined, subtle, and elevated than the one below it due to the increasing subtlety of its mental object.

### The Classification of Sublime and Mundane Consciousness

In this philosophical system, these states of consciousness are classified into broader structural categories:

* **Sublime (Elevated) Consciousness (27 states):** This group comprises the 15 material-realm states and the 12 immaterial-realm states. They are termed "sublime" because they do not belong to ordinary consciousness, but are generated and sustained exclusively by advanced individuals.
  * Causal division: 9 wholesome states, 9 resultant states, and 9 inoperative states.
  * Level of absorption: 3 states each for the first, second, third, and fourth levels of absorption (totaling 12 states), and 15 states belonging to the fifth level of absorption.
  * Affective tone: 12 states accompanied by pleasant mental feeling (joy), and 15 states accompanied by neutral equanimity.

* **Mundane Consciousness (81 states):** This encompasses all consciousness within the conditioned world, combining the 54 sensory-realm states, 15 material-realm states, and 12 immaterial-realm states.
  * Causal division: 17 wholesome states, 32 resultant states, and 20 inoperative states.
  * Affective tone: 31 states accompanied by pleasant feeling (including pleasant bodily sensation), 47 states accompanied by neutral equanimity, and 3 states accompanied by unpleasant feeling (including painful bodily sensation).


# CHATHURA WAS HERE — PHILOSOPHY RESEARCH & DETAIL EXTRACTION WORKFLOW

## Purpose

Use the selected Buddhist or Abhidhamma concept to create a reliable research document for later story development.

This stage does **not** create the documentary story.

The workflow is:

**Find Sources → Understand → Verify → Explain in Sinhala → Convert to Universal Philosophy → Extract Human and Story-Relevant Details → Final Accuracy Check**

# STEP 1 — Find the Related Source Sections

Find the chapters and sections from both books that are directly or strongly related to the selected concept.

Do not use entire books unnecessarily.

Collect only the relevant chapters, explanations, examples, definitions, and connected concepts.

Keep track of the source, chapter, section, page if available, and important terminology.

If the two sources differ in explanation, do not silently merge them. Preserve the distinction.

Then provide the relevant material from both sources to Gemini.

---

# FINAL DOCUMENT FORMAT

The completed document should contain only these three sections:

## 1. Sinhala With Buddhist Terms

A clear, refined, source-faithful explanation of the selected Buddhist or Abhidhamma concept in Sinhala.

## 2. English Without Buddhist Terms

The same underlying philosophy explained as universal human experience without relying on Buddhist terminology.

## 3. Details for Future Story Development

A refined explanation of the relevant human experiences, misunderstandings, causes, consequences, behaviours, real-world situations, people, environments, visual possibilities, emotional questions, and possible relevance to Chathura's longer philosophical journey.

## GEMINI PROMPT 01 — SINHALA EXPLANATION

*Using only the Buddhist and Abhidhamma source material provided above, create a clear and understandable Sinhala explanation of this concept.*

*Preserve the important Buddhist, Abhidhamma, Pāli, and Sinhala technical terminology, but explain what those terms actually mean in natural Sinhala.*

*Do not simply give definitions.*

*Explain the full idea as a connected process. Explain what causes what, what happens next, how the different concepts relate to each other, what happens within human experience, what people normally misunderstand, and why the concept matters.*

*Where useful, use simple everyday human examples, but do not introduce examples that change or distort the original teaching.*

*Compare both sources carefully. If one source provides additional detail, include it. If the sources differ, clearly explain the difference instead of inventing a reconciliation.*

*Do not add Buddhist teachings that are not supported by the supplied source material.*

*Write mainly in clear connected paragraphs rather than bullet points.*

*The final explanation should be understandable to a Sinhala reader who has little formal Abhidhamma knowledge while remaining accurate to the sources.*

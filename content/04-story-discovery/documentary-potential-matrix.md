# Documentary Potential Matrix

This matrix scores each of the 148 accepted idea cards as a **starting point for finding real documentary stories**. It does not measure religious, philosophical or moral importance. A low score can belong to a profound idea that simply offers little to film or research.

## How to read it

Each card is scored from 1 to 5 on eight criteria (maximum 40):

| Criterion | Question asked |
|---|---|
| Human tension | Is there a real contradiction, choice, need, loss or unresolved relationship? |
| Active reality | Could the question appear where something is changing, beginning, ending or being decided now? |
| Visual world | Could it show through actions, environments, objects, weather, work, movement or ritual? |
| Place and journey | Could Chathura naturally travel into a place where the question exists? |
| Sri Lankan breadth | Could it arise across many parts of Sri Lankan life without stereotyping anyone? |
| Ethical readiness | Can it be explored without diagnosing, blaming, exploiting or morally classifying anyone? (5 = low risk) |
| Independence from doctrine | Would the question matter to a viewer who knows nothing about Buddhism? |
| Distinctiveness | Does it offer something different from stronger cards already in the bank? |

**Provisional judgements.** *Active reality*, *Place and journey* and *Sri Lankan breadth* are provisional for every card. They cannot be confirmed until real research finds real situations. *Ethical readiness* is also provisional: the risk depends on the particular people involved.

**The total is not objective.** It is a sorting aid. Tiers were assigned editorially, using the scores together with the ethical reading and the overlap analysis. A card can score well and still sit in Tier B because a specific caution needs a stronger real situation.

**Tiers**

- **Tier A — Strong research starting point.** A researcher can begin looking for real situations now.
- **Tier B — Useful with a stronger real-world situation.** Worth pursuing only if research finds a situation that carries the question clearly and ethically.
- **Tier C — Supporting philosophical lens.** Helps interpret material found under other questions. It should not start research on its own.
- **Do not advance — unresolved ethical or fidelity problem.** Not to be used for research until the concern is resolved. The card's status in the idea bank is unchanged.

**Relationship notes.** The reason column begins with the card's role from [overlap-map.md](overlap-map.md):
- *Lead of Gxx* marks the recommended research entry point for an overlap group.
- *Supporting to [ID]* means the card is normally researched under that lead. It keeps its own row and its own limits.
- *Related, researched separately* marks cards that look similar but ask a different question.
- *Distinct idea* has no close overlap.
- **Shortlisted** marks the 24 questions in [research-shortlist.md](research-shortlist.md). *Reserve* marks candidates for the next cycle.

Merged aliases do not have rows. Each is represented by its surviving card; see Appendix B of [philosophy-map.md](philosophy-map.md). Held cards are excluded.

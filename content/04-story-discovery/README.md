# Story Discovery

This folder turns the idea bank into a starting point for finding **real** documentary stories in Sri Lanka for *Chathura Was Here — Stories • Journeys • Memories*.

It sits between two stages:

```
Abhidhamma sources  →  Idea bank  →  Story discovery  →  Story research  →  Story selection  →  Screenplay
 (content/02-concepts)     (content/03-idea-bank)   (this folder)     (leads, verified)    (human decision)
```

## Purpose

The idea bank holds 148 accepted human ideas drawn from Abhidhamma sources. Each is a conceptual interpretation, not a story. This layer:

1. groups those ideas into 14 **human territories** that make sense without Buddhist terms;
2. identifies where ideas **overlap** and which card to start from;
3. **scores** every accepted idea for its usefulness as a documentary research starting point;
4. selects a balanced **shortlist of 24 open questions** for the first research cycle, each with a research brief;
5. provides a **story-lead template** and an **instruction for the research agent** who will look for real situations.

## How this differs from the idea bank

| Idea bank | Story discovery |
|---|---|
| Records what the sources support and how faithfully an idea represents them. | Asks which faithful ideas are good places to *start looking* for real stories. |
| Organised by source concept (C001–C102). | Organised by human territory (T01–T14). |
| Judges fidelity. | Judges research potential, ethics and balance. |
| Status: accepted, held or merged. | Readiness tier: A, B, C or Do not advance. The idea-bank status is never changed. |

Philosophical depth and documentary potential are different things. A low score here says nothing about an idea's importance.

## How future researchers should use these files

1. **Choose one question** from the shortlist (SQ01–SQ24).
2. **Read its brief completely**, then the lead card and every supporting card it lists, including their *Limits and alternatives*.
3. **Read the territory entry** in the philosophy map and the overlap group entry, so that distinctions and cautions carry forward.
4. **Research reality** following the research agent instruction. Search, verify and record.
5. **Open a story-lead card** for each real possibility, using the template. Keep facts, participant accounts, unverified claims and interpretations separate.
6. **Report back**, including leads rejected and any case where reality contradicted the idea.

If a shortlisted question proves unworkable, choose a replacement from the reserve list at the end of the shortlist, preferably from the same territory.

## What this stage does not establish

- No real person, community, place or event has been identified or verified.
- No idea has been shown to occur in anyone's life.
- No story has been selected.
- No idea has been assigned to an episode, season, object, coordinate or chronology.
- No doctrinal claim has been accepted as fact, and no scientific equivalence has been claimed.
- Scores and tiers are editorial judgements, not measurements.

## Rules that do not change

**Hypothetical directions are not verified stories.** Every *possible everyday expression* in an idea card, and every *story signal* or *possible form of reality* in this folder, describes a kind of situation to look for. None of them is evidence that such a situation exists, and none may be passed downstream as a story.

**Story research comes before screenplay development.** A screenplay agent may only work from a story lead that has been verified by research, has documented consent, and has been selected by a human decision. No one writes scenes, narration or story arcs from an idea card or from this folder.

**The philosophy stays underneath.** It shapes the questions Chathura carries. It never decides what real people's lives mean, and every documentary must stand without it.

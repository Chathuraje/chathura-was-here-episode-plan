## 4. Why the story exists independently

Press reporting describes small handloom factories and family-run businesses closing after the Covid period and the financial crisis when loan repayments became unmanageable, skilled weavers migrating abroad, experienced craftsmen retiring, and businesses operating at a fraction of previous capacity while demand remains. Sri Lankan weaving is described as divided between the Thalagune and migrant weaving traditions.

If every reference to the idea bank, to the series structure and to the philosophy were removed, what would remain is the situation in section 5 and the activity in section 9. That is the test this lead has to pass, and it is the reason the lead was opened.

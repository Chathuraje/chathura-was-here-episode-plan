## 4. Why the story exists independently

Published accounts describe gem mining in Sri Lanka as commonly worked by small partnerships, locally called attus, in which investors put up money and miners put in labour, with finds shared by long-standing agreement more often sealed by handshake than on paper. One account gives the customary division as one fifth to the landowner, one fifth to the financier and the remainder to the workers. A pit is typically worked by about four people: one filling baskets with the gem-bearing gravel called illam, one lifting, one receiving and one carrying to the washing point.

If every reference to the idea bank, to the series structure and to the philosophy were removed, what would remain is the situation in section 5 and the activity in section 9. That is the test this lead has to pass, and it is the reason the lead was opened.

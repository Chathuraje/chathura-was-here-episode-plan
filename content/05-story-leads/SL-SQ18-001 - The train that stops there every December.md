## 4. Why the story exists independently

On 26 December 2004 the Ocean Queen Express was struck by the tsunami at Peraliya; reported death tolls are around 1,000, including passengers and local residents who had taken shelter in the train. Each year on the anniversary the same service stops at the spot for a commemoration; the twentieth anniversary was marked there on 26 December 2024. Reporting records that the same guard who survived the disaster, Wanigaratna Karunatilleke, continued working the line. A memorial, the Tsunami Honganji Viharaya, with an 18.5-metre Buddha figure, stands at the site.

If every reference to the idea bank, to the series structure and to the philosophy were removed, what would remain is the situation in section 5 and the activity in section 9. That is the test this lead has to pass, and it is the reason the lead was opened.

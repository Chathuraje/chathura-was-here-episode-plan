## 4. Why the story exists independently

Blood donation in Sri Lanka is described as entirely voluntary and non-remunerated, coordinated by the National Blood Transfusion Service under the Ministry of Health, which holds a WHO Collaborating Centre designation and operates a network of blood banks across the country. Published figures describe around 450,000 units collected annually, with a large majority collected through mobile campaigns held in communities, schools, temples and workplaces.

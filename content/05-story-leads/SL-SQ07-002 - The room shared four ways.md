## 4. Why the story exists independently

Reporting describes Katunayake as a destination for young women from the country's poorer provinces for about forty years, working across more than eighty factories in the zone, most living in crowded boarding houses or rented rooms with shared facilities. 2025 reporting gives an average monthly wage of around 35,000 rupees, against a living-wage estimate from the Asia Floor Wage Alliance of around 94,000 rupees. Reporting also describes long hours and production targets that push workers into overtime.

If every reference to the idea bank, to the series structure and to the philosophy were removed, what would remain is the situation in section 5 and the activity in section 9. That is the test this lead has to pass, and it is the reason the lead was opened.

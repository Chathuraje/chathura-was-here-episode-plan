## 17. Ethical risks

The obvious framing, that the fishermen are faking, is unfair and must be refused. They are selling the only thing a visitor wants to buy. Nobody should be filmed in a way that mocks them, and the film must not pretend to show authentic fishing if it is filming a paid pose.

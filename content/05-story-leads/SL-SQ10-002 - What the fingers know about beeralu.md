## 4. Why the story exists independently

Beeralu is a bobbin-lace technique introduced in the colonial period and worked mainly by women along the southern coast. Published accounts describe it as slowly disappearing because the work is long and arduous, alongside recent revival efforts including state promotion and use by fashion labels.

If every reference to the idea bank, to the series structure and to the philosophy were removed, what would remain is the situation in section 5 and the activity in section 9. That is the test this lead has to pass, and it is the reason the lead was opened.

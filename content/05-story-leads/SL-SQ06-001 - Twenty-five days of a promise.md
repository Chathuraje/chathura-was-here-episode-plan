## 4. Why the story exists independently

The Nallur festival runs for twenty-five consecutive days in August or September and is described as the longest continuous religious festival in the country, drawing very large numbers including Tamil expatriates returning to fulfil vows. Devotees are described as observing rigorous personal vows, many walking barefoot throughout, and some carrying kavadi. Festival events include the chariot festival, the kavadi festival, the water-cutting festival and the fire-walking festival.

If every reference to the idea bank, to the series structure and to the philosophy were removed, what would remain is the situation in section 5 and the activity in section 9. That is the test this lead has to pass, and it is the reason the lead was opened.

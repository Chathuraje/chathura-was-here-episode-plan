## 5. What is happening now

The Ministerial Consultative Committee on Justice recorded 1,131,818 pending cases across the courts at its meeting of 27 February 2025. Reporting describes criminal prosecutions typically taking around seventeen years and civil litigation running for decades. Prison figures reported alongside this describe a system designed for around 10,000 inmates holding over 40,000, with roughly two-thirds awaiting trial, and around 8,500 inmates held because of delays in Government Analyst reports. A reform package has been proposed, including more appellate judges, new High Courts, provincial appellate courts and additional court staff, and the Supreme Court held its first fully paperless trial on 26 March.

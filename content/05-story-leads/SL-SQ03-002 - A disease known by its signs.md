## 4. Why the story exists independently

CKDu among paddy farmers was first reported in 1994 and is endemic in parts of the dry zone. Published prevalence figures in some districts run between about 15 and 23 per cent, associated with farming occupations. The recognised causes of chronic kidney disease, including hypertension, diabetes and glomerulonephritis, are not associated with it. Research has examined drinking water from wells, agrochemical exposure, groundwater chemistry and biomarkers, without an agreed cause.

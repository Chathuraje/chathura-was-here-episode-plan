## 4. Why the story exists independently

Suwa Seriya is described as launching in July 2016 in the Southern and Western provinces and expanding island-wide, providing free pre-hospital emergency care with 297 ambulance units, a workforce of about 1,500, and a reported average response time of 15 minutes 32 seconds. A Diploma in Paramedical Sciences for emergency medical technicians was developed with the Faculty of Medicine at the University of Kelaniya and the Sri Lanka College of Emergency Physicians.

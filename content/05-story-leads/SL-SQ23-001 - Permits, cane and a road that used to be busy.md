## 4. Why the story exists independently

A dated newspaper investigation reports the cane village contracting from roughly 1,500 families a decade earlier to about 20 active families, with named officeholders and craftsmen interviewed, including the secretary of the Radawadunna Cane Craftsmen's Association. Raw cane requires government permits and comes from Polonnaruwa, Ampara and Mannar; the article reports allegations that larger operators obtain cane through political connections while smaller craftspeople face supply constraints, and that some businesses import cane from Malaysia.

## 4. Why the story exists independently

The hanaporuwa is a bow-driven vertical-lap cutting device described as the traditional Sri Lankan gem-cutting machine. Published accounts report that both hanaporuwa and modern Sri Lankan-made machines are in use in Ratnapura lapidary shops, and that traditional methods remain prized for cabochon cutting of star sapphires. Formal cutting classes are recorded as beginning in the early 1970s at the Gem Bureau in Ratnapura.

If every reference to the idea bank, to the series structure and to the philosophy were removed, what would remain is the situation in section 5 and the activity in section 9. That is the test this lead has to pass, and it is the reason the lead was opened.

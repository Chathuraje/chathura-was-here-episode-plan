## 4. Why the story exists independently

Henawala is described as the last village in Sri Lanka where hana fibre weaving is still practised. Published accounts state that of roughly 95-100 families in the village, about 10 remain actively engaged in the weaving, and that several master weavers addressed as gurunanse are in their seventies and eighties.

If every reference to the idea bank, to the series structure and to the philosophy were removed, what would remain is the situation in section 5 and the activity in section 9. That is the test this lead has to pass, and it is the reason the lead was opened.

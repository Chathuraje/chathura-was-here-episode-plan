## 4. Why the story exists independently

Laksha work applies resin from the lac insect to turned wood. In the niyapoten veda technique the lac is drawn out and severed with the thumbnail, and the finished piece is polished with a talipot leaf and coconut oil. Published accounts name Hapuwida in Matale as a village with a handful of remaining artisans, and at least one press piece is titled as being about Sri Lanka's last lacquer workers.

If every reference to the idea bank, to the series structure and to the philosophy were removed, what would remain is the situation in section 5 and the activity in section 9. That is the test this lead has to pass, and it is the reason the lead was opened.

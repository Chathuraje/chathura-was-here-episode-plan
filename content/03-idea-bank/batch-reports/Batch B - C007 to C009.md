## Held questions

1. **C008-I02 (held).** Can a card on acting with doubt be made faithful? Pradeepika (line 36) excludes ordinary uncertainty from the unwholesome doubt, which concerns religious objects only. Unless a later concept treats doubt more generally, keep this held.
2. **C007: harm done while laughing** (Margaya line 11). The source says killing animals for sport with laughter is still hatred-rooted with "subtle" hatred, yet both sources say these states always carry painful feeling. How laughter and painful feeling coexist is not explained. Not carded.
3. **C007-I03 (grief).** Accepted as a limited analogy, but a reviewer should confirm the ethical limits are adequate before any research agent uses it. The doctrinal classification of grief as unwholesome must never be applied to real mourners.
4. **C009: rebirth and disability** (Pradeepika lines 168–172). The text links congenital blindness, deafness and muteness to weak wholesome action. This is doctrinal and ethically harmful if carried into ideas; flagged so no later agent draws on it.
5. **C009: functional states unexplained.** Both extracts list the three rootless functional states (including the laughter-producing state) but neither explains them. The explanation appears to sit in Concept 10 (`Concept 10 - අහේතුක ක්‍රියා සිත් තුන`). The C009 source note does not cover them.
6. **C008: Margaya's remedy for doubt** (line 18) says criticism of matters beyond ordinary knowledge increases doubt and faith is the cure. Not carded; any future use must not frame questioning as harmful.

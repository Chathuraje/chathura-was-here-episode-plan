## Held questions

1. **Comfort and hardship as support for good and harm** (Pradeepika lines 5495–5719, PDF 406–417 / printed 395–406). Bodily pleasure, pain, climate, food and lodging are named as decisive support for faith and good acts, and also for killing, stealing, conceit and longing. No card was made, because the harmful side is an unexplained list and the idea overlaps C086-I03 and C080-I01. The coordinator may want to add these lines as supporting passages to those cards.
2. **Harm prompting good** (lines 5313–5363). Examples are giving in the hope of pleasures, practising to remove known anger, and doing good after harm to escape its results. This may be useful supporting material for C055-I01 and C033-I02.
3. **Self-imposed hardship from devotion** (lines 5009–5041). Faith, virtue, learning, generosity and wisdom lead people to "burn themselves" in practice and in seeking the means to give. This could be a future card on costly devotion. It is not made here, to keep to three distinct mechanisms.
4. **Imitation of grave acts** (line 5229). One way a heinous act supports another is seeing someone else do it and judging it right. It is used in C082-I02 only in generalised form. Any research use must avoid the matricide framing.
5. **C082-I01 and clinical language.** The card must not be merged with, or presented as, rumination or anxiety research without separate evidence.

## Suspected source text errors or oddities

- **Editor-flagged numbering in Pradeepika** (all left unchanged in the file). Line 4037: nissaya answer-set items run 7 → 9, with no item 8. Line 4593: upanissaya items are numbered 2, 4, 4. Line 7060: the seventh āhāra answer-set is numbered 8. Line 8730: the atthi answer-sets run 9 → 12 → 11 → 12 → 13, so "12" appears twice (lines 8732 and 8822).
- **Pradeepika line 224.** The hetu 2nd answer-set lists "අදෙෂ, අමොහ යන හේතු තුන" for three-rooted minds and omits අලොභ.
- **Pradeepika line 2768.** The samanantara 5th answer-set (indeterminate → indeterminate) glosses the result as "අකුසල ස්කන්ධයන්ට". Context requires indeterminate aggregates.
- **Pradeepika line 6135.** The pacchājāta 2nd answer-set (unwholesome) quotes the Pāli as "පච්ඡාජාතා කුසලාඛන්ධා". Context requires අකුසලා.
- **Pradeepika line 1254.** The list of eight co-occurring forces gives "ආරම්මණ පුරෙජාත" twice. Compare line 1461, which gives "ආරම්මණ පුරෙජාතාවිගත".
- **Pradeepika lines 8480 and 8514.** Rebirth-linking minds are given as 19, then as 15 within the same presence answer-set. This is probably five-constituent (15) against all planes (19), but it is not stated.
- **Pradeepika line 2226.** It says of the non-percipient being "දෙවන ජාතියෙහි … මහා කල්ප පන්සියයකින් පසු". The wording is unclear.
- **Margaya line 104.** It introduces decisive support as "නිශ්ශ්‍රය ප්‍රත්‍යය තුන් ආකාර". This should be උපනිශ්ශ්‍රය.
- **Margaya line 74.** "දෙපනස් සාධිපති ජජවනය" is a typographical duplication.
- **Genuine source difference.** For co-nascent faculty, Margaya line 156 lists seven mental faculties and omits life faculty. Pradeepika lines 7134 and 7164 list eight. On presence versus non-disappearance, Margaya line 200 treats them as identical, while Pradeepika line 9032 gives a distinct emphasis. These are recorded, not resolved.
- OCR-style letter confusions throughout Pradeepika (for example ඥන for ඥාන, වෙෂ or වෙදෂ for ද්වේෂ). The meaning is recoverable.

## Possible overlaps

- **C082-I01 — When a troubled state becomes its own fuel** is near C022-I01 (When regret keeps the wrong alive), C020-I03 (Restless yet still gripping the same thing) and C038-I01 (Trying to know the mind with the mind). It is kept separate because its mechanism is any harmful state taken as object breeding more of itself, contrasted with a clear review of the same state. It is not limited to regret or restlessness.
- **C082-I02 — How one crossed line makes the next easier** is near C056-I02 (Replaying one act until it weighs like habit), C026-I02 (When shame and caution fall silent together) and C074-I01 (A dormant urge that waits for its trigger). It is distinct because it concerns cross-over, where one kind of wrong loosens restraint for other kinds, and imitation.
- **C082-I03 — Real virtue turned into a sense of superiority** is near C012-I01 (Doing good to match or outshine others), C074-I03 (When giving turns into outdoing others), C011-I03 (Envy and pride slipping into a generous act) and C086-I01 (When a good trait resembles a harmful one). It is distinct because an already established genuine quality becomes the support for conceit and self-view afterwards, rather than comparison driving the act. The coordinator may still judge C074-I03 and C082-I03 close enough to cross-reference in the index.
- **Rejected candidates that would duplicate existing cards:** no single cause (C080-I03); one dominant drive at a time (C075-I02); effort and focus on a harmful road (C075-I01); later regret over a gift (C059-I01).

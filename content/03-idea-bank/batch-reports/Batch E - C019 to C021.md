## Held questions

1. **C019-I01 merge.** C019-I01 (Abilities that take the colour of their aim), C075-I01 (When focus and effort serve a harmful aim) and C011-I01 (Knowledge that serves harm as easily as good) share one mechanism: a capacity with no moral direction of its own. C019 is where the source first defines the ethically variable factors (Pradeepika C019 lines 61–63). C075-I01 is narrower (harmful aims, "wrong path"), and C011-I01 applies it to learning. Recommendation: keep one general card and merge the other, preserving C075's "wrong path" detail and C011's learning focus.
2. **C020-I03 as a limited analogy.** The source describes co-present restlessness and one-pointedness in a momentary unwholesome mind-state (Pradeepika C020 line 89). Linking it to worry or rumination is my extension. A reviewer should confirm that the card does not read as a clinical or moral judgment of worry.
3. **Decision "mostly" wrong.** Pradeepika C021 line 45 says decision (අධිමොක්ඛ) “mostly” (බොහෝ සෙයින්) believes the untrue as true. Margaya C021 line 15 says only “rightly or wrongly”. C021-I01 preserves both. Should a reviewer check whether “බොහෝ සෙයින්” here means “often” or “very strongly”?
4. **Mental life faculty.** Pradeepika C020 line 97 stresses that it is momentary and wrongly taken as an undying self. Margaya C020 line 33 stresses continuity through death until final nirvāṇa. Recorded as a difference of emphasis, not carried into any card.
5. **Rejected but reusable candidates.** Pleasant feeling feeding the wish to repeat it, and painful feeling feeding aversion (Pradeepika C020 line 53). This is thin here and may fit later dependent-origination concepts (compare C073-I02). Wise and unwise attention (Pradeepika C020 line 109), which is only named. Thinking as the effort to reach what the mind has not reached (Margaya C021 line 7), rejected because the sources insist initial and sustained application are simultaneous (Margaya C021 line 11). Kamma as a debt (Pradeepika C020 lines 75–79), which is doctrinal only.

## Source agreement notes

- C019: no substantive disagreement. Pradeepika adds the crafty-friend simile. Margaya adds opposed factors (light and darkness) and the division of labour between mind and factors.
- C020: differences of emphasis on the mental life faculty (see held question 4) and on attention (Pradeepika: selecting one object and excluding others, line 105; Margaya: repeatedly returning the mind to objects, line 37). Complementary similes on one-pointedness (Margaya hundred viewpoints, lines 27–29; Pradeepika pothole cart, line 89).
- C021: difference of emphasis on decision (see held question 3). Margaya explicitly warns that the bell simile must not be read as a sequence (line 11). Pradeepika says the two “always arise together” (line 41).

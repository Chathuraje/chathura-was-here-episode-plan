## Held questions

1. **Divine-ear attention method** (Book 4 lines 480–490; Pradeepika lines 59–67). The meditator attends to sounds from coarse and far to subtle and near, then by direction, and extends the range finger-width by finger-width. Sounds heard together form one uproar and must be separated one at a time. This could support a card such as "Many sounds heard as one noise until each is separated". It was not made, because it is bound to a doctrinal power and overlaps C040-I01 and C013-I01. The coordinator should decide whether a hearing or attention concept elsewhere is a better home.
2. **Cūḷapanthaka** (Book 4 lines 228–264). He fails for four months to memorise one verse, his brother expels him as incapable, and the Buddha says failing to recite does not make one incapable. He then understands through rubbing a cloth that grows dirty from his own hand ("the fault is in my hand, not the cloth"). The candidate card was "Judged incapable by one kind of test". It was not made here, because the resolution depends on a created cloth and instant arahantship. Check whether a concept on meditation subjects or temperaments carries this story.
3. **Modesty about ability** (Book 4 line 286). A practitioner reduces many forms to one so that people will not recognise a noble one's power. The passage is too thin for a card.

## Suspected source text errors

- **Book 4 line 39**: "පඨවි කසිණය තෙක් ඇති අට කසිණයන්හි" ("up to the earth kasina") should read "ඕදාත කසිණය තෙක්" ("up to the white kasina"). Line 60 and Pradeepika line 71 confirm this.
- **Book 4 line 98**: the difficulty chain puts obtaining the supernormal knowledge *before* the fourteenfold taming. This contradicts line 43 and Pradeepika line 88, which put taming before the knowledge. It is probably a slip for "absorption".
- **Pradeepika line 106 against Book 4 line 506**: the blood colour of an unhappy mind is compared to a ripe *mango* (අඹ) in Book 1 and to a ripe *rose-apple* (දඹ) in Book 4, both described as black. Book 1 is probably in error.
- **Book 4 line 13**: the inclusion note still refers to `data.md` (renamed `Abhidharma Margaya.md` in commit ef70024). This is stale, not a text error.
- **Book 4**: the transcriber's inline comments already flag several printed readings: line 27 (missing full stop), line 106 (ස්ථවියරන්), line 110 (උදපවා), lines 278–291 (double-transcription diff and හිතති → බිත්ති), lines 383–386 (illegible පර්යඞ්ක), and lines 622 and 674 (orthography resolutions).
- **Book 4**: the spelling `ඥන` for `ඥාන` recurs throughout (for example lines 82, 124 and 466). It looks systematic (OCR or font), and line 280 notes the related ඤාණ/ඥන variation.

## Content that must not be carried into ideas

- Book 4 lines 654–666: low birth, ugliness and poverty are attributed to past greed, hatred and delusion. Used in everyday language, this would blame the poor and disfigured.
- Book 4 lines 500–510 and Pradeepika lines 104–106: knowing mental states from the colour of heart-blood. This is historical physiology, not science.
- Book 4 line 730 and Pradeepika line 47: madness caused by seeing demons with the divine eye. This must not be equated with mental illness.
- All powers, miracle stories, cosmology, past-life ranges and future-Buddha claims (for example Book 4 lines 293–458, 570–614, 948) are doctrinal.
- Book 4 line 572: non-Buddhist ascetics described as having weak wisdom. This is sectarian ranking.

## Possible overlaps

- **C089-I01** (skill not reached in time) and **C016-I02** (quick understanding built on unseen preparation): both concern depth of preparation, but I01 is about speed of access under threat and protecting others. They are related, not merged. It is also related to C075-I02, since the four bases of power (Book 4 lines 160–182) share the same four drives as the dominants.
- **C089-I02** (remembering backwards) and **C013-I01** (sustained, returning attention): both involve returning to a task. I02 is specifically about sequential reverse recall and persistence at a gap. It is also related to C047-I01 (kinds of remembering). Not merged.
- **C089-I03** (forgiveness out of reach) and **C022-I01** (regret that keeps the wrong alive): I03 shows remorse resolved through a concrete act of apology, a counterpart to C022-I01. It is also related to C059-I01. Not merged. Future concepts on speech or wrong action may overlap.
- Rejected candidates overlapping existing cards: mind-reading by inference (C047-I02), seeing only one end of a process (C020-I02, C065-I02), and overwhelming extraordinary perception (C016-I01).
- The concepts on meditation subjects in Chapter 9 (kasinas, the signs, the fourteen modes) may produce cards on graded practice that overlap C089-I01.

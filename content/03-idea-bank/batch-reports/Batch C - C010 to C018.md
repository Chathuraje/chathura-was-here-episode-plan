## Held questions

1. **C010-I01.** Can the adverting function (turning toward an object without knowing it) stand as a limited analogy for everyday attention, given that it describes single momentary mind-states? It duplicates C034-I01 and C042-I01. Proposed: merge into C034-I01.
2. **C011-I01.** Worldly learning versus beneficial understanding (Pradeepika C011 line 70) repeats the mechanism and even the weapons example of C004-I01 (same book, Concept 4) and is close to C019-I01. Proposed: merge into C004-I01, with C011 as a supporting reference.
3. **C012-I01.** Good done after seeing others do good, "out of තරහ" and for praise (Pradeepika C012 line 48), shares its mechanism with C074-I03. Proposed: merge into C074-I03. The meaning of තරහ here (anger, pique, or possibly an OCR form of තරඟ, "competition") needs checking against the printed page (PDF 76 / printed 65).
4. **Doing good when it brings no joy** (Pradeepika C011 lines 53–66, 101; Margaya C011 line 3). A good act may be done with neutral feeling when conditions for gladness are missing, and still be done with understanding. Not carded because the conditions list is mostly doctrinal. Could become a card if a later concept supplies a mechanism.
5. **Pleasure that always needs something added** (Margaya C015 line 12, rice versus cake analogy). The positive term is bodiless rebirth, so no human card was made. Revisit if a concept on contentment or sense restraint gives a human basis.
6. **Ethical exclusion.** Pradeepika C012 line 48 says poor, disabled, less intelligent and helpless people are born so because of weak past good. Pradeepika C011 line 74 says kind people who do not believe in karma do only lesser good. Pradeepika C012 line 44 says meditators without the right rebirth conditions may become mentally disturbed. None of these may be used as premises, framing or explanations for real people.

## Suspected source text errors

- **Margaya C017, line 21.** "කුසල් සිත් තිස" (30 wholesome states) in the 121-state breakdown by kind. The correct count is 37 (8 sense-sphere + 5 fine-material + 4 formless + 20 supramundane). With 30, the stated totals (12 + 30 + 52 + 20) give 114, not 121. Probably "සත් තිස" lost a word.
- **Margaya C010, line 15.** "මෙනෙහි කර සිතය" is probably "මෙනෙහි කරන සිතය" (a dropped syllable).
- **Margaya C012, lines 16 and 18.** "කාමාවචරයා කුශලයා ගේ" reads oddly; probably "කාමාවචර කුශලයාගේ".
- **Pradeepika C011, lines 31–49.** The glosses render සොමනස්ස as "සැප වේදනා" (pleasant/bodily-pleasant feeling). Elsewhere the text keeps සුඛ and සොමනස්ස distinct. This is loose wording, not a doctrinal error.
- **Pradeepika C012, line 48.** "තරහයට" may be an OCR or typesetting variant (see held question 3).
- **No error found.** Arithmetic was checked in the charts at Pradeepika C010 lines 66–72, C012 lines 101–107 and 117–122, C018 lines 44–56, and in the counts at Margaya C012 lines 35–47, C015 lines 20–24, C017 lines 13–15 and 23, and C018. All add up.
- **Minor scope difference, not an error.** Pradeepika C015 line 49 mentions only arahants for the attainment of cessation. Margaya C015 line 14 includes non-returners.

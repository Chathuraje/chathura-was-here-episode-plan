## Held questions

1. **C075-I03 — When shamelessness is as steady as conscience.** The source gives only symmetrical definitions (Margaya line 100; Pradeepika lines 362–368). Does the mental-factors chapter (the concepts on shame, fear of wrongdoing, shamelessness and recklessness) explain how these become strong? If it does, re-evaluate or merge there. There is also a high risk of morally labelling documentary subjects.
2. **C075 wrong concentration and the doubt-accompanied mind.** Margaya (line 24) excludes it, but Pradeepika (line 144) includes all twelve unwholesome minds. This does not affect any card, but it needs a specialist ruling.
3. **C075 scope of the dominants.** Margaya limits them to two- and three-rooted impulsion minds (lines 109–115). Pradeepika gives 69 minds for desire-to-act (line 385) and 73 for effort (line 405), which look like counts of where the factor *occurs*, not where it can *dominate*. This is unresolved.
4. **C074 hatred's nine grounds** (Pradeepika lines 276–289: "he harmed me / is harming / will harm; the same for my loved one; he helped my enemy"). This is a strong grudge mechanism. No card was made here, because hatred-rooted concepts in Chapter 1 are the natural home. The coordinator should check whether that batch used or needs this passage.

## Suspected source text errors

- **C074 Pradeepika**: the hatred-type factor is spelt `පටිස` (for `පටිඝ`) throughout (e.g. lines 165, 480, 512, 553, 577, 611, 633). This looks like a systematic OCR or typing error.
- **C074 Pradeepika lines 255–262**: a six-item list is numbered 1–5 and 7. It is already annotated at line 264 as a misprint in the original.
- **C074 Pradeepika line 322 against line 324**: the condition for sloth and torpor reads `කය නැමෙන බවය` ("body bending") at line 322 but `කය නොතැමෙන බවය` ("body not bending") at line 324. Margaya line 62 has `කය නො සොල්වන බව` ("not moving the body"). Line 322 probably dropped a negation.
- **C074 Margaya line 18**: `වශයේන` for `වශයෙන්`. Line 81: `භවරාානුසය` for `භවරාගානුසය`.
- **C074 Pradeepika line 9**: the inclusion note still refers to `data.md` (now `Abhidharma Margaya.md`). It is not a text error, but it is stale.
- **C075 Pradeepika line 344**: `වීර්ය පඤ්චවීමකොට` ("with effort as fifth") appears to be a garbled carry-over of the `විතක්ක පඤ්චමකං` phrasing used for absorption factors (lines 77–97, themselves spelt `විතක්ය පඤ්චවීම`). The intended sense, confirmed by Margaya line 100, is "only where effort is present".
- **C075 Pradeepika line 304**: `විරිසින්ද්‍රිය` for `විරියින්ද්‍රිය`.
- **C075 Pradeepika lines 414 and 426**: `එස්සා හාරය` / `එස්සාහාරය` for `ඵස්සාහාරය` (consistent within the file).

## Content that must not be carried into ideas (flag for all downstream agents)

- C075 Pradeepika lines 45–53: the claim that weak past good kamma leads to rebirth as a lowly person or "someone else's slave". Used in everyday language, it would blame poverty on the poor.
- C075 Margaya line 57 and Pradeepika line 268: sex-faculty claims that men's and women's thoughts differ, and that girls' play with sewing and cooking pots is "female activity".
- C074 Pradeepika lines 171 and 200: other religions' ascetic practices described as "cow conduct" and "dog conduct".
- C074 Pradeepika lines 522–526: doubt attributed to reasoning from modern sciences. This is an apologetic position.

## Possible overlaps

- **C074-I03** (giving as outdoing) and **C005-I01** (same act, different reason): both concern hidden motives behind an outwardly good act. I03 is specifically about comparison and conceit. Related, not merged. It may also overlap future cards on envy (ඉස්සා) or conceit (මාන) from the mental-factors chapter.

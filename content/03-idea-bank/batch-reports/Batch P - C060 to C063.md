## Held questions

1. **Karma versus other causes in untimely death (C060).** Pradeepika (lines 60–72) treats outside conditions as openings for destructive karma; Margaya (lines 23, 35) treats them as causes in their own right and calls a karma-only view wrong. C060-I01 relies mainly on Margaya's unknowability statement. Should a later reviewer weigh Pradeepika's framing more heavily?
2. **Do relatives influence the near-death object? (C061/C062).** Pradeepika C061 line 29 says the objects are not brought by relatives, teachers or gods; line 51 and Margaya C062 line 25 list chanting, sermons, incense and touch present at the deathbed as possible objects. C062-I01 frames this as a tension rather than resolving it.
3. **Self-inflicted death (Margaya C060 line 33).** Deliberately not used. Any future research touching this must follow safe-messaging practice.
4. **Pradeepika C061 line 91** (life-continuum moments increasing in old age as faculties weaken) was rejected as doctrinal physiology. A reviewer could reconsider it as a limited analogy about the world receding in old age, but it would need strong care.

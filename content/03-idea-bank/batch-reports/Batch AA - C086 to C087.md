## Held questions

1. **Temperament typology and documentary subjects.** C086 infers temperament from gait, sleeping posture and food preference, and attributes it to past lives, hell or animal births and element balance. Recommend a standing rule: no research or screenplay agent may type a real person by these signs or call anyone "lustful", "hating" or "deluded".
2. **C087 Pradeepika line 486** lists intersex people and "paṇḍaka" (and those of rootless or two-rooted rebirth) as unable to benefit from kasina practice. As with the Batch S note on C071, this must never inform any card; recommend adding to research guidance.
3. **Practising before one's conduct is clean** (Margaya C087 line 15). Meditation is said to benefit someone whose conduct is impure and gradually give strength to keep precepts. A real human tension (starting a good discipline before feeling worthy of it), but supported by one sentence and near C055-I01. Not carded; could be revisited if a later concept elaborates.
4. **C086-I03 and austerity.** The source pairs a desire-driven temperament with squalid lodging and disrespectful people. The card warns against using this to justify poor conditions; the coordinator may wish to keep that caution visible.
5. **Kasina fruits** (Pradeepika C087 lines 464–484) are supernormal powers and are excluded entirely.

## Suspected source text errors or oddities

- **C087 Pradeepika line 259.** Says the non-distraction function (අවික්ෂෙප කෘත්‍යය) is performed by the faith faculty. Elsewhere (line 253) non-distraction is concentration's function; faith's function in the standard list is resolve (adhimokkha). Probably a transcription or authorial slip; not corrected.
- **C087 Pradeepika line 95.** The phrase "එයින් යොගීන්හට කමටහනය යයි දෙවැදෑරුම් වේ" is repeated twice in succession, evidently a duplication.
- **C087 Pradeepika lines 289–292.** Editor's comment records a repair: a spurious page-marker 83 was rejoined with 82, and page 85 was transcribed from the scan. Locators on PDF 82–85 depend on that repair.
- **C086 and C087 Pradeepika Book 4 line 13** cite "data.md", the old name for `Abhidharma Margaya.md` (see commit ef70024 message).
- **C086 Margaya line 23** `සත්ප්‍රාස` for `සප්පාය`; **C087 Pradeepika line 340** `බුද්දක` for `ඛුද්දක`; line 261 `මුධප්‍රසන්න` for `මුද්ධප්‍රසන්න`. OCR-style letter confusions; meaning recoverable.
- **C086 food preference disagreement.** Pradeepika line 83: hating type likes sour food; Margaya line 11: harsh/pungent (කටුක) food. Recorded as a genuine minor difference.
- **Absorption counts.** Pradeepika uses the fourfold jhāna scheme (C086 lines 212–228: kasinas give four; divine abidings three; equanimity fourth only), Margaya the fivefold (C086 lines 45–51: five; four; fifth only). Consistent once the scheme is known, but easy to misread as conflict.
- **C087 Pradeepika line 312** lists "රාගකාම" twice in the gradations of sensual desire. This matches the traditional list, so it is probably not an error.

## Possible overlaps

- **C086-I03 — What steadies one person unsettles another** is near **C013-I03 — Answering each inner obstacle with its opposite**. C013-I03 matches a counter-state to a momentary obstacle; C086-I03 fits surroundings and practices to a person's lasting leaning and relies on self-report. Listed as related, not merged. The coordinator may judge them close enough to merge.
- **C087-I01 — When pushing harder keeps the goal away** is near **C075-I02** (which drive leads) and **C003-I02** (needing a push when tired). Mechanism differs: over-effort creating agitation at the approach to success. Related, not merged.
- **C087-I02 — Trust without judgement, cleverness without trust** is near **C021-I01** (being sure without checking) and **C086-I01** (faith resembling craving, wisdom resembling hostility). C086-I01 concerns resemblance of surface; C087-I02 concerns the balance between the two. Same-batch related.
- **C086-I02 — Praising and blaming because others do** is near **C021-I01** and **C052-I02**. Different mechanism (borrowed evaluation and its stickiness). Related.
- **C087-I03 — Recreating the conditions of a past success** is related to **C086-I03** (effect-based suitability) and **C013-I02**. No close duplicate.

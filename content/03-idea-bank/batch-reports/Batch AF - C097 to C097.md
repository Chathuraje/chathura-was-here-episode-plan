## Held questions

1. **Corruptions of insight and C016-I01.** Pradeepika lines 2101–2177 (PDF 571–576) and Margaya lines 99–125 give the full account that C016-I01 had only in summary. That account covers the ten corruptions, craving (නිකන්ති) as the real corruption, the triple grasping "mine / fine / relished", and the Mahānāga story. No new card was made. Should the coordinator add these passages to C016-I01's source trace? The craving-for-the-experience angle could be added to that card as a nuance.
2. **Purification of mind for pure-insight practitioners.** Margaya line 23 gives them a concentration born of insight. Pradeepika line 1026 defines the purification only as access concentration plus the eight attainments, although line 2609 acknowledges the dry-insight worker. This needs a specialist ruling. It does not affect any card.
3. **Overlap with other concepts inside the same extract.** Pradeepika line 13 says PDF 607–620 overlaps Concept 100 and PDF 626–638 overlaps Concept 98. That covers the 37 requisites of enlightenment and what each path abandons. Batches for C098 and C100 should not duplicate C097 cards. The bodhipakkhiya material (lines 2842–2969) also overlaps C076.
4. **C097-I02** concerns trust and unworthiness. It sits close to C076-I01 (effort in response to others' sacrifice). They are kept separate because C076-I01 asks what the sacrifice does to effort, while C097-I02 asks what a gift given on a belief about the receiver asks of them. Confirm this at merge review.

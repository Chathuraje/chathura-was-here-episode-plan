## Held questions

1. **C071, missing faculties.** Margaya line 7 explains blindness, deafness and absent sex-faculty by weak or obstructing kamma. Any future card touching disability or intersex people must not draw on this concept. Recommend a standing rule in the register.
2. **C073, the "moving from a harsh region" simile** (Margaya line 15). A plausible human process (attachment to a hard place falling away once a better life is known) but held back: it risks ranking real places as bad and contradicts the common experience of attachment surviving migration. Could be revisited if a research agent finds relocation material where people themselves describe this change.
3. **C072 as supporting concept.** If a later batch produces a card on the death-proximate cognitive process with genuine human content, C072 could be listed as supporting. No card now.
4. **C073-I03 and blindness imagery.** The source's illustration uses congenital blindness. The card warns against using it as a metaphor for ignorance; the coordinator may wish to add this to research guidance.

## Suspected source text errors or oddities

- **C072, rebirth-absent matter: nine versus eight.** Pradeepika lines 63–72 (PDF 122 / printed 111) lists nine kinds (sound, five mutable, continuity, decay, impermanence). Margaya line 15 lists eight, omitting continuity (සන්තති). Both give explicit totals. Recorded as genuine disagreement, not corrected.
- **C072, Pradeepika chart line 113.** The mind-born columns step from 17 down to 16 at group 8 of the decline table, although the text (line 16) says mind-born matter is produced up to the death consciousness. The editor's note (line 181) confirms this is as printed. Possibly a printing error in the original chart or a counting convention not explained in the text; not resolved.
- **C072, Pradeepika lines 78–80.** Says intimation and lightness-group matter at birth occur only for the Bodhisatta and a chief bull, immediately after stating these are never present at rebirth. Not a contradiction (birth versus rebirth-moment), but easy to misread.
- **C071, nose decad.** Pradeepika line 115 restricts "no spontaneous being lacks the nose decad" to beings in bad destinations; Margaya line 13 states it after discussing bad-destination beings but without the restriction. Scope difference only.
- **OCR-style letter confusions** throughout the Pradeepika files, especially න/ත (e.g. C071 line 131 `දෙවත සිතෙහි` for `දෙවන`; C073 line 24 `ස්වබුද්ධිමාත්‍ර නකි` for `තර්ක`; line 102 `ශාස්ත්‍ර නකී`). Meaning is recoverable; nothing was changed.
- **C071 Margaya line 17** itself notes other teachers' views on the 77th day; not an error, but the day count is not settled within the tradition.

## Possible overlaps

- **C073-I02 — Getting what you want and wanting it again** is near C006-I01 (Knowing the cost and still wanting it) and C067-I01 (Giving up the wanting, not the thing). Mechanisms differ: I02 is renewal of wanting because satisfaction fades; C006-I01 is wanting despite known cost; C067-I01 is releasing desire versus removing the object. Listed as related, not merged.
- **C073-I03 — What a description cannot give you** is near C020-I01 (Knowing the sign without knowing the reason) and C001-I02 (Learning for a title instead of for change). Different mechanisms (second-hand versus first-hand understanding; recognition versus understanding; motive for learning). Related to C020-I01 only.
- **C073-I01 — When relief is worth more than pleasure** has no close existing match.
- **C071 rejected candidate** (body as a continuing flame or river) would duplicate C001-I01.
- Within the batch, I01 and I02 both concern the limits of pleasure; they are kept separate because I01 is about valuing freedom from pain over enjoyment, and I02 about why obtained enjoyment renews desire.

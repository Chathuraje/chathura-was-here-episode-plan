## Held questions

- **C050-I01.** Can the claim that "not every greed is a hindrance, but every hatred is" (Margaya C050 line 17) support a human idea about resentment and absorption without importing the technical meaning of jhāna? Release only if later concepts on the hindrances (නීවරණ) give a fuller account.
- **C046 and C048 sequence disagreement.** Pradeepika (C046 line 117, lines 119–151) has one sense process followed by four mind-door processes (past form, colour, concept and recognition, then reaction). Margaya (C048 lines 23–27) has three mind-door processes (past form, name, meaning) and calls it "one teacher's view". C046-I01 relies on the shared point (recognition precedes the reaction or the knowing), not on either count. A reviewer should confirm this is acceptable.
- **C049 disability framing.** Pradeepika C049 line 43 and C050 line 31 describe people born blind or deaf, with limited intelligence or living by begging as a "low" birth from weak merit. Recommend a project-wide rule that this framing is never used for idea premises or story selection.

## Suspected source text errors

- **C049 Pradeepika line 103.** The worldling's count is written in words as සුපනස (54) and in numerals as (45). Lines 77–81 give 54. The C050 Pradeepika table (line 67) gives 45 without absorption and 54 with it.
- **C049 Pradeepika line 103.** Trainees are said to get "පනසකි" (fifty) consciousnesses, then totalled as සපනස් (56).
- **C049 Pradeepika lines 113 and 117.** The printed table omits "unwholesome 12" in the second row, so the column sums to 29 against a brace total of 41 (already noted by the transcriber at line 117).
- **C050 Pradeepika line 33.** "පටිසජවනයන් වූ දෙස මූල සිත්" is probably a misprint or OCR error for පටිඝ ජවන.
- **C050 Pradeepika table, lines 61–80.** Headed "වීථී සිත්", but the fine-material and immaterial rows appear to include the door-freed sublime resultants (by my count, worldling fine-material 38 process consciousnesses plus 5 resultants = 43; worldling immaterial 23 plus 4 = 27). This is an arithmetic check, not a confirmed error.
- **C050 Pradeepika line 45.** "පරතොසොස" appears for the usual පරතොඝොස (hearing from another). Possibly an OCR or spelling variant.
- **C047 Margaya line 13.** "වීක්ථි" for වීථි (typo).

## Possible overlaps

- **C046-I01 and C001-I01.** Both involve names and concepts. C001-I01 is about wholes being names for arranged parts; C046-I01 is about reactions following recognition. Different mechanisms; cross-referenced.
- **C046-I01 and C047-I01.** Both distinguish layers of perception and memory. C046-I01 concerns the order of recognition and reaction; C047-I01 concerns which object memory takes. Kept separate.
- **C047-I01 and C001-I01.** Both touch on loss of a place. C001-I01 locates the loss in a changed arrangement; C047-I01 separates memory of the place, of being there and of the feeling. Cross-referenced.
- **C048-I01.** Watch for overlap with any later ideas on rumination, attention or thinking (vitakka, manasikāra) from other batches.
- **C050-I01 and C006-I01.** Both concern wanting; C050-I01 contrasts it with resentment. Low overlap. Watch for later hindrance-based ideas from other batches.
- **Merge record.** The Margaya "following process" material (C048 lines 23–27) was folded into C046-I01 as supporting evidence rather than written as a separate C048 card.

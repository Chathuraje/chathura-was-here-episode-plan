## Held questions

1. **C094 breath-following disagreement.** Margaya line 5 instructs following the breath from nose-tip to chest to navel; Pradeepika lines 126, 130 and 148 forbid following beginning, middle and end, and line 206 (third step) speaks of knowing them while staying at the nose-tip. C094-I02 follows Pradeepika only. A specialist should decide whether Margaya is a simplified lay method or a genuine divergence.
2. **C094 scope of the practice.** Margaya line 3 says many practise it both within and outside the Buddhist dispensation; Pradeepika line 46 says the sixteen-step method succeeds for a monk of this dispensation who has not adopted other views. Unresolved; no card depends on it.
3. **C091-I03 speech criteria.** Pradeepika lines 156–162 give four cases (untrue/harmful and true/beneficial, each pleasant or unpleasant). The well-known canonical version has six, including true but unbeneficial speech. Is the compression the commentator's or an extraction loss? The card flags it in Limits.
4. **C091-I02 generalisation.** The benefit (line 386) concerns the Buddha specifically. Extending an imagined admired presence to parents or mentors is an interpretation; reviewers may prefer to hold it if that bridge is thought too wide.
5. **C096 three sources of wisdom** (lines 58–62: reflection, hearing, development). Not made a card because it overlaps C073-I03. The coordinator may wish to add C096 as a supporting concept to C073-I03.

- **C094 Pradeepika line 36**: `අනා සිත්තකය` for `අනාසිත්තකය`.

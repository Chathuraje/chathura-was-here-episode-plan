## Held questions

1. **C098 sense desire at non-returning.** Margaya line 21 says the non-returner's path removes sense desire (කාමරාග) completely. The Pradeepika Book 4 chart places greed (line 93), covetousness (lines 111, 134), clinging to sense pleasures (line 131) and attachment to gain, fame, praise and pleasure (line 95) at the arahant's path. Is this only a difference of classification? It affects how firmly C098-I02 can link the worldly-conditions split to the order of hostility and greed.
2. **C099 number of reviewing impulsions.** Margaya line 37 says seven; Pradeepika Book 4 lines 30 and 40 say four or five. Needs a specialist ruling. No card depends on it.
3. **C099 "ten skills in absorption"** (දශවිධ අර්පණා කෞශල්‍යය, Book 4 line 24) are referred to but not defined in this extract. The header (line 13) says the file overlaps Concepts 87 and 88 on purpose. The coordinator should check whether the batch for C087–C088 has an effort-balance card, and whether C099-I01 should merge with it or with C076-I02.
4. **C098 phrasing of the Mahātissa and "woman's song" examples** (Book 4 lines 185, 189). These frame women as triggers of defilement. They were not used. Downstream agents should not use them either.

## Content that must not be carried into ideas

- C099 Margaya line 9 and Pradeepika line 57: only people born with a knowledge-accompanied (three-rooted) rebirth consciousness can attain absorption; the dull cannot. This must not be used to rank anyone's intelligence or ability.
- C098 Margaya line 41: the body of someone in cessation cannot be harmed by fire or water. C099 Book 4 line 42: Moggallāna's rapid entry to tame a nāga. Doctrinal powers claims.
- C098 Book 4 lines 334–378: subtypes of noble persons by number and place of remaining rebirths.

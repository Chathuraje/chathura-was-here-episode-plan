## Held questions

1. **C016-I02 — Quick understanding built on long unseen preparation.** Held. Pradeepika (lines 62, 84) grounds quick attainment in preparation across lives and aeons. Should a within-one-life reading be allowed as a limited analogy, or does that alter the claim too much?
2. **Disillusion after lifelong practice without results** (C013 Pradeepika line 129). The source says people without the right rebirth-consciousness may practise all their lives, fail, become weary or unhinged, and conclude the practice is empty. Not made a card because the cause is rebirth-linked. Note that C056-I03 ("Blaming the path after years without results") already covers a similar mechanism from another concept; the coordinator may want to add C013 as a supporting reference there only if the reviewer accepts the doctrinal caveat.
3. **Body-weariness as motive** (C014 Pradeepika lines 29, 50; Margaya line 12). Rejected for now. Any future use must avoid presenting disgust with the body as healthy or as a health claim.
4. **C013-I03** widens "doubt" (in the source, doubt about the Buddha, teaching and Sangha) to doubt in general. The reviewer should confirm this widening is acceptable as a labelled analogy.

## Source disagreements and complementary detail (summary)

- C013: prompting. Pradeepika states all form-sphere states are prompted; Margaya says the classification has no real use and they are counted as prompted for convenience. Emphasis difference only.
- C014: removal of the kasiṇa sign. Pradeepika describes wishing to see the stars the light covers, so the light opens like a window; Margaya describes attending to the sign as "space". Complementary accounts.
- C016: path factors. Margaya stresses gradual development; Pradeepika stresses simultaneous arising in the path moment. Complementary.

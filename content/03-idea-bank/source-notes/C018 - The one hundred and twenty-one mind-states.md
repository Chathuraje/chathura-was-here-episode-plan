## Central meaning

This is the summary of chapter one. 81 mundane states plus 8 supramundane states give 89; with the 40 expanded supramundane states, 121 (Pradeepika lines 23–25). Of the 121, 67 are absorption states (Pradeepika line 27). Pradeepika's chart gives the 59/91 beautiful states by feeling, knowledge and prompting (lines 35–56). Margaya lists all 121 by name with the instruction to memorise them, "otherwise further study is impossible" (lines 3, 7–125).

## Essential distinctions

Only the classification axes of chapter one, explained in the earlier concepts: root, feeling, view, knowledge, prompting, plane, and path or fruit. No new explanation is given.

## Agreement, complementary detail and disagreement

The sources agree on the totals. Pradeepika summarises with a chart; Margaya gives the full enumerated list. No disagreement was found. Chart totals were checked and add up (Pradeepika line 56).

## What cannot safely be carried into everyday language

The list presupposes the whole doctrinal classification, including planes of existence and supramundane attainments.

## Human meaning paragraph

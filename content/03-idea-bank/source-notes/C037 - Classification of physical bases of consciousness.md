# C037 — Classification of physical bases of consciousness

## Sources and coverage

- `Abhidhammattha Pradeepika.md`: read in full (lines 1–163). Frontmatter: Book 1, chapter 3, PDF 295–300 (printed 285–290). Page markers at line 37 (PDF 296 / printed 286), 51 (297/287), 79 (298/288), 115 (299/289), 140 (300/290). Extract begins mid-page on PDF 295 (line 21).
- `Abhidharma Margaya.md`: read in full (lines 1–41). Heading `# 6. වස්තු සංග්‍ර‍හය`, parts I (lines 3–25) and II (lines 27–41). No page locators.

## Central meaning

A "base" (වස්තු) is the physical place where mind-states arise. In beings with both mind and matter, mental states depend on matter. There are six kamma-born bases: the five sense-sensitivities (eye, ear, nose, tongue, body) and the heart-base (හදය වස්තුව). Minds are grouped as always base-dependent (43), sometimes base-dependent (42) and never base-dependent (4), and consciousness is grouped into seven elements (විඤ්ඤාණ ධාතු). The bases present differ by plane of existence.

## Essential distinctions

- **Door and base are the same sensitivity seen two ways**: a door because objects enter by it, a base because consciousness arises depending on it (Pradeepika line 29; Margaya line 9).
- **Heart-base**: a subtle matter supported by about half a measure of blood in the heart cavity, invisible, not graspable by medicine, absent in the dead; most mind-states arise depending on it (Margaya line 11; Pradeepika lines 41–43). Historical physiological doctrine.
- **Fine-material beings lack nose, tongue and body sensitivities** (not the organs), because these serve only sensual enjoyment and such beings arose through meditation on dispassion towards sense pleasures; eye and ear remain because they serve seeing the Buddha and hearing the teaching (Pradeepika lines 49–53; Margaya line 15).
- **Why 43 minds never arise in formless realms** (Margaya lines 31–39): no doors or functions; hatred does not arise in jhāna-attainers; formless beings do not laugh; and stream-entry path cannot arise because, without ears, they cannot hear the teaching from another. Except Buddhas and Paccekabuddhas, no one can produce the stream-entry path by their own power without hearing from another. Once a stream-enterer, a person can reach the later paths without others' help (line 39).

## Agreement, complementary detail and disagreement

Counts agree: 43 / 42 / 4 (Pradeepika lines 81–130; Margaya lines 21–29). Margaya's list at line 21 counts 33 heart-dependent minds including five-door adverting and two receiving minds; Pradeepika places those three under mind-element (line 90) and lists 30 at lines 94–101. Same total, different grouping; not a conflict. Pradeepika adds the seven consciousness elements and plane tables (lines 57–161). Margaya alone gives the reasons for formless absences, including the need to hear the teaching from another.

## What cannot safely be carried into everyday language

The heart-base is historical physiology and must never be presented as a fact about the heart or brain. Planes, formless beings, the absence of senses in brahmas, and rebirth after stream-entry are cosmology. The claim about hearing from another concerns a specific liberating attainment, not learning in general.

## Human meaning paragraph

Most of this concept is a map of where consciousness is said to be seated and which bases exist in which realms; it carries no direct human mechanism. One passage does describe a human process: the decisive first understanding in the path cannot, for ordinary people, be produced alone; it needs hearing from another, while the steps after it can be completed without help. Carrying this into everyday learning would be an extension, so it is held.

## Idea decisions

- **C037-I01 — Needing another's voice for the first step.** Held. The only supporting passage is Margaya line 39, it concerns a specific soteriological attainment set inside cosmology, and generalising it to learning or change needs a separate fidelity decision.
- Rejected candidate: *Senses kept only for what they serve* (brahmas lacking nose, tongue and body sensitivities). Premise is cosmological and rebirth-linked.
- Rejected candidate: *The heart as seat of mind* — historical physiology; cannot become an idea premise.
- Rejected candidate: *No laughter without a body* (Margaya line 37). Single unexplained doctrinal remark.

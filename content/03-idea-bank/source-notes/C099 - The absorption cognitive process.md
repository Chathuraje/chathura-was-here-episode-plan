## Central meaning

An absorption process (අර්පණා වීථි) is a mind-door process whose impulsions are exalted (fine-material or immaterial absorption) or supramundane (path and fruit). When a meditator first reaches an absorption, the mind-door adverting is followed by three or four sense-sphere impulsions named preparation (පරිකර්ම), access (උපචාර), conformity (අනුලෝම) and change-of-lineage (ගෝත්‍රභූ), and then a single moment of absorption, after which the process sinks into life-continuum. A person of slow direct knowledge (දන්ධාභිඥ) has all four; a person of quick direct knowledge (ක්ෂිප්‍රාභිඥ) has no preparation moment. In later "attainment" processes the absorption moment repeats many times, for as long as the person wishes. After emerging, the meditator reviews the absorption factors one by one. The five masteries (adverting, entering, sustaining, emerging, reviewing) must be developed before moving to the next absorption, which is reached by seeing the present absorption's grosser factors as faulty and the next as more peaceful.

## Essential distinctions

- **First attainment versus attainment at will.** In the first-time process the absorption moment occurs once (Margaya lines 15–17). In the attainment process it recurs many times (Margaya line 21; Pradeepika Book 1 line 69: "for as long as one wishes").
- **Mastery as a separate stage.** Book 4 lines 36–52 define the five masteries and require them before seeking the second absorption. Book 1 lines 41–43 make the first absorption, practised in the five masteries, the base for all higher attainments.
- **Effort in balance** (Book 4 lines 24–26). If absorption does not come, keep practising with wisdom. Some tighten effort when the counterpart sign appears, hoping to reach absorption quickly, and restlessness makes them fail. Some see the fault in excess effort and fall into laziness, and also fail. The one who falls into neither reaches it. Line 97 defines equanimity of effort as effort neither over-started nor over-loosened.
- **Moving on by seeing coarseness** (Book 4 lines 56, 66–70, 124). The meditator gives up attachment (නිකාන්තිය) to the present absorption. Initial and sustained application seem coarse; later, joy seems coarse "as though elating the mind"; later, pleasure seems coarse compared with equanimity.
- **Ten kinds of equanimity** (Book 4 lines 78–111), and the third absorption's very sweet bodily happiness felt without longing for it (line 113).
- **Removed factors disappear at the arising moment**, not during access (lines 62, 74, 113), except that bodily pleasure and pain, joy and grief are removed before the fourth absorption (line 126).

## Agreement, complementary detail and disagreement

- **Agreement:** all three sources give the same sequence of preparatory impulsions, the slow/quick distinction, the single absorption moment on first attainment and the repetition in attainment.
- **Complementary:** Margaya gives the vīthi counts (18, 36, 72, line 11), the meaning of the four names (line 33) and the reviewing processes (line 37). Book 1 Pradeepika gives the entry conditions (line 57: a teacher, pure conduct, a suitable dwelling, a meditation subject, and no obstruction of result, kamma or defilement) and the diagram at line 76. Book 4 gives the effort passage, the five masteries, the higher absorptions and the ten equanimities.
- **Disagreement 1, number of reviewing impulsions:** Margaya line 37 says seven sense-sphere impulsions occur in a reviewing process (four or five only when reviewing quickly for wonders, line 39). Book 4 lines 30 and 40 say four or five.
- **Disagreement 2, kinds of absorption process:** Margaya line 5 lists five (absorption, path, fruition attainment, direct knowledge, cessation attainment). Book 1 line 35 lists three (absorption, path, attainment). This looks like a different grouping, not a conflict of substance.
- **Complementary on eligibility:** Margaya line 9 says only those born with knowledge-accompanied rebirth can attain absorption; Book 1 line 57 says the same in terms of a three-rooted rebirth. Both are doctrinal.

## What cannot safely be carried into everyday language

The claim that only people born with a certain rebirth consciousness can attain absorption (Margaya line 9; Book 1 line 57) must never be used to rank anyone's abilities. Slow and quick knowledge are doctrinal categories, not measures of intelligence. Wonders (Margaya line 39), Moggallāna's taming of the nāga (Book 4 line 42), supramundane attainments and "the most refined worldly happiness" (Book 1 line 79) are doctrinal. The moment-by-moment impulsion counts are not a timing model of attention and must not be presented as neuroscience.

## Human meaning paragraph

Two processes survive translation as limited analogies. First, the source treats the first time a deep state is reached as a single moment, different in kind from being able to enter it, remain in it, leave it and look back on it whenever one chooses. It also requires that second ability before any further step. Second, it describes a trap near success. As the sign of progress appears, eagerness tightens effort and the resulting agitation spoils the attempt. A person who sees that fault may then slacken too far, and fail again. Both readings are interpretation. The source concerns meditation, not skills in general.

## Idea decisions

- **C099-I01 — Straining near the goal, then overcorrecting into slackness.** Accepted for research. Based on Book 4 lines 24–26, with line 97. Shares the effort-balance theme of C076-I02, but this card is about two specific failures: tightening as success comes near, and overcorrecting after seeing that fault. Flagged for a merge review.
- **C099-I02 — Doing it once is not yet mastering it.** Accepted for research as a limited analogy. Based on the first-time versus attainment processes and the five masteries. Related to C089-I01 (speed of entry under threat), which is a narrower point.
- Rejected candidate: *Outgrowing a joy that once felt like the goal* (seeing joy and pleasure as coarse, lines 66–70, 124). The mechanism overlaps C016-I01 (mistaking a striking experience for arrival) and C014-I02 (going beyond while depending), and the "coarseness" judgement is specific to absorption.
- Rejected candidate: *Enjoying fully without longing for more* (line 113, third absorption). Overlaps C067-I01 and C073-I02, and the "happiness spread through the body by mind-born matter" is doctrinal physiology.
- Rejected candidate: slow versus quick knowledge (Margaya lines 9–17). Tied to rebirth eligibility, overlaps C016-I02 and C085-I01, and risks ranking people.
- Rejected: the ten kinds of equanimity, the counts of absorption processes and the four impulsion names. Technical classification.

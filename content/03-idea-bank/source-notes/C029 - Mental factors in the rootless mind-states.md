## Central meaning

The eighteen rootless mind-states (අහේතුක) are made up of seven unwholesome results, eight wholesome results and three functional states. They contain none of the six roots and none of the unwholesome or beautiful factors. Pradeepika calls them very weak (ඉතා දුර්වල, line 29). The ten sense-consciousnesses (seeing, hearing, smelling, tasting, touching) are the weakest. They have only the seven universal factors (line 35; Margaya line 5). Receiving and investigating states add applied thought, sustained thought and decision (line 39). The joyful investigating state adds joy (line 45). Mind-door adverting (determining) adds energy but lacks joy and desire-to-act (line 49). The smile-producing state of arahants has everything except desire-to-act (line 53; Margaya line 9).

## Essential distinctions

- None of the rootless states has desire-to-act (ඡන්දය) (Margaya line 3).
- Energy (විරිය) is present only in mind-door adverting and the smile-producing state (Pradeepika chart line 88).
- Decision (අධිමොක්ඛ) is absent in the ten sense-consciousnesses (chart line 87).

## Agreement, complementary detail and disagreement

The sources agree on every count. Pradeepika gives the grouping into five and the charts. Margaya lists each state by type of result. No disagreement was found.

## What cannot safely be carried into everyday language

The smile-producing state belongs only to arahants and is doctrinal. The whole analysis concerns momentary units of mind in a cognitive series. This source does not describe the sequence (seeing, then receiving, then investigating, then determining), which belongs to the chapter 4 concepts (C038–C048). Any "raw seeing before judgement" reading would impose a sequence this text does not give.

## Human meaning paragraph

Taken on its own, the passage says only that the bare moment of sensing carries very few mental factors. It has no thought, decision, effort, wanting, or moral colouring, and the text calls it weak. That is a structural claim about doctrinal units. It does not describe a human process that a storyteller could follow without the cognitive-process chapter.

- Rejected candidate: *The smile of someone with nothing left to crave*. It is doctrinal, about arahants, and the source gives no description beyond the factor count.
- Rejected candidate: *Weak, rootless states*. The point about strength is developed with an explanation in C033 and is carried there (C033-I01).

# C006 — How the eight greed-rooted mind-states arise

- `Abhidharma Margaya.md`: read in full (lines 1–19). Heading `# 7. ලෝභමූල සිත් අට උපදනා සැටි`. No page locators.

## Central meaning

Greed-rooted consciousness (ලෝභමූල සිත) comes in eight types. Three distinctions separate them. The first is feeling: joyful (සොමනස්ස) or neutral (උපෙක්ඛා). The second is whether the state is joined with a wrong view (දිට්ඨිගත සම්පයුත්ත / විප්පයුත්ත). The third is whether it arises spontaneously or needs prompting (අසංඛාරික / සසංඛාරික).

## Essential distinctions

- **Wrong view.** In Pradeepika (lines 33–42), this is an explicit belief that removes the wrongness of an act. Examples: there is no result of action, taking what you like is not wrong, lying for your own benefit is fine. Margaya (1) adds rationalising examples: “there is no wrong in taking the surplus of the rich,” “wealth gained unrighteously can be taken,” and “the gods gave us these.”
- **Greed without wrong view** (Pradeepika, third state, lines 64–66). The person does not disbelieve in consequences. They know the harms of wrong action and the benefits of good action, but cannot hold down desire. Pradeepika attributes this largely to habit carried through saṃsāra. It also arises when people act for gain or to avoid trouble.
- **Prompting** (Pradeepika lines 44 and 58–62; Margaya (2), (4), (6), (8)). Prompted states arise through urging or encouragement. Margaya names two sources: another person's urging, or the person spurring on their own retreating mind (පසු බස්නා වූ තමාගේ සිත තමා විසින් ම උත්සාහවත් කරවා ගැනීම). Pradeepika adds that prompted unwholesome states can carry sluggishness or heaviness (ථීනමිද්ධ).
- **Supporting conditions** for joyful feeling, wrong view and spontaneity (Pradeepika lines 29–31, 39–42, 48–52). Examples: association with people of wrong view, living apart from the teaching, careless reflection, patience, practised skill, suitable climate and food. Some conditions are rebirth-linked. They are doctrinal and are not carried into ideas.

## Agreement, complementary detail and disagreement

The two sources agree on the eightfold structure. Pradeepika explains *why* each factor appears. Margaya gives concrete action examples and a strength ranking (line 19). Greed that completes one of the seven unwholesome courses of action is strong in result. Attachment to spouse, children and wealth is weak and uncertain in result. States with view are stronger than those without, spontaneous states are stronger than prompted ones, and — unexpectedly — neutral-feeling states are stronger than joyful ones. Pradeepika gives no strength ranking, so this is complementary detail, not a conflict.

## What cannot safely be carried into everyday language

The rebirth-linked causes (the kind of rebirth consciousness) and the claim about habit carried through saṃsāra are doctrinal. The strength ranking concerns karmic result, not psychological intensity, and must not be presented as a measurable finding. Neutral feeling here does not mean calmness or indifference in the modern sense.

## Human meaning paragraph

Wanting something can come with a story that makes the act seem acceptable, or it can go ahead while the person fully admits it is wrong. It can arrive easily and eagerly, or it can need a push from another person or from oneself before a hesitant mind acts. The sources treat these as different kinds of mind-state rather than a sequence. The everyday reading below is interpretation: a person's stated beliefs, their knowledge of consequences and their need for encouragement are separate things that can come apart.

## Idea decisions

- **C006-I01 — Knowing the cost and still wanting it.** Accepted. Based on Pradeepika's third state. This develops the workflow's worked example.
- **C006-I02 — The belief that makes taking seem fair.** Accepted. Based on the wrong-view distinction and Margaya's rationalising examples.
- **C006-I03 — Needing a push to do what you already want.** Accepted. Based on prompting from others or from oneself.
- Rejected candidate: *Wanting without enjoying* (neutral-feeling greed ranked stronger). Only Margaya supports the ranking, it concerns karmic result, and the source does not explain it. Keep it as an open question rather than a card.
- Rejected candidate: detailed supporting conditions (patience, health, food). These are mostly doctrinal lists with too little explanation to support a human mechanism.

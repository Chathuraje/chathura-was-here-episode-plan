# C097-I03 — Goodness that ends once its reward arrives

**Status:** Accepted for research. This is a conceptual interpretation. No real story has been verified.

## Core idea

Good conduct kept as a means to something else has a built-in end point. It lasts until the reward is secured, or until keeping it costs more than the reward is worth.

## Human mechanism and tension

The source separates conduct "with a limit" from conduct "without a limit". The first is kept with an eye on gain, fame, relatives, a limb or life. When the gain or fame arrives, the person drops it, and the source calls such conduct torn and broken. Conduct without a limit is not measured against any of these. The source adds related grades. Low conduct is kept for praise, to raise oneself or to look down on others. "Grasped" conduct is kept to be reborn as a god. Middling conduct is kept only for one's own release. It also names stagnant conduct: content with what has been achieved, it neither declines nor grows. My interpretation, beyond the text, is that a bargain gives goodness a reason to exist and also a reason to stop. People rarely notice the limit until the reward comes or the price rises.

## Possible everyday expression

Hypothetical research direction: people who kept a discipline, honesty or service for a stated goal, such as a job, a marriage, an election, a promotion or a visa, describing what happened to that conduct once the goal was reached or lost. No person, event or outcome has been established.

## Open human question

What keeps us behaving well after the thing we were behaving well for has been gained?

## Limits and alternatives

The source ranks conduct by motive for doctrinal ends, including rebirth. Its praise of keeping precepts even at the cost of life must not be carried forward. Conduct that relaxes after a goal may reflect a deliberate, time-bound commitment, exhaustion or changed circumstances rather than a hidden bargain. Mixed motives are normal, and outsiders cannot see which one held a person's conduct in place.

# C033-I02 — Loosening a grip by growing its opposite

**Status:** Merged into C013-I03 (Answering each inner obstacle with its opposite), which has the same mechanism: a specific counter-state for each obstacle. Differences preserved here: C033 pairs the three roots (non-greed against greed, non-aversion against aversion, wisdom against delusion) rather than the five hindrances, and it claims the wholesome roots are *stronger* and can *destroy* the unwholesome ones. No real story has been verified.

## Core idea

The source claims that greed, hostility and confusion are undone by their specific opposites. Generosity undoes greed, goodwill undoes hostility, and understanding undoes confusion. They are not undone simply by being held down.

## Human mechanism and tension

The source pairs each unwholesome root with a wholesome counterpart. It says the wholesome roots are stronger than the unwholesome ones and have the power to destroy them. Non-greed destroys greed. Non-aversion destroys aversion. Non-delusion, which is wisdom, destroys delusion. The pairing is specific: each grip is met by its own opposite, not by a general effort to be good. My interpretation is that this describes change by cultivating a replacement, not by suppression. Someone gripped by possessiveness would loosen it by practising giving. Someone gripped by resentment would loosen it through goodwill. Confusion would give way to clearer understanding. The tension is that this is slow and indirect. People usually try to stop a habit head-on, while the source points to growing something else in its place.

## Possible everyday expression

Hypothetical research direction: people who describe leaving behind a long-held resentment, a habit of hoarding, or a family feud. The research would ask whether it ended through stopping the old feeling or through something else growing, such as a shared project, an act of help or new knowledge. No person, event or outcome has been established.

## Open human question

Can a hold that will not break by force be loosened by strengthening something else?

## Limits and alternatives

"Stronger" and "destroy" are doctrinal claims in the source, not measured findings. The same passage links this power to karmic results, which is excluded here. The source does not describe a practice or timescale. People may leave resentments behind through time, changed circumstances, reconciliation initiated by the other side, or simply forgetting. Change attributed to "growing generosity" may have other explanations that only careful listening can bring out.

# C050-I01 — Why resentment unsettles more than wanting does

**Status:** Held. The interpretation rests on a one-sentence doctrinal rationale inside a cosmological argument. No real story has been verified.

## Core idea

Some wanting can sit alongside a deeply settled mind, but ill-will in any form works against it.

## Human mechanism and tension

The source explains why hatred-rooted states never occur in the planes reached through deep meditative absorption, while greed-rooted states do. Both greed and hatred can obstruct absorption. But not every instance of greed becomes an obstruction, whereas "there is no hatred that is not" one. A second source gives a related reason: birth in those planes comes through turning away from sense pleasures, so hatred does not arise there. My interpretation, clearly beyond the text, is that resentment and deep settledness may exclude each other more strictly than mild wanting and settledness do. A person absorbed in a craft or prayer might still quietly want a good result, but it is harder to imagine them absorbed while nursing a grudge. The tension would be that people often treat wanting as the main enemy of calm, while ill-will may be the sharper one.

## Possible everyday expression

Hypothetical research direction: people whose work needs sustained absorption, such as drummers, weavers, carvers or those who meditate, describing what breaks their focus: hopes about the outcome, or anger at someone. No person, event or outcome has been established.

## Open human question

Is it harder to become absorbed while resenting someone than while wanting something?

## Limits and alternatives

Held for three reasons. The source concerns meditative absorption (a technical state) and rebirth planes, not everyday concentration. It gives a rule, not a mechanism. The reading "mild wanting" is my gloss; the source says only "not every greed". Alternatively, strong craving may disrupt focus as much as anger, and some people report that anger sharpens effort. Release only if later concepts on hindrances give fuller support.

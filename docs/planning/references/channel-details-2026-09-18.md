## Snapshot scope

This is a faithful planning-relevant snapshot, not a full export or a replacement for the source document. It records the parts that affect the Step 1 rules, framing continuity, and later decisions. The document is external and is not present in the repository.

## Project and editorial guidance observed

- The channel is described as cinematic, immersive, story-driven documentary work in which the viewer experiences the place and story from Chathura’s point of view.
- The document explicitly uses **Story → Place → Experience**.
- Episodes begin inside the story with footage and early voice-over; season/episode information, coordinates, and title arrive after curiosity develops.
- A location name is not automatically revealed.
- Footage, natural sound, atmosphere, observation, and room to breathe take priority over explanatory narration.
- The general runtime guidance is 5–10 minutes, with 6–8 minutes ideal and story-led flexibility. It gives separate working targets for Episodes 1 and 100.
- The closing order is “A Film by Chathura” and then the project logo.

## Framing continuity observed

- Episode 1 begins in a crowded environment and then cuts to Chathura traveling toward Meemure.
- Episode 1 continues through the monastery material and ends after Chathura disappears into the forest.
- The document identifies the material before the Meemure road and after the forest disappearance as the two missing pieces completed by Episode 100.
- Episode 99 is described as ending with the same crowded footage that opens Episode 1. Under the current planning instruction, this relationship remains provisional.
- Episode 100 begins with the exact same crowded environment as Episode 1, reaches the familiar cut point, and continues instead.
- It proceeds to Chathura’s room, the ten objects, the missing journey toward Hunnasgiriya and the road into Meemure, and the planned frame where Episode 1 originally continued.
- It later reaches Episode 1’s forest ending and continues beyond it.
- The document emphasizes that familiar footage takes on different meaning after the intervening journey.

## Historical rules observed

The document contains the older public/discovered Episode 100 model, season-oriented structure, and one-object-per-season language. It also states that by the Episode 1/100 chronology all ten objects exist together. These historical rules are retained as evidence of the older model; where they conflict, the newer requirements govern development:

- objects attach to ten chronological groups rather than seasons;
- chronological groups, release series, seasons, and filming schedules remain separate;
- the current planning target is the 100-film opening/finale model;
- all ten objects are planned as already acquired in Episode 1 chronology, but planned ownership is separate from verified acquisition and shot visibility.

The historical distribution, season-length, and PDF/archive questions do not block a small unnumbered concept-to-idea pilot. They affect later release packaging, discovery strategy, and archival reproducibility.

## Evidence boundary

This reference documents creative intent. It does not provide actual footage IDs, timecodes, edit-decision records, or independent verification of events. It must not be used to convert planned dialogue, actions, acquisitions, or continuity into verified facts.

# GRP-05 "Slowing Down" — idea review

Reviewed 2026-09-19 by claude. Sixteen ideas (IDEA-0073 to IDEA-0088), twelve episodes (EPD-0036 to
EPD-0047), twenty concepts (C009, C010, C020, C025, C029, C034–C048), plus C032, which belongs to another
group and is carried by EPD-0043's lesson. All sixteen ideas were rewritten into the
situation/concept-merge shape; `premise`, `what_camera_could_observe`, `possible_arc` and `location` were
deleted from every one of them, and every real place name was scrubbed from idea text and loglines. This
is the largest group in the slate and the one whose concepts are most nearly a single continuous
teaching, so the review was run as much on the joins between ideas as on the ideas themselves.

## 1. Philosophy findings and what changed

Every concept link was tested against its digest (`summary_en`, `human_interpretation_en`, `key_terms`,
`does_not_transfer`, `uncertainties`, `cross_source_notes`). Findings in order of seriousness.

1. **IDEA-0079 "One Strike, A Thousand Echoes", C010 — the link read a real person as a liberated one.
   Re-grounded, not dropped.** The old `why` said the keeper's "quiet, unprompted stillness … echoes the
   source's picture of the rootless functional minds, a layer that reaches for nothing". The third of the
   three rootless functional minds *is* the arahant's smile, and the C010 digest's `does_not_transfer` says
   in as many words that "no real person in a film should be framed as having this kind of smile, since
   that would imply a claim about their spiritual attainment" [C010-P:L41; C010-M:L19]. The old `why` half
   guarded this with a parenthesis and then made the claim anyway. The link is now re-grounded on the other
   two of the three minds, which are exactly what a life of waiting is built from: the adverting mind that
   turns the mind-stream toward something and cannot itself know it, "the hinge on the door, not the person
   walking through", and the determining function that settles "this object is such" just before any
   response, both neutral [C010-P:L29–L37]. The digest's own story seed for C010 is a keeper who spends the
   day listening for something that will come, so the re-grounding is the digest's own reading. An absolute
   prohibition on implying any attainment for the keeper is now in the link, in `what_must_be_real` and in
   the risks. EPD-0042's lesson cites only C048 and C047, so it is unaffected.
2. **IDEA-0082 "No One at the Wheel", C038 — the group's own framing is not in this source.** The C038
   digest states plainly that "the group's framing 'no separate seer behind it' is not stated in these two
   extracts" and that "any stronger no-self claim should be sourced from elsewhere, not attributed to
   C038". This matters here more than anywhere, because this episode's title asserts exactly that. The
   digests for C041 and C043 *do* support it — C041's `does_not_transfer` says "what is denied is a
   separate seer standing behind it", and C043's `human_interpretation_en` says "nobody stands behind the
   chain doing the seeing" — so the claim is kept and re-attributed. IDEA-0082's C038 link now carries what
   C038 actually supplies (no act is done by a single mind-moment; the knife simile for why the mind cannot
   study itself) and says outright that the no-seer claim is not attributed to it. The group record's
   `depth` field makes the same attribution error; it is not this review's file to edit, and is listed in
   §5.
3. **IDEA-0081 "Turning Toward", C025 — a technical absence rewritten as a mood.** The old `why` had
   effort and decisiveness "sharp when a fare looks likely, absent through long empty stretches", which
   maps the sources' exclusion table onto a person's energy across a day. What the digest supports is
   narrower and stranger, and is what EPD-0040's lesson actually cites: the ten sense-consciousnesses are
   too weak to carry *any* of the six occasional factors, so the bare moments of seeing and hearing contain
   no effort, no decision and no wish to act at all [C025-P:L75]. Rewritten to that, with the digest's
   warning attached that energy and decisiveness are not virtues in this framework and appear equally in
   harmful minds, so nothing about this driver's drive may be offered as a quality.
4. **IDEA-0086 "Salt That Never Stops Forming", C042 — a production cycle presented as a notation.** The
   old `why` said the pans' repeated cycle "echoes the source's own patterned, repeating notation for a
   fast process". A work rhythm is not a notation, and the C042 digest is clear that the charts exist
   because the sequence cannot be watched at all. Re-grounded on what the digest does support: the charts
   draw *every* moment as three dots, arising, standing and dissolving, because mind and matter share that
   rhythm and differ only in how long the middle beat lasts [C042-M:L3; C039-M:L5]. A salt pan is the rare
   case where a material thing's three beats run slowly enough to watch, so it is the scale analogue of
   what the chart stands in for — stated in the idea as a comparison of scale and never as a claim that a
   pan shows mind-moments. The digest's rule that the charts must never be animated as scientific readouts
   is carried with it. **The same idea's C037 link was also re-grounded**: it previously used the heart-base
   material only to "frame the workers' physical labour", which is decoration. It now carries the join that
   makes C037 belong in this episode at all — the base a mind leans on is itself made of the matter C039
   has just described as continuously replaced, so the support is being rebuilt underneath the thing it
   supports [C037-P:L27].
5. **IDEA-0074 "What the Net Cannot See", C040 — the grading turned into a lesson about attention.** The
   old `why` said the sources' grading "is exactly what separates a flicker a crew member ignores from the
   one they act on", which slides toward effort. The C040 digest blocks this by name: "'pay more attention
   and you will see more' is not quite the source's claim", because the grade is set by the object's
   prominence and timing, with attention only one condition among several. Rewritten, and the idea now
   forbids presenting an unnoticed signal as anyone's lapse. The film also gained the strongest thing in
   the C045 digest that it was not using: Margaya's separation of the object's own desirable or undesirable
   quality, judged by ordinary standards, from the person's response, which is theirs [C045-M:L28–L34] —
   and with it the digest's note that "beauty is in the eye of the beholder" contradicts the source.
6. **IDEA-0073 "The Work Between Moments" — the weakest idea in the group, and the one the coverage work
   fixed.** Its situation was entirely abstract ("a real, bounded task"), which the new shape cannot carry,
   and its C020 `why` named the seven factors without a single one of the digest's four guards. All four
   are now in the link: attention here is not mindfulness and cannot by itself choose good over bad
   [C020-P:L109]; volition as kamma is not "intention is what counts" [C020-P:L75–L79]; the life-faculty is
   explicitly not a soul or "life energy" [C020-P:L97]; one-pointedness is not productivity focus and is
   present in a restless mind, the jolting chariot that still carries its passenger [C020-P:L89]. The idea
   also gained the digest's own best line for this film, that recognition works by marks — each recognising
   leaves a mark, the next recognises by it, recognition grows firm — "that is how a craftsperson learns to
   see a flaw at a glance, and why they often cannot explain how they know". IDEA-0075 was folded in to give
   it a real trade (see §3). **C032 was checked and kept.** It belongs to another group, EPD-0043's lesson
   teaches it, and the digest supports what the idea says: feeling is threefold with no fourth way
   [C032-M:L5; C032-P:L43]. The idea now also carries the digest's distinction between hidden pain (a
   toothache, a mind burning with worry, "which no one knows about unless they ask") and open pain, which is
   the actual reason this film may never read a feeling off a face.
7. **IDEA-0087 "Only Colour, Not Yet a Flower" — a finding about where C041 does *not* belong.** The
   obvious home for the merged IDEA-0075 looked like this film, since both watch a trained eye separate raw
   colour from a name. It is wrong on the sources' own terms: C041's full seventeen-moment process requires
   the eye-sensitivity, the object, **the light** and the attention all to be strong together
   [C041-P:L25], and a pre-dawn market is precisely the case C044 describes, where weak light makes the
   object strike late and the process runs short. So C041 was not carried into IDEA-0087, and IDEA-0075 was
   merged into IDEA-0073 instead, where a lamp held close over a material is a worker deliberately arranging
   all four of C041's conditions. IDEA-0087's own links were tightened with the digest's guard that
   "'perception is an illusion' distorts the text" — colour is a real event for the source and only the
   assembled, named thing is a convention — and with its record of an unreconciled tension in Pradeepika,
   which calls the visible object "unmixed" in one place and a mixture of many clusters' colours in another
   [C036-P:L61; C036-P:L41]. The idea leaves that open rather than choosing.
8. **IDEA-0084 "What the Body Still Carries" — the group's most sensitive idea, held rather than
   dropped.** A film about an elder's hearing loss sits inside a doctrinal frame whose digests carry
   explicit prohibitions: C037's `does_not_transfer` bars turning "beings with complete faculties" into a
   judgement about disabled people and bars presenting the hearing-and-stream-entry claim as saying deaf
   people are spiritually disadvantaged; C009's bars applying Pradeepika's rebirth claim about blindness,
   deafness or muteness [C009-P:L168–L172] to any living person; C034's bars the same for
   [C034-P:L47]. Unlike the comparable case in GRP-07, this one is not an editorial invention: the
   situation is the C037 digest's *own* story seed ("a family finds new ways to reach an elder who has
   gradually lost their hearing"), and the C045 digest independently proposes it too. So it is held, not
   dropped — but held rather than folded into a kept film, precisely so that a real person's hearing loss is
   not carried into an episode whose doctrinal frame the digests forbid applying to it. All three
   prohibitions are now written into the idea itself rather than left implied.
9. **Smaller corrections made across the group.** IDEA-0076's C035 link now says the traditional
   descriptions of sense sensitivity ("the size of a louse's head", reported as tradition at [C035-M:L9])
   are not anatomy, and the idea refuses the phrase "doors of perception", which the digest rules out; its
   C037 link now says plainly, as the digest asks, that the heart-base is a point where the tradition and
   modern physiology part ways. IDEA-0077's C047 link carries the digest's own note that the source's
   worked example names a real monument and that this is source text, never a location suggestion, and the
   digest's warning that "'living in the past is unhealthy' adds a judgement the source does not make" — the
   single easiest wrong turn for a film about a returning migrant worker. IDEA-0083 now forbids slow motion
   offered as a view of the moment, because C044's digest says the moment-counts "should not be dramatised
   as timings or reaction speeds", and carries the digest's other guard, that "if you didn't really notice
   it, you aren't responsible" is not the source's claim. IDEA-0088 and IDEA-0082 both carry, in the link
   text rather than only in the risks, the fact that the sources never use the word *choice* for impulsion
   and describe no chooser behind it [C041-P:L46], so the series' reading is named as the series' own.
10. **No source contradiction was resolved.** Where the digests record a disagreement or an open question —
    the two sources' different identifications of the mind door (the life-continuum itself versus its arrest
    moment), Margaya's three follow-on processes against Pradeepika's four [C048-M:L23–L27; C046-P:L117–L151],
    the 28-versus-29 count of non-decisive impulsions, whether a mind has a distinct standing phase at all —
    the ideas leave it open and say so. Nothing in this group asserts more confidently than its sources do.

## 3. Merges

- **IDEA-0075 "The Weight of a Stone" → IDEA-0073 "The Work Between Moments."** The most consequential
  merge in the group, because IDEA-0073 was the only idea in the slate with no situation at all. It now has
  one: a material read by eye in about a second, a verdict that cannot be explained by the person giving it,
  and a working sequence that cannot be undone. The fold added a grounded **C036** link — the eye meets only
  colour and the grade, the name and the price are added from what has been seen before [C036-P:L37–L41;
  C036-M:L9] — which is the concept the digest's own story seed for C020 (a sorter separating rough stones
  by colour) reaches for. C036 moves from three carries to four, and more importantly IDEA-0073 moves from a
  brief to a film. IDEA-0075's C041 link was deliberately **not** carried forward (see §1, finding 7);
  C041's four conditions belong to a lamp held close, and the idea says so, but EPD-0043's concept set and
  lesson are about the universals, and C041 is already carried three times.
- **IDEA-0078 "The Interpreter's Pause" → IDEA-0085 "The Score for a Vanishing Sound."** Both make a fast,
  invisible sequence audible, and the C038 digest's own story seed is a court or hospital interpreter. The
  fold gave IDEA-0085 the two strongest things C038 has and it was not using: the source's own list of what
  needs a whole series, which includes *understanding another person's words* [C038-M:L3], and Margaya's
  knife simile — the mind is the hardest thing to study because it is the thing doing the studying, so even
  students know these processes only by inference [C038-M:L5]. That is the exact problem a teacher who can
  only sing what she is teaching is solving. It also brought a **C046** link: the object replayed, then
  named, and only then understood well enough to be produced [C046-P:L117–L151], which is a student's
  months compressed into the sources' beats and is flagged in the idea as a comparison of scale. C046 moves
  from one carry to two.
- **IDEA-0080 "The Bid" → IDEA-0083 "The Close Call."** Different trades, same moment: a fast public
  judgement under doubt with money or a match riding on it, and nothing to revisit afterwards. The fold
  brought **C029** and **C009**, and the C029 digest's own story seed is an umpire at a local tournament
  deciding a close call, so the concept was arriving where the digest already put it. What it adds to the
  film is the thing a sports treatment would never reach: the digest's reading of the rootless table as a
  sequence — bare seeing carries only the seven universals, receiving and investigating add landing, staying
  and settling, determining adds effort for the first time, and not one of those steps contains any wish to
  act [C029-P:L31–L55]. Whatever a crowd reads into an umpire's face in that second, the sources place
  nothing of the kind there. C029 moves from one carry to two and C009 from two to three.

## 4. Holds and drops

- **HOLD — IDEA-0084 "What the Body Still Carries."** Reasons in §1, finding 8. It is well grounded, it is
  the C037 digest's own story seed, and it is the counterpart the group's opening film deserves — but the
  twelve slots are filled, and its concept ground (C037, C035, C048) is carried live by IDEA-0076,
  IDEA-0086, IDEA-0087 and IDEA-0079. Held for a later slot, or for a group that can carry a household's
  long adaptation without a doctrinal frame the digests prohibit applying to it. If it is ever placed, its
  elder's separate, ongoing, revocable consent is the first condition of making it at all.
- **No drops.** All four unused ideas were either merged or held. None duplicated another group's work and
  none was weak enough to lose.

## 5. Still weak, and lessons needing an update

1. **`development/analysis/abhidhamma-depth-map.json` will fail its check until it is regenerated.** Three
   episodes gained a concept: EPD-0043 +C036, EPD-0044 +C046, EPD-0045 +C009 and +C029. That changes the
   `linked_episode_ids` of four concepts and the `summary.concept_status_counts`. I deliberately did **not**
   edit that file: it is a single global artefact, other groups are being finalised in parallel, and they
   would collide on the same summary block. It should be regenerated once all ten groups are done.
2. **The group record's `depth` field makes the attribution error described in §1, finding 2.** It says the
   cognitive process means "'I see' is a rapid sequence of mind-moments with no separate seer behind it",
   and the group `lesson` correctly notes that the sources never state this. But the C038 digest is specific
   that *C038* does not support it while C041 and C043 do, and the group's own concept role gloss for C038
   is where the claim is attached. `development/groups/GRP-05.json` is not this review's file to edit; the
   fix is a one-line re-attribution, not a change of claim.
3. **Three episode lessons are now narrower than their episode's concept set.** None is inaccurate, so per
   the brief none was edited. EPD-0043's lesson covers the seven universals and feeling but not C036's point
   that the eye meets only colour and the grade is added afterwards. EPD-0044's covers the charts, the
   series and the seventeen moments but not C046's naming-before-meaning steps. EPD-0045's covers the
   shortened process and the doubt/decision exclusion but not C029's and C009's account of what the
   machinery beneath a ruling does and does not contain. Each is a one- or two-sentence addition.
4. **Six concepts are carried by exactly one kept idea each: C020, C039, C043, C044, C045 and C048.** In a
   group of twenty concepts and twelve films that is unavoidable arithmetic, not a fault, and there is no
   honest way to fix it by invention. It is the reason all three unused-but-sound ideas were merged rather
   than held: each merge moved a concept from a weaker carry to a stronger one. The thinnest of the six is
   C020, which now rests entirely on IDEA-0073 — an idea that had no situation at all until this pass.
5. **C040 sits just under IDEA-0087 and was deliberately not added.** A market graded in pre-dawn light is
   literally C040's own criteria — size, light, nearness — at work, and the link would be defensible. It was
   left out because EPD-0039's lesson does not carry it, C040 is already carried twice, and a fourth episode
   gaining a concept would add depth-map churn for a concept that does not need it. If the film is ever
   developed and the light turns out to be its real subject, this is the first link to add.
6. **Two kept ideas depend on things nobody can schedule, and one on a person nobody can replace.**
   IDEA-0088 needs a day where supply genuinely runs short, and now says in `how_it_unfolds` and in its
   risks that no shortage may be engineered, worsened or waited for as an opportunity. IDEA-0083 needs an
   appeal close enough to be agonising, which may simply not happen in the matches available. IDEA-0079
   depends entirely on one keeper being willing to describe his own private hours, and has no fallback if he
   is not; that is the single largest research risk in the group.
7. **Consent is the recurring weak point, not doctrine.** Five of the twelve kept films involve people who
   are filmed at a moment when refusing is hardest: passers-by who never notice being looked at (IDEA-0081),
   growers at the end of an overnight journey (IDEA-0087), passengers who did not choose a camera in the
   vehicle (IDEA-0082), people at the front of a queue being handed less than the person before them
   (IDEA-0088), and an umpire who still has to live among the people a disputed call went against
   (IDEA-0083). Each idea's `drop_if` now names its own version of this, but it is a production problem that
   the idea stage cannot solve.
8. **IDEA-0073's merged situation is the loosest join in the group.** Folding a specific trade into a film
   that was written to be open works at the level of the concept, and the situation is now real rather than
   abstract, but a reader will notice that the idea still declines to say which trade. That is deliberate —
   it is the group's pilot idea and carries its own source doc — but if the openness ever feels evasive in
   the brief, the right repair is to restore IDEA-0075 as its own film, not to blur IDEA-0073 further.

# GRP-03 idea review — Anger and Fear

Reviewed 2026-09-19 by claude. Thirteen ideas (IDEA-0033 to IDEA-0045), nine episodes (EPD-0019 to
EPD-0027), four concepts (C007, C022, C026, C028). All thirteen ideas rewritten into the
situation/concept-merge shape; `premise`, `what_camera_could_observe`, `possible_arc` and `location`
deleted from every one. One new idea was created in the spare block (IDEA-0221) and marked `hold`.

This is the group that sits on aversion, anger, fear, envy and remorse, so the digests' `does_not_transfer`
and `uncertainties` entries are not background here: several of them describe, almost exactly, the mistake
each of these films is most likely to make. Where that is the case the limit is written inside the concept
link, not left to a generic risk line.

## 1. Philosophy findings and what changed

Every concept link was tested against its digest (`summary_en`, `human_interpretation_en`, `key_terms`,
`cross_source_notes`, `does_not_transfer`, `uncertainties`). Findings in order of seriousness:

1. **IDEA-0041 "Which Word Carries the Water", C022 — the group's most dangerous single line, and the
   idea walked straight at it.** The digest places under `does_not_transfer` the warning that Pradeepika's
   description of equal-conceit includes egalitarian-sounding statements such as "we are all equal in every
   respect" [C022-P:L109], that the source is about how a person measures themselves and *not* a teaching
   against social equality, and that quoting it out of context "would badly mislead". It separately flags
   L109 in `uncertainties` as needing expert confirmation before any public use. This film is a meeting of
   farmers arguing for an equal share of a scarce resource: it is the one situation in the whole slate where
   a doctrinal line about equal-conceit could be heard as saying that claiming an equal share is a fault.
   The same digest bars the other direction too, that "I am inferior" counted as conceit must never be used
   to moralise about low self-worth — and a tail-end farmer saying he always comes last is exactly the person
   that would land on. Both bars are now inside the primary link, in `what_must_be_real` and in the risks,
   and the film is restricted to the general distinction between the two grips, with the sub-kinds of birth,
   virtue and merit [C022-P:L107] explicitly excluded.
   **The logline was also rewritten, and EPD-0024's with it.** The old one read "Elders argue for how it has
   always been done; younger farmers argue for their own standing and results", which pre-assigns wrong view
   to one generation and conceit to the other — precisely the diagnosis the episode's own `caution` disavows
   two paragraphs later. It now says the argument slides between what is true about the water and who is owed
   respect, without sorting the room.
2. **IDEA-0040 "Lamps on the Lagoon", both links — one loose, one inverted. Dropped.** Its C022 link offered
   a crew's steady, unbroken all-night rhythm as "an image of sustained, unsettled attention" for
   restlessness. Restlessness is agitation that will not settle on its object [C022-P:L31]; sustained
   attention is its opposite, so the link does not weaken the concept, it reverses it. The C007 link read a
   livelihood's ordinary uncertainty as an occasion for aversion although no aversion appears anywhere in
   the situation. The one thing in the digests this film genuinely sits on is the compendium's moth-and-lamp
   image [C022-P:L67], and the digest's own note on that seed says the crew must never be framed as the
   moths. There is nothing to repair and nothing to fold forward: the concept links were removed rather than
   rewritten, the idea is dropped, and the title's place reference was scrubbed on the way out.
3. **IDEA-0037 "What the Body Remembers", C028 — the factor counts were used as a severity score.** The old
   `why` argued that both hate-rooted states are "the most crowded on the sources' list" and therefore never
   light. The digest's first `does_not_transfer` entry says in as many words that the counts are not a
   severity score and that a 22-factor state is not worse than a 15-factor one. Re-grounded on the claim the
   digest does support, that both hate-rooted minds are counted without joy at all while greed-rooted minds
   can carry delight [C028-P:L31]. Two further limits are now inside the link, because this is a film about
   one man's character over decades: the absence of joy is stated of a single mind-moment, "not of anyone's
   character or life", which is the whole difference between this film and a verdict; and effort and thought
   are present in *every* unwholesome mind [C028-P:L27], so the old temper may not be presented as a loss of
   control that excuses it. On the C007 side, three claims are now explicitly fenced: the bodily effects at
   L47 are traditional teaching and not physiology, the lower-realms-at-death claim [C007-P:L51] stays out,
   and "anger only hurts yourself" may not be used in a film whose subject harmed other people, since the
   same text says plainly that this state leads to harming others [C007-P:L53].
4. **IDEA-0033 "The Mended Net", C007 — the source's killing example was being carried into a dispute
   between real crews.** The old primary link ran on Pradeepika's example of a wish turning to hatred the
   moment something living stands in its way [C007-P:L59], read as "a rival boat working another crew's
   ground is exactly that turn". That example is about a wish becoming "remove the obstacle" where the
   obstacle is a living being, and applying it to a named rival crew attributes to real people an intention
   they have not expressed. Re-grounded on what the digest supports for this situation: aversion defined by
   its object and aim [C007-P:L32–L34], the constant painful feeling [C007-P:L25–L28], and Margaya's own
   list of occasions — a request refused, one's word not accepted, others thought to have done wrong
   [C007-M:L17], which is the list folded in from the IDEA-0034 merge. L59 now lives only where the digest
   itself puts it, in IDEA-0035, with its own guard attached. Margaya's refusal to exempt anger at an enemy
   who comes to harm you [C007-M:L11] is written into the link, because a wronged crew is exactly where a
   viewer supplies the "righteous anger" exception the sources never make.
5. **IDEA-0033, C022 — a concept borrowed by analogy. Withdrawn, not repaired.** The old supporting link
   read a contested fishing ground as *āvāsa*, one of the five things stinginess clings to. The digest
   glosses that kind as one's dwelling, and the five kinds it records are dwelling, family, gain, praise and
   knowledge; shared working water is not among them on any reading of the digest, and stretching "dwelling"
   to "territory" imports a meaning the source does not give. The claim is withdrawn. C022 is re-grounded on
   what it does support for this film: hate's wider family, which both sources extend to discontent, disgust
   and displeasure and Pradeepika extends to the wish for revenge [C022-P:L121], plus the four present in
   every unwholesome moment [C022-P:L31]. The link is stronger after the withdrawal than before it.
6. **A simile that is not in the sources, used by four ideas.** IDEA-0034, IDEA-0036, IDEA-0038 and
   IDEA-0044 all described restlessness as "wind that keeps shaking the leaves of a tree" and cited it
   [C022-P:L73; C022-M:L19]. The digest inventories this source's similes — dark forest, monkey-glue, snake,
   rust, moth, feather, crossroads — and wind-in-the-leaves is not among them. The image has been removed
   from all four rather than re-attributed, and restlessness is now carried on the claim the digest does
   record: it is one of the four factors present in every unwholesome mind [C022-P:L31; C022-M:L3].
7. **IDEA-0035 "The Watch Hut", C007 and C022 — the right example, the wrong citation, and a missing
   guard.** The old C007 link attributed to [C007-M:L3] the statement that fear is "the wish to get away
   from a threat judged too strong". Margaya at L3 only gathers fear under the word hate; the judged-too-
   strong description belongs to Pradeepika in the C022 material [C022-P:L121], and the citation has been
   corrected. L59 is retained here, because this is where the digest's own story seed places it, but with
   the guard the seed implies written in: the example describes a turn inside a mind, and the film may never
   suggest that any real farmer wants an animal destroyed, may never film or encourage harm, and may never
   present deterrence as the example's conclusion. The C022 `does_not_transfer` line that "fear is healthy
   survival instinct" does not map cleanly onto this material is now in the link — load-bearing for a film
   about a person afraid of something genuinely dangerous — alongside the "sleep is laziness" bar, so that a
   watcher nodding off at four is not offered as an illustration of *thīna-middha*.
8. **IDEA-0039 and IDEA-0042 — a textually unsettled aspect of stinginess, now avoided.** Both films turn on
   the four aspects of not bearing one's things to be shared, used, matched or known about. The digest flags
   the fourth: Margaya L43 reads as "indicating" one's own attainments where Pradeepika has "not bearing
   others to know of it", probably an OCR error, "needs checking against the print". IDEA-0039 in particular
   is about a method kept out of sight, so the "cannot bear it to be known" aspect is the one it would
   naturally reach for. Both links now say the film rests nothing on that aspect. Both also carry the
   digest's refusal of the English equation "stinginess is greed" [C026-M:L19], which is the single most
   likely misreading of either film.
9. **IDEA-0043 "Willing Hands", C026 — an analogy that needed both of its limits.** The idea was already
   honest that the link is analogical, but only guarded one side of it. The digest's `does_not_transfer`
   adds the other: "being pushed excuses it" is ruled out, because prompting changes the texture, not the
   moral quality [C026-P:L43]. Both are now in the link, together with the digest's open question about how
   the delusion-rooted minds are classified by prompting [C026-P:L47], so the film does not present the
   spontaneous/prompted split as tidier than the sources leave it.
10. **IDEA-0045 "Not Yet Decided" — already careful; tightened and given the source's own image.** The doubt
    boundary [C022-P:L179; C022-M:L57] was correctly honoured and remains the reason C022 is linked at all.
    Added: the digest's bar on quoting any fixed recipe of factors for a real person's state, which rules out
    narrating an inventory over this household; the note that L179 mentions eight objects of doubt without
    listing them, so the film cannot say what they are; and, on the positive side, Pradeepika's crossroads
    image for the doubt it does mean [C022-P:L179], which the film may use for exhaustion without importing
    the classification.
11. **No source contradiction was resolved.** Where the digests record a disagreement or an open question —
    Pradeepika and Margaya's different reasons why view and conceit never coincide; whether the
    delusion-rooted minds count as spontaneous; Margaya's disputed fourth aspect of stinginess; what lifts
    strong remorse [C022-P:L151] — the ideas leave it open and say so.
12. **Two sensitivities that touch the whole group, checked and found absent.** The C007 digest's hardest
    lines are that Margaya technically classes grief at a loved one's death as hate-rooted [C007-M:L19;
    C007-P:L49], that "in ordinary language this would sound cruel and false", and that a film must never
    imply mourners are hateful. No idea in this group uses grief, and none has been given one. Likewise
    nothing in the group uses the claim that these minds at the time of death lead to lower realms
    [C007-P:L51]; it is now named as a bar in the two ideas closest to it.

## 3. Merges

- **IDEA-0034 *Match Point* → IDEA-0033 *The Mended Net*.** What it added: Margaya's own list of everyday
  occasions — a request refused, one's word not accepted, others thought to have done wrong [C007-M:L17] —
  which is now IDEA-0033's primary grounding in place of the killing example it was misusing; and the
  aftermath strand, what the people who were angriest are doing a few minutes later, which is now in
  `situation.how_it_unfolds`, in `concept_merge.what_it_reveals` and as a named risk. That strand is what
  keeps the film from reading as a portrait of angry people, which is the reading the C026 digest rules out.
  The merge changed no concept links, so EPD-0019's `concept_ids` are unchanged; its logline was updated to
  carry the decision the days of mending actually contain.
- **IDEA-0036 *Third Gear* → IDEA-0038 *Held Up*.** What it added: the only thing IDEA-0038 lacked, a person
  inside the situation on whom all of the displeasure lands. A repair shop's subject is someone who receives
  other people's refusals for a living; a bus driver and conductor occupy that position under real pressure,
  in one hour, in front of everybody. It is now in `who_is_involved`, in `what_is_at_stake`, in the C007 link
  and in the loglines of both the idea and EPD-0023. It also brought Margaya's "a thing asked for and not
  given" [C007-M:L17] into a link that had only Pradeepika's general occasion. No concept change.
- **IDEA-0044 *The Number You're Given* → IDEA-0045 *Not Yet Decided*.** What it added: the counters, the
  numbers and the half-finished paperwork, which turn out to be what a long deferred decision is actually
  made of — a year of it is spent waiting for offices to confirm things that could be reversed a week later.
  That is now in `situation.what_happens`, and it brought a grounded restlessness strand [C022-P:L31] into
  IDEA-0045's C022 link, which previously existed only as a boundary. No concept change, so EPD-0027's
  `concept_ids` are unchanged.

## 4. Holds and drops

- **HOLD — IDEA-0221 *What Cannot Be Undone* (new, from the spare block).** Remorse is named in the group's
  own `depth` and in its `lesson.the_teaching` ("Envy, stinginess and remorse arise only with this aversion,
  one at a time"), it is one of the four readings the C022 digest treats at length, and no kept idea carries
  it. The film is the digest's own example: a wrong done in the pride of youth and recalled in old age
  [C022-P:L141], and the distinction the source draws between remembering it, which it does not treat as the
  fault, and brooding over it, which it says makes the wrong heavier [C022-P:L143]. It is held, not slated:
  the nine episodes are filled, and it should not displace a kept film. Its own hardest claim, that strong
  remorse is lifted only with certain help [C022-P:L151], is flagged by the digest as needing expert checking
  and is excluded from the idea.
- **DROP — IDEA-0040 *Lamps on the Lagoon* (retitled *Lamps on the Water* before dropping).** Reasons in
  finding 2. Its concept links were removed rather than rewritten, and nothing is folded forward.
- No idea was dropped as a duplicate of another group's work.

## 5. Still weak, and lessons needing an update

1. **Envy has no film, and I did not invent one.** After remorse was covered by the held IDEA-0221, envy
   (*issā*) remains the one factor named in the group record's own `concepts[]` gloss for C022 with nothing
   carrying it. It survives only as a boundary inside IDEA-0042 and IDEA-0039, where Pradeepika's separation
   of envy (looking outward) from stinginess (looking inward) [C022-P:L133] is used to keep those films
   honest about which concept they are in. The C022 digest's own seed for envy is results day at a school
   gate, and the digest itself names the risk — children shamed publicly, no child's result identifiable.
   That is a real gap and the honest options are a slate revision or leaving it; it is not something to fill
   by invention.
2. **No episode `lesson` is made inaccurate by any rewrite, and none was edited.** Two are now narrower than
   their film. EPD-0019's lesson runs entirely on the wanting-turns-to-anger example [C007-P:L59], which is
   the citation the idea has stopped leaning on; the lesson is not wrong, since the turning point it
   describes is real in the source, but it no longer matches the idea's centre of gravity and would read
   better with Margaya's occasions [C007-M:L17] beside it. EPD-0023's lesson covers the displeasure and the
   restlessness but not the merged strand, that all of it lands on the crew. Both would be one-sentence
   additions, and both belong to whoever owns the lessons.
3. **Three kept films depend on events nobody can schedule.** A breakdown (IDEA-0038), an allocation meeting
   (IDEA-0041) and a communal work day (IDEA-0043) all have to happen on their own. IDEA-0038 is the sharpest
   case, and its `how_it_unfolds` now says plainly that the most likely outcome is that no breakdown occurs
   at all. All three forbid arranging or extending the event, which means the scheduling risk is transferred
   whole to production planning.
4. **IDEA-0041 remains the group's most exposed film even after the repair.** It asks real neighbours to be
   filmed losing an argument about water they need, in a village they will not leave. The consent problem is
   not the people who speak most; it is the people who lose. Its `what_must_be_real` now requires consent
   from those people specifically, and its `drop_if` includes the case where the material could only be cut
   by assigning the two grips to identifiable speakers.
5. **IDEA-0037 is the group's hardest ethical case and the philosophy repair does not solve it.** A film that
   is sympathetic to a man who harmed people risks saying, by structure rather than by narration, that the
   harm mattered chiefly because of what it cost him. The digest's bar on "anger only hurts yourself" is now
   in the link and in the risks, but this is an edit problem, not a doctrine problem, and it will have to be
   solved again in the cut.
6. **C028 is carried by two kept ideas and in both cases as a structural claim rather than as something
   filmable.** That is in the nature of the concept — it is an inventory of what occurs with what — and no
   merge in this group could change it. It is worth knowing that if either EPD-0026 or EPD-0027 falls, the
   concept falls to a single carry.
7. **Repo-wide check:** at the time of writing, `npm run check` reports exactly one failure, "Depth concept
   groups and linked episodes match the slate", which is the known repo-wide depth-map problem being fixed
   separately. Every idea-shape, selection and episode check passes. No episode `concept_ids` changed
   anywhere in this group — every merge folded situation detail into ideas that already carried the same
   concepts — so the depth map is untouched by GRP-03 and cannot be a cause of that failure.

# Retired: the pre-rewrite idea reviews

These ten reports describe a **model the project no longer uses**. They are kept as provenance for
the doctrinal work recorded in them, not as guidance. Do not follow their recommendations.

## What they were

Between 2026-09-19 and 2026-09-21 every candidate idea was reviewed against its concept digests
under the *human-situation* model. In that model an idea was "a plan of a concept merger and the
human situation it lives in", carried no location at all, and competed with its siblings for a fixed
number of episode slots. Each report therefore records:

1. the philosophy findings — every concept link tested against its digest, with the `why` rewritten,
   links dropped where the digest did not support them, and each `does_not_transfer` line that bears
   on a film written into the link or the risks;
2. the kept slate in episode order;
3. merges, holds and drops;
4. what remained weak, and which episode lessons needed an update.

## Why they are retired

Three changes since have invalidated their structure while leaving much of their doctrinal reasoning
intact:

- **The idea shape changed.** `situation` (what happens, who is involved, what is at stake, how it
  unfolds) was replaced by `place`, `two_layers` and a `sequence` of beats pairing what is on screen
  with what the voice says. The series is now footage-led: the location is the subject, not a setting
  chosen later. Every idea was rewritten, and about forty lost their subject entirely because it
  filmed a real person's inner life, illness, disability, bereavement or hardship — which these
  reports' own digest findings forbid.
- **Selections were retired.** These reports argue merges, holds and drops because ideas competed for
  episode slots. Every idea is now `keep`, and selection happens through Chathura's confirm/reject
  verdict against a per-group quota instead.
- **Episodes were deleted.** `development/episodes/` is empty, so every `EPD-` reference in these
  reports points at a record that no longer exists.

## What replaced them

- `docs/planning/08-the-footage-led-slate.md` — the current record of the slate.
- `docs/planning/09-concept-coverage.md` — which concepts each candidate carries, regenerated by
  `development/analysis/concept-coverage.mjs`.
- `docs/planning/prompts/footage-led-idea-brief.md` — the current brief. The old one is kept beside
  it as `retired-idea-finalisation-brief.md`.

## What is still worth reading here

The per-concept philosophy findings. Several were substantial and were carried into the rewrite:
the C049 bar on the source's examples of "rootless" persons, the C038 misattribution of the
"no separate seer" framing, the C074 line cited for the wrong claim, the C056 near-death/weighty
inversion, and the C022 disputed fourth aspect of stinginess. Those findings are why the rewritten
ideas carry the guards they do.

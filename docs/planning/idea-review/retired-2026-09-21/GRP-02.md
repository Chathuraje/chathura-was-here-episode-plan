Folded into `what_happens`, `who_is_involved`, `how_it_unfolds` and `concept_merge`.

Folded into `who_is_involved`, `concept_merge` and `risks`.

No merge changed a kept idea's concept links, and therefore no episode's `concept_ids` changed. Each merged idea's extra link (C021 on 0015, C004/C005 on 0016, C005 on 0023) is already carried elsewhere in the group, so adding it would have altered episode concept sets, their lessons and the shared depth map without improving coverage.

## 4. Holds and drops

- **HOLD — IDEA-0024 What the Bank Took.** Strong, and the only idea in the group that shows a thing wanted, got and then lost. It has no slot: the group's two refusals (0025, 0026) already close the arc at a scale that does not require filming a family's worst morning, and its C004/C032 pairing points forward to what blocked wanting turns into rather than to this group's ending. Held for a later stage on loss, and only if a real, freely and continuously consenting situation is ever found. No episode has been rewired.
- **No drops.** Nothing in the group is weak enough to discard, and no idea here duplicates another group's.

Three lessons already state the exclusions the ideas were missing (EPD-0012 on clothes for protection, EPD-0017 on acts done from need, EPD-0018 on work done with practical wisdom), which is why the corrections were needed in the ideas rather than in the lessons.

The C032 digest states this is not in either extract and belongs to teaching outside them. Every C032-linked idea now names it as editorial. The group record itself was not edited.

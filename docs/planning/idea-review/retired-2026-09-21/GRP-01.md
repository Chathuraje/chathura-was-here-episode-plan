# GRP-01 "The Names of Things" — idea finalisation

Reviewed 2026-09-19 by claude. Twelve ideas (IDEA-0001 to IDEA-0012), eight episodes (EPD-0001 to
EPD-0008), six concepts (C001, C002, C003, C018, C077, C084). All twelve ideas were rewritten into the
situation / concept-merge shape; `premise`, `what_camera_could_observe`, `possible_arc` and `location` were
deleted from every one, and every real place name was scrubbed from idea text and titles.

## 1. Philosophy findings and what changed

Every concept link was tested against its digest (`summary_en`, `human_interpretation_en`, `key_terms`,
`does_not_transfer`, `uncertainties`, `cross_source_notes`). Findings in order of seriousness:

1. **IDEA-0001 "One Mountain, Many Names", C018 — the link was decorative, and is dropped.** The old `why`
   read the view from a summit as "the whole land visible at once, like the full map of the mind seen in one
   view". Nothing in the C018 digest supports an analogy between a landscape and the classification of
   consciousness; the digest is a set of totals and a list (81 + 8 = 89, or 81 + 40 = 121, with the
   instruction to learn the list by heart [C018-P:L23–L27; C018-M:L125]), and its `does_not_transfer`
   warns specifically that the numbers are not the point and that a film dwelling on them becomes a lecture.
   The link could not be re-grounded because there was no doctrinal content in it to re-ground. Dropped, and
   **EPD-0001's `concept_ids` are now `["C084","C001"]`.** This makes the episode consistent with its own
   lesson, whose `sources` block already cites only C084 and C001 and never mentions C018. C018 remains
   covered — properly, as a primary — by IDEA-0007 at EPD-0003, which is where the group's arc puts the map.
2. **IDEA-0001, C001 — the ending made a claim the sources explicitly withhold.** The old `why` set
   "conventional truth (the many names)" against "what remains when the names fall silent at dawn", i.e. a
   silent summit as the ultimate showing itself. The digest blocks this twice: it flags 'nothing is real' as
   the flattening the Margaya's wording invites, and the Pradeepika says the final realities are known by
   omniscient knowledge rather than by human or divine knowing [C001-P:L59] — which the digest itself lists
   under `uncertainties` as needing an expert reading. Rewritten so the film's ground is the positive claim
   the source does make, that everyday names were chosen deliberately so people could understand easily
   [C001-P:L51], with the explicit note that "stone", "wind" and "light" are conventional wholes too. The
   digest's "a film must not tell a participant that the name they use is wrong" is now inside the primary
   C084 link, where it is load-bearing for a film on an interfaith path.
3. **IDEA-0005 "The Same Boat", C001 — the film was built on the wrong puzzle.** The old `why` was "the
   conventional 'same boat' against its ever-changing parts", i.e. identity over time. The C001 digest names
   this failure in as many words in its own story seed for this exact situation: "Turning into a 'Ship of
   Theseus' lecture (a Greek puzzle, not this source's point, which concerns composites rather than identity
   over time)." The sources' test is division, not endurance: a table is legs and boards, and laying the
   pieces on the floor leaves no table although nothing was destroyed [C001-M:L46; C001-M:L52–L56].
   Re-grounded on division, which also moved the film's spine: the situation is now the weeks in which the
   boat is in pieces and everyone still uses the name for the heap. This is what the IDEA-0002 merge is for
   (§3). EPD-0004's lesson already ends on the division test ("Take the boat apart and you find planks, not
   'a boat'"), so the episode needed no change; the idea had drifted away from its own episode.
4. **IDEA-0004 "The Well", C002 — the four great elements were glossed from popular assumptions.** The old
   `why` read "earth, water and heat as real qualities". The digest's `does_not_transfer` is explicit: the
   extract only names the four great elements [C002-P:L40] and does not define them, so a film must not
   gloss them as literal soil, water, flame and wind. Re-grounded on what the digest does support —
   materiality defined as what is altered by opposing conditions such as cold and heat [C002-P:L40;
   C002-M:L17], and the ultimates described as momentary activities or forces rather than substances
   [C002-P:L34; C002-M:L3] — with the bar written into the link, `what_must_be_real` and the risks.
   EPD-0005's lesson already carried this caution; again, the idea was the weaker document.
5. **IDEA-0012 "The Ring of Fire", C002 — the link settled a disagreement the sources leave open.** The old
   `why` said what is actually there is "heat, light and motion, not 'a ring'". That does two forbidden
   things at once: it glosses the undefined great elements (as above), and it decides a contradiction the
   digest records and refuses to resolve — the Pradeepika says the ultimates are not visible to the eye
   [C002-P:L34] while the Margaya says the colour element is [C002-M:L17], and the digest lists this under
   `uncertainties` as needing expert reading. Rewritten to the one claim both sources make, that the
   ultimates are activities rather than substances [C002-P:L34; C002-M:L3], with the film explicitly not
   asserting that the flame is the ultimate and the ring is not. The C001 primary link (the ring of fire as
   the Margaya's own image, [C001-M:L42; C001-M:L48]) was accurate and is kept, with the digest's two
   warnings — 'life is an illusion', and treating a religious occasion as spectacle — moved inside it.
6. **IDEA-0006 "The Taster", C077 — the film's most likely conclusion is the opposite of the source's.** A
   grading room invites the viewer to decide that taste is subjective. The digest's `does_not_transfer`
   says the sources hold the reverse: whether an object is desirable is settled by the result-consciousness,
   measured by an average person, and "cannot be deceived" [C077-P:L193–L201]. That is now inside the
   primary link and the risks. A second limit was added that the old idea had no guard against at all: the
   trade's grading ladder must not be read onto the sources' own inferior/superior aspect, which runs
   through a rebirth cosmology and a premodern social order [C077-P:L203–L207]. The positive grounding was
   strengthened to the sources' own account of the bases as a meeting, with the stick-and-drum image and the
   "village with no one living in it" [C077-M:L47; C077-P:L1337–L1345; C077-P:L1333–L1399].
7. **IDEA-0007 "Learning the Map", C018 and C003 — accurate, but four limits were unguarded.** The primary
   C018 link (the map as a thing learned and recited [C018-M:L125]) is exactly what the digest supports. Now
   written in: the types are momentary kinds of consciousness and not personality types; the map is not a
   ladder of better and worse people; the arithmetic is not the point; and the digest's own note that the
   familiar four-sphere sum is *not printed in either source*, so any chart on screen is an assembled figure
   and cannot be shown as a quotation. On C003, the two sources use "one" in two different senses — mind is
   one in its general nature and divided only for teaching [C003-M:L5] versus a stream that merely seems to
   be one [C003-P:L25] — which the digest says must not be merged; the idea now keeps them apart. The
   author's heart-base-versus-brain argument [C003-P:L59–L69] is likely to be in the curriculum being
   filmed, and the digest's bar on presenting it as settled science is now explicit. The C002 link says
   plainly that the fourth reality can only be named as a horizon and that nothing a camera can film belongs
   to it.
8. **IDEA-0009 "Clearing the House", C002 — the primary link was mis-grounded, and was removed rather than
   rewritten.** The old `why` put "matter on one side, mind (memory, feeling) on the other: what is really
   there in an object". Memory and feeling are not in the object, and the digest guards this exact edge
   ('mind over matter'; the sources keep mind and matter as distinct kinds of reality). The idea's real
   ground is C084 and C001, which is what it now carries; the C002 link is gone and was **not** folded into
   the kept idea it merges into.
9. **IDEA-0008, IDEA-0003 and IDEA-0011 — accurate links, sharper limits.** C084's time-and-direction
   concepts [C084-M:L9; C084-M:L11–L19; C084-P:L27–L41] support IDEA-0008 exactly as written; added were
   the digest's bars on 'language constructs reality' and on the social-construction reading, which C001's
   digest says changes the sources' meaning. IDEA-0011's being-concept link is the cleanest in the group
   [C084-P:L27–L41], and now carries the C077 digest's first prohibition — 'you do not exist' / 'the self is
   an illusion' — which matters more here than anywhere else, because the subject is a real child who cannot
   consent.
10. **Two episode loglines were resynced to the rewrites (Task 4).** EPD-0001's ended "at dawn they all
    stand in front of the same stone, wind and light", which is exactly the silent-dawn-reveals-the-ultimate
    move finding 2 rules out; it now ends on the names doing real work the whole way up. EPD-0004's stated
    the Ship of Theseus framing finding 3 removes ("repaired so many times that no original plank is left,
    but everyone still calls it by the same name"); it now names the weeks in which the boat lies in
    separate pieces, which is still consistent with its lesson, since the lesson carries both the
    replacement and the division. IDEA-0001's `what_the_viewer_could_understand` keeps its episode lesson's
    wording about the shared cold, wind and light — it has to match the lesson, and the claim is about
    shared conditions, not about ultimates — but now says so explicitly, since those are names too. The
    other six loglines were re-read against their rewritten ideas and say nothing the ideas no longer say.
11. **No source contradiction was resolved and no doctrine was invented.** Where the digests record an open
    question — the visible/invisible disagreement about the ultimates, the two senses of the mind being
    "one", the Pradeepika's "does not change from its own nature" beside the impermanence of nāma-rūpa, the
    missing eighth item in the list of meaning-concepts, the unprinted four-sphere sum — the ideas leave it
    open and say so.

## 3. Merges

- **IDEA-0003 "The Name in Use" → IDEA-0001.** Required: EPD-0001 is the slate's only two-idea episode, so
  both of its ideas had to end `keep` or `merge`, and the brief forbids rewiring the episode. IDEA-0003 is
  the pilot's abstract form of IDEA-0001's premise, so the merge is the honest resolution rather than a
  device. What it added: the strand of names as *instructions people act on* rather than labels — someone
  giving directions, someone calling a stop, someone writing the place into a record — which is now in
  IDEA-0001's `what_happens` and `who_is_involved`; and the guard it carried, the digest's "a film must not
  tell a participant that the name they use is wrong" plus the warning against taking sides where naming has
  a political history, which is now inside IDEA-0001's primary C084 link and its risks. No new concept link:
  0003 carried C084 alone.
- **IDEA-0002 "Thread" → IDEA-0005 "The Same Boat".** What it added: the cloth-and-thread division test
  [C084-M:L7], which is the sources' own example and the exact repair finding 3 calls for — the boat film
  was built on persistence through replacement, and now it is built on division. It also brought the
  question of the *moment* at which a heap of parts acquires its name, which is now the hinge of IDEA-0005's
  `what_happens`, and the detail of where the old parts go (fence posts, firewood, a patched roof), which is
  the C001 digest's own observation about this situation. No new concept link: 0002 carried C084 + C077,
  both of which 0005 already had, but 0005's C084 link is now grounded in the division test rather than in a
  general "collective concept".
- **IDEA-0009 "Clearing the House" → IDEA-0010 "The Lantern".** What it added: the observation that the
  parts of a named whole are themselves named after people — inherited bamboo, a frame kept from last year,
  a relative's tools — so that what comes down at the end is not only a shape but a set of borrowed and
  remembered things going back to being material; and the human weight of the un-naming itself, which is
  IDEA-0010's own ending and its stated weakness ("festival spectacle with no person to follow"). Both are
  now in IDEA-0010's `what_happens` and its C084 link. Its C002 link was dropped, not folded (finding 8), so
  EPD-0008's `concept_ids` are unchanged.

## 4. Holds and drops

- **HOLD — IDEA-0011 "The First Letter."** Strong and doctrinally the cleanest case of the being-concept
  anywhere in the group: the sources list the person-concept resting on the aggregates by name, and a naming
  is the one occasion when the name is visibly newer than what it names. It is held for two reasons. First,
  there is no slot: eight episodes, eight kept ideas. Second, it is the only idea in the slate that depends
  on no season, festival, procession, dig or repair — just a family — which makes it the standing
  replacement if one of the four event-dependent kept ideas (IDEA-0004, IDEA-0010, IDEA-0012, IDEA-0005)
  fails on access or timing. Its concept ground is not lost meanwhile: C084 is carried by six kept ideas and
  C077 by three.
- **No drops.** Nothing in this group was weak enough, or duplicative enough of another group's work, to
  justify one. The one link that was dropped outright (C018 on IDEA-0001) and the one removed on a merging
  idea (C002 on IDEA-0009) are in findings 1 and 8.

## 5. Still weak, and lessons needing an update

1. **EPD-0001's `concept_ids` changed (C018 removed), so the depth map will report this group as stale.**
   I deliberately did not touch `development/analysis/abhidhamma-depth-map.json`: it is a single global
   artefact, other groups are being finalised in parallel, and they would collide on the same summary block.
   It should be regenerated once all ten groups are done. No episode lesson is affected, because EPD-0001's
   lesson never cited C018.
2. **No episode lesson needs an update.** Each kept idea's `what_the_viewer_could_understand` was written to
   its episode's `lesson.in_simple_terms`, and none of the rewrites made a lesson inaccurate. Two lessons are
   now *narrower* than their idea rather than wrong: EPD-0004's covers the division test but not the
   moment-of-naming question folded in from IDEA-0002, and EPD-0008's covers the aggregates and craving but
   not the point that the parts are named after people. Each would be a one-sentence addition, and neither
   is required.
3. **C018 is carried by exactly one kept idea, and C003 by two.** That is real thinness in the group whose
   declared job is to lay out the whole map. It cannot be honestly fixed here: C018's content is a list and
   two totals, and the only situations in which it is genuinely present are ones where somebody is learning
   it. Adding it back to IDEA-0001 would restore exactly the decorative link finding 1 removes.
4. **The IDEA-0002 merge carried a test, not a trade.** The weaving household itself is not in IDEA-0005;
   what crossed over is the cloth-and-thread example, the moment-of-naming question and the fate of the
   discarded parts. That is enough to re-ground the film, but if C084's division test ever needs to be *seen*
   in a material that genuinely dissolves rather than reasoned about a hull in pieces, the right repair is to
   restore IDEA-0002 as its own film, not to stretch IDEA-0005.
5. **The IDEA-0009 merge is the weakest join in the group.** A house clearance and a festival lantern are
   both named wholes being taken apart, but they are not the same film, and only the transferable
   observation crossed over. The trade was deliberate: IDEA-0009's own situation is the most ethically
   exposed in the slate (a grieving family, an inheritance, consent given at the worst possible moment), and
   folding its observation into a public, collective build gets most of the insight at a fraction of the
   exposure. If it ever reads as bolted on, drop the folded strand rather than resurrect the house film
   without a much stronger consent plan.
6. **Four kept ideas depend on events nobody controls** — a well dug in the window (IDEA-0004), a
   substantial boat repair (IDEA-0005), a procession with fire performers (IDEA-0012) and a full lantern
   build including the dismantling (IDEA-0010). All four now say plainly in `how_it_unfolds` that reality
   decides, and all four forbid arranging the event. The scheduling exposure is real and belongs in
   production planning, which is why IDEA-0011 is held rather than dropped.
7. **IDEA-0006 remains the group's most exposed film on labour.** The doctrinal work is sound, but a
   tasting room is a place where a few seconds of one person's attention set the value of many people's
   work. Its risks now say so, and its `drop_if` refuses access granted on condition that working conditions
   are not filmed.
8. **Repo-wide check:** the remaining `npm run check` failures list only ideas and episodes belonging to
   groups still being processed, plus the known depth-map failure being fixed separately. No GRP-01 id
   appears in any of them.

# GRP-10 idea review — The Way Through

Reviewed 2026-09-19 by claude. Sixteen ideas (IDEA-0173 to IDEA-0188), twelve episodes (EPD-0087 to EPD-0098),
twenty concepts. All ideas rewritten into the situation/concept-merge shape; `premise`,
`what_camera_could_observe`, `possible_arc` and `location` deleted from every one.

## 1. Philosophy findings and what changed

Verified every concept link against its digest (`summary_en`, `human_interpretation_en`, `key_terms`,
`does_not_transfer`, `uncertainties`). Findings, in order of seriousness:

1. **IDEA-0181, C098 — an unverified anecdote was cited as evidence.** The old `why` rested on "the flying
   elder undone by a song". The digest carries no flying elder; it records two brief illustrations of
   defilements arising (Mahātissa; a monk hearing a woman's song) and states explicitly that these are the
   source's own teaching examples, "not verified facts about real places or people and should not be used as
   such". Rewritten to rest only on the three kinds of abandoning — suppression, temporary displacement,
   cutting off — at [C098-P4:L205–L249], with the digest's prohibition on labelling any real person a noble
   person carried into `what_must_be_real`.
2. **IDEA-0180, C016 — a simile attributed to the wrong concept.** "Felling a young tree before it fruits"
   belongs to C098's account of the three abandonings, not to C016. C016's own passage says the path removes
   the ground in which a defilement could arise, so it cannot come back [C016-P:L76–L82] — and the C016 digest's
   own story seed for that passage is a farmer choosing between cutting a weed back and digging it out, which
   is this film. Re-grounded on L76–L82 and the misattribution is now named in the link so it is not repeated.
3. **IDEA-0186, C073 — a phrase from another concept.** The old `why` quoted "dwelling at ease, here and now"
   as C073's description of its deepest state. That phrase is C102's (`diṭṭhadhamma nibbāna sukha vihāra`,
   [C102-P:L92]). Kept the C073 link but re-grounded it on what the film actually needs: the digest denies the
   peace any location, direction or shape, so a literal departure is a counter-image, not an illustration
   [C073-P:L62; C073-P:L74]. The misattribution is flagged inside the link.
4. **IDEA-0187, C015 — the link rested on a passage the digest flags.** It used the cake-and-rice image and
   the "contentment needing nothing added" reading. The digest's `does_not_transfer` says the cake-and-rice
   image is the Margaya's own teaching illustration and must not be quoted as canonical, and its
   `uncertainties` say the Margaya's claim that formless beings have a happiness "very close to nibbāna's"
   "needs a teacher's framing before any use". Applied to an elderly person living with very little, it also
   risked presenting old age and having little as the answer. Re-grounded on the digest's warning instead: a
   long peaceful existence, however refined, is still a result that ends [C015-M:L22]. The link now guards the
   ending rather than decorating it.
5. **IDEA-0175, C013 and C099 — the flow-state trap.** Both digests explicitly block this film's central
   move: C013 says "absorption is the same as flow or being in the zone at work" is a distortion, and C099 says
   "a craftsman's or athlete's absorption can only be an echo or analogy, not an instance of it". The links
   are kept, because the C013 image (bring the object close, look long until it is clear, [C013-P:L48–L52]) is
   the source's own and is what the episode's lesson states; both `why` texts now carry the prohibition, and
   `what_must_be_real` says the film never claims the cutter is meditating or absorbed in the texts' sense.
6. **IDEA-0175, C031 — the weakest surviving link.** C031 is an analytic count of what accompanies an absorbed
   mind; its digest says the counts are "not measurements of experience that a person could feel or report"
   and that work focus is not these states. Nothing in a gem-cutting room carries it. Kept, with an honest
   `why` that marks it as background for the film's restraint, and flagged below as wanting to move.
7. **IDEA-0177, C015 — sensitive framing kept in view.** The fast/slow-learner passage [C015-P:L45–L47] is
   sound, but the digest says it "should not become a ranking of people's worth". The link and the risk list
   now say a master and an apprentice at different paces may be shown only as a difference in pace.
8. **IDEA-0176, C085 — typecasting real people.** The old logline assigned practices by character
   ("a grieving widow", "a proud young trader", "a doubtful engineer"). The digest supports matching an object
   to a person's temperament, but that is a teacher's judgement about a practice, not a label a film may hang
   on a participant, and the widow framing edged toward grief-as-a-condition-to-be-treated. Logline rewritten
   with no typecasting; the risk is listed.
9. **IDEA-0184, IDEA-0185 (C089, C090, C101) — supernormal powers.** All three digests require that the
   powers be presented only as what the texts describe, neither asserted as fact nor debunked, and C090 warns
   against turning the protection stories into a promise that good people are kept safe. Both ideas now say
   this in the links, in `what_must_be_real` and in the risks. C090's noble power is explicitly "belongs to
   the fully freed alone", so the worker's and crew's steadiness is stated as an echo, never an instance.
10. **Verified as accurate and left standing:** C073 in IDEA-0174 (the Margaya argues from pawning and selling
    land, [C073-M:L5–L7] — the digest's own example, not an imposed reading); C100's rope-and-canal simile
    [C100-P4:L44–L46]; C017's "same stage, different texture of arrival" [C017-P:L25]; C016's "carries one
    across" [C016-P:L25]; C088's four cloths [C088-P4:L280–L284]; C014's men hanging from a roof [C014-P:L68];
    C087's disc preparation [C087-P4:L209–L213]; C085's forty subjects matched by a teacher [C085-P:L47];
    C096's practical strand of wisdom [C096-P4:L30, L66–L72]; C097's ordering and its lay ending
    [C097-M:L3, M:L10]; C099's five masteries [C099-P:L69–L79]; C102's four preliminary duties
    [C102-P:L43–L62]; C076's raft of eight bound logs [C076-P:L1413] and the charioteer's balance
    [C076-P:L783].

Also done across all sixteen: every real place name scrubbed from idea text (the gem town, the lagoon, the
region names all gone; "chena" replaced with plain description); "the dry zone", "hill country" and similar
regional reasoning removed; kinds of setting kept only where the situation needs them (a class, a crossing, a
plot worked in cycles). No episode `location` block was touched. EPD-0098's ending is described only as the
planned crowded-location event shared with Episodes 1 and 100, with no other shot language.

## 3. Merges

- **IDEA-0173 (The Levelled Disc) → IDEA-0176 (Forty Doors).** Added the object itself to the class film: clay
  picked clean of grit, levelled like a drum skin, re-levelled every time it cracks, kept dull on purpose.
  This turns C087 from a passing mention into a link with a physical body, and brings the digest's warning
  that the powers listed as the practice's fruits must never be heard as a promise. Folded into
  `situation.what_happens`, `who_is_involved`, `concept_merge`, the C087 link, `what_must_be_real` and the
  risks.
- **IDEA-0184 (Not Thrown About) → IDEA-0185 (The Rarest Readiness).** Added the quiet end of the list of ten:
  the one mark the texts reserve for the fully freed is not a feat but an even response to the repulsive and
  the alluring [C090-P4:L85–L87; C090-M:L13]. The readiness film now also watches the ordinary unpleasant work
  between alarms, which gives C090 a real reading instead of a gesture, and carries 0184's dignity conditions
  for low-status labour into the risks.

Neither merge changed an episode's `concept_ids`: every concept the merged idea brought was already linked by
the kept idea.

# GRP-09 idea review — Nothing Stands Alone

Reviewed 2026-09-19 by claude; this report written 2026-09-20 from the finished records, with every
concept link re-tested against its digest and a second corrective pass made on the same day
(findings 1, 7, 10 and 11 below record what that pass changed). Eleven ideas (IDEA-0153 to IDEA-0163), eight episodes
(EPD-0079 to EPD-0086), seven concepts (C075, C078, C079, C080, C081, C082, C083). All eleven ideas
are at version 2 in the situation/concept-merge shape; `premise`, `what_camera_could_observe`,
`possible_arc` and `location` are gone from every one, and no real place name survives anywhere in
idea text or idea titles.

## 1. Philosophy findings and what changed

This is the conditionality group, so the whole slate sits one short step away from two failures the
digests name directly: "kamma as fate", and reading a person's circumstances as something they
earned. That check is finding 1. The rest are in order of seriousness.

1. **The kamma-as-fate boundary, tested idea by idea.** Three digests carry the dangerous material.
   C075's tree simile links good roots in a past life to wealth, honour and qualities and says a
   person reborn from weak merit may live as a servant with barely enough to eat [C075-P:L41–L59];
   its `does_not_transfer` says this "must not be carried into any film as an explanation or
   justification of poverty, class, caste or disability". C082 blocks "'you reap what you sow' as
   cosmic justice", describing kamma-condition results as inert and quiet rather than reward or
   punishment [C082-P:L6357–L6367; C082-P:L6721], and rules that the passages on the five gravest
   acts "must not become a moral threat or a verdict on anyone who appears on screen"
   [C082-P:L5285; C082-P:L5289]. C080 adds that the text denies a self who acts or receives
   [C080-P:L1619], that past action and present result are conditions and "not personal authorship
   or blame", and that reading ageing and grief as punishment distorts it, since birth itself
   conditions them [C080-P:L1569]. C081 adds the non-agency method — no one runs the wheel, it is
   not a reward-and-punishment system administered by anyone [C081-P:L541–L545] — and bars
   pop-psychology "past-life trauma".
   Against that: **the two kept ideas whose subjects are actually exposed both carry the guard
   explicitly.** IDEA-0153 (a farming household's season, which can end in a thin harvest) names the
   tree simile in its risks in as many words. IDEA-0162 (a working community through a hard wet
   season) carries three: that circumstances do not decide character, that the tree simile "must
   never be applied to people on screen", and that decisive support is a strong support and not a
   sole cause [C082-P:L5623–L5643; C082-P:L5697–L5719]. Its `what_the_viewer_could_understand` and
   EPD-0085's lesson both say so too.
   **Three kept ideas lacked the guard; two have now been given it, where the digest actually
   requires it rather than as boilerplate.** IDEA-0155 needed it most and got it in both places, the
   way 0153 and 0162 do it: its primary C081 link now carries the non-agency method — no one runs
   the wheel, it is not a reward-and-punishment system administered by anyone — with the plain
   instruction that the household's shrinking livelihood and legal jeopardy may never read as
   something their past earned them, and a matching named risk was added (finding 7).
   **IDEA-0163** gained one risk only: a night's firing ends in a real loss for named people, and
   C082's kamma condition is technical, its results described as inert and quiet rather than reward
   or punishment [C082-P:L6357–L6367; C082-P:L6721], so a lost load must not read as desert or luck.
   Nothing was added to its links, because its C082 link is about nutriment and presence, and
   loading an unrelated relation into it would have been boilerplate.
   **IDEA-0160 was checked and deliberately left alone.** It films a crew at work, not anyone's
   circumstances or fortunes; the C082 material it actually links is mutuality, whose own bars (the
   stated limit, and "not community cooperation or mutual aid") it already carries in full. The
   digest requires no kamma guard there, so none was invented.
2. **No concept link is unsupported.** All eighteen links across the eight kept ideas are grounded in
   something the digest states rather than in something the situation suggested. The five that carry
   the most weight were checked word by word: the rice-seedling nutriment simile, where the seed's
   strength is chief for beginning and water and fertiliser for growing and lasting
   [C075-M:L130] (IDEA-0153); the four-men-and-a-vine image for the four ways the chain is taught,
   with both books denying a first beginning [C081-P:L33–L54; C081-P:L66; C081-M:L63] (IDEA-0155);
   the three scales of teaching, with Margaya's insistence that the brief and the detailed accounts
   are both needed [C078-M:L13; C078-P:L35] (IDEA-0156); mutuality as sticks upright only by leaning
   on each other's tips, *with the same passage's limit* (IDEA-0160, below); and the natural kind of
   decisive support, named as bodily pleasure and pain, weather, food and lodging (IDEA-0162).
3. **IDEA-0160 "Leaning Poles" is the group's best-behaved idea and the model for the rest.** The
   digests' single most repeated warning across this group is that "everything is connected" is too
   loose — C082 says it four separate times, C078 and C080 once each, C083 once. IDEA-0160 does not
   merely avoid the slogan, it films the refutation: the C082 link states the limit inside the link
   ("not everything that arises together supports everything else, and mind-born matter does not
   support the mind in return" [C082-P:L3346; C082-P:L2822]), the C083 link keeps the proportion
   right (mutuality is one of the nine ways mind-and-matter support mind-and-matter [C083-M:L29]),
   and the risks carry C082's separate bar on reading mutuality as community cooperation or mutual
   aid. The decorations that hang from the frame and give it nothing back are the one-way half of
   the same passage. Nothing here needed repair.
4. **IDEA-0163 "When the Kiln Goes Out" was re-grounded off the absence condition.** Its own
   `selection.reason` records this: the film now rests on nutriment (support by something that must
   keep arriving) and presence (support given by a thing still being there), with the absence
   condition present only as the fact that the stopping belongs to the process. That is the right
   call, because C082's `does_not_transfer` blocks exactly the reading a kiln invites — "'letting go
   makes room for the new' borrows the absence condition too loosely… not about life choices or
   decluttering" — and blocks the warm reading of presence too ("'just being present is a gift'…
   it also supports craving" [C082-P:L8712–L8722]). Both bars are now named risks on the idea, and
   EPD-0086's `caution` carries the first of them.
5. **IDEA-0157 "Boat to Plate" avoids the eleven/twelve trap.** C079's `uncertainties` warn that the
   extract's compiler calls the passage "eleven-limbed" [C079-P:L12] while the books elsewhere list
   twelve factors, and that the wording "should not be quoted as 'eleven factors'". The link says
   "one clause pattern repeated eleven times, each clause taking up what the last produced" — eleven
   clauses joining twelve factors, which is the digest's own recommended reading, and it does not
   name a count of factors at all. The C079 bar on a domino chain with one cause per step is carried
   in both the link and the risks, and EPD-0081's `caution` states it.
6. **IDEA-0156 "What Stopped the Pump" carries C078's own sharpest bar.** The digest blocks
   "everything happens for a reason" on the ground that the source rules out a maker and that a
   condition is not a purpose or a plan. It is in the risks, in the episode's `caution`, and the
   idea's `concept_merge.what_it_reveals` reframes the film around the question the sources prefer:
   not what caused this, but in what particular way did each thing help.
7. **IDEA-0155 "The Vine Gatherers" was doctrinally clean and ethically under-guarded. Fixed.** The
   C081 link was as well founded as anything in the group, and the risks already blocked the one
   misreading the vine invites — that the image says where things begin, when in the source it is a
   picture of a *way of teaching* and both books deny a first beginning. But the situation is a
   household whose usable growth is shrinking, whose price is set by someone else, and whose
   gathering may not be legal, and neither the idea nor EPD-0082's `caution` carried C081's
   non-agency bar or the wider no-verdict bar from finding 1. The bar is now inside the C081 link,
   and a fourth risk was added: no hint that the household's hardship, their shrinking gathering
   ground or their legal exposure is deserved or earned, since the source denies a self who acts or
   receives and denies anyone running the wheel, and the Pradeepika's linking of past merit to
   present condition must never be applied to people on screen. EPD-0082's `lesson` was not touched;
   its `caution` still covers only the first-beginning error, which remains true as far as it goes.
8. **The supporting C079 link on IDEA-0155 makes one editorial extension.** It says a woven object
   whose strands each hold their own place echoes "an order that stays fixed however the account is
   entered". The fixed clause order is in the source; that the order survives a reversed *telling*
   is an inference, and C079's `uncertainties` note that only the forward (anuloma) formula is in the
   extract and the cessation formula "has not been added". The inference is reasonable and already
   stated in EPD-0082's `the_teaching`, so it was left standing — but it is the one place in the
   group where an idea says slightly more than a digest does.
9. **No source contradiction was resolved by choosing a side, and no flagged editorial reading was
   used.** The digests record several open points that bear on this group: the rounds differ between
   the two books (C081 `cross_source_notes`, undecided); Margaya's placement of the
   dependent-origination teaching in the Vibhaṅga "needs an expert check before being quoted on
   screen" [C078-M:L11]; and C083's own editorial suggestion that the feeling-to-craving step "leans
   rather than locks" goes beyond the text and "needs doctrinal review before any on-screen use"

10. **Place names, in the ideas and now in the episode loglines.** No idea in the group carried one.
    Three episode loglines did, in exactly the class GRP-04's pass removed from its own ("the dry

    "Through a long wet stretch that does not let up", with "the families of a working community"

## 3. The merge

**IDEA-0158 *The Rope Crossing* → IDEA-0159 *The Old Roof*.** One merge in the group, and it is a
real one rather than a pointer: comparing IDEA-0159 at version 1 with version 2 shows exactly what
moved.

- **C080 changed role, from supporting to primary, and changed grounding.** At version 1 the link
  read only "nothing travels from the old roof to the new one, yet the new roof exists only because
  of the old" — a restatement of the film, not of a source. It now carries the digest's own argument:
  nothing comes over from what went before and yet nothing appears without a cause, because if the
  two were simply the same curd could not come from milk, and if simply different curd would have
  nothing to do with milk [C080-P:L955–L957; C080-P:L961–L965]. IDEA-0159 is now the only idea in the
  group with two primary links, which is the correct weighting for the only film carrying C080.
- **The crossing's simile came across whole, into `concept_merge.why_together`.** IDEA-0159 now names
  the source's full set of images for continuity without transfer — milk and curd, a lamp lit from
  another lamp, and the man crossing a stream on a rope tied to a tree on the near bank who lands by
  his own weight with nothing of that bank coming with him — which was the entire doctrinal content
  of IDEA-0158.
- **The strongest of those images became situation, not decoration.** The digest calls a skill
  learned as a child the clearest case: the child is gone, the skill lives in the adult's hands and
  in no one else's. IDEA-0159's `who_is_involved` and `what_is_at_stake` were rewritten around it —
  older hands effectively teaching while working, younger ones "who will hold the skill after the
  people who taught them are gone", and the household's live decision about whether to renew again
  at all. The film is now a roof *and* a craft continuing in the same way, which is what promoted
  C080 to primary. EPD-0084's `how_the_film_shows_it` already says "old and young together".
- **What was deliberately not folded in:** the crossing's own livelihood stakes — a job that ends the
  day a bridge is built, a community with no alternative route. They belong to a different situation
  and would have been bolted on. IDEA-0158's `selection.reason` gives the reason it lost the slot
  rather than the roof: the crossing depended on a simile being acted out in front of the camera, and
  IDEA-0159's risks now carry the same warning in general form ("building a film on a simile so
  exact that the people in it become illustration" was IDEA-0158's own risk; IDEA-0159's equivalent
  is the bar on loading the family's ordinary words about their house with a doctrinal meaning they
  did not intend).
- Bookkeeping is consistent: IDEA-0158 has `superseded_by: "IDEA-0159"`, IDEA-0159 has
  `merged_with: ["IDEA-0158"]`, and EPD-0084's `concept_ids` (C081, C080) needed no change because
  the role promotion added no new concept.

## 4. The hold and the drop

**HOLD — IDEA-0161 "What the Meal Leaves Behind."** Held, not dropped, and the reason is coverage of
a concept *strand* rather than of a concept. C082 is the group's best-carried concept by count, but
all four kept carriers use its structural relations — mutuality, decisive support, nutriment,
presence. None of them shows the object condition, which is the strand the Pradeepika actually
spends most of its object-condition section on: the memory of one's own act of giving supporting
quiet gladness, or pride, or possessiveness ("my good deed"), or doubt, or regret
[C082-P:L1723–L1727; C082-P:L1880–L1888]. That is one of the few places in the whole group where the
doctrine describes something a viewer already recognises in themselves, and the digest is blunt that
"'honour your good deeds' is not a safe slogan here". A household that has just made a large
occasion together, and remembers it four different ways, is that passage without a word of
commentary. The eight slots are filled by eight sound films, so there is nowhere to put it now; it
should be reconsidered for any group that takes up memory, merit-making or what a family owes its
own past. The idea's consent guards are already the strictest in the group (nobody interviewed
toward a revelation, nothing shown that the family has not agreed to afterwards). Its one soft spot
was the reach in its C075 nutriment link, which has since been rewritten down to what the digest
supports (§1, finding 11).

**DROP — IDEA-0154 "Second Sight."** Two independent reasons, either of which would be enough.
First, **duplication**: IDEA-0124 in GRP-07 films the same visiting cataract camp, is already used by
EPD-0062, and links it to the matter concepts that actually describe the eye — its sensitivity as one
conditional item among twenty-eight, the grip that makes it "mine", and colour as all the eye ever
receives. GRP-09 would have been filming a second crew at a second camp to illustrate a thinner
point. Second, **exposure**: the film's doctrinal frame here would have been C075's faculties, and
C075's `does_not_transfer` forbids carrying the tree simile's account of past merit into any film as
an explanation of poverty, class, caste or disability [C075-P:L41–L59]. IDEA-0154's own risks admit
this ("framing blindness or its relief through doctrine… risks implying a cause in the patient").
GRP-07 dropped IDEA-0117 on exactly this ground; the same reasoning applies with more force in a
group whose entire subject is what conditions what. There is a third, smaller point recorded in the
idea's `selection.reason`: its position reasoning was backwards, since restoring sight is a condition
*supplied*, not a condition ceasing, so it never fitted the group's closing movement either.
**Nothing was folded forward.** C075 remains covered by IDEA-0153 and IDEA-0162; C083 by IDEA-0156,
IDEA-0160 and IDEA-0163. Neither concept lost a carry.

## 5. Still weak, and lessons needing an update
Five items from the first pass have since been fixed and moved to §1: the kamma-as-fate guard missing
from IDEA-0155 and IDEA-0163 (findings 1 and 7), IDEA-0161's overreaching nutriment link, IDEA-0155's
false Object 9 claim, EPD-0084's "Every year", and the regional identifiers in three episode loglines
(findings 10 and 11). What follows is what genuinely remains.

1. **C080 is carried by one kept idea, and it is the concept the group's `depth` leans on hardest.**
   The group record says dependent origination is "revealed as the chain running through the whole
   series", and C080 is the concept that gives each of the twelve links its meaning. On the slate it
   lives entirely in a roof renewal, as continuity-without-transfer. The merge of IDEA-0158 made that
   single carry much stronger (§3) but could not make it two, and there is no honest way to invent a
   second: the rest of C080's content — the meanings of formations, becoming, clinging, the
   death-moment material, the cosmology — is either abstract or, by the digest's own statement,
   unobservable and not to be presented as observed. This is the group's real doctrinal thinness and
   it should be stated to Chathura rather than papered over.
2. **C075 is on the slate only by two of its seven lists.** The group's gloss for C075 names roots,
   absorption factors, path factors, faculties, powers, dominants and nutriments. The kept ideas use
   the nutriment strand (IDEA-0153) and the faculty strand (IDEA-0162), and nothing else. The
   digest's bars that go with the unused strands — that wrong effort and wrong concentration are
   path factors heading toward a bad destination, that shamelessness is named a "power", that
   "focus and hard work are good" must not become productivity messaging — are therefore untested by
   any film in the group. That is not a fault in the ideas; it is a note for whoever writes the
   briefs, since a director filling out either film could drift into that territory without any
   guard in the record.
3. **IDEA-0155's C079 link still makes one editorial extension** (§1, finding 8): that the formula's
   fixed order survives a reversed *telling*. The fixed clause order is in the source; the survival
   is an inference, and C079's `uncertainties` note that only the forward formula is in the extract.
   It was left standing because it is reasonable and because EPD-0082's `the_teaching` already says
   it, but it is the one place in the group where an idea says slightly more than a digest does.
   Flagging it rather than fixing it is deliberate: the repair is a doctrinal judgement, not a
   wording slip.
4. **IDEA-0155's `position_hint` still reads "opening candidate" while the slate places it fourth.**
   The false Object 9 claim is gone, but the position word is a leftover from before the order was
   fixed. It is a hint, not a decision, and no check reads it, so it was left as the previous pass's
   creative judgement rather than overwritten.
5. **Two connection references are one-way.** IDEA-0153 declares "leads into IDEA-0161" and IDEA-0159
   declares "follows IDEA-0160", but neither target names the source back. The repo check only
   verifies that connection targets exist, so this passes; it is untidy rather than wrong.
6. **Two episode lessons are narrower than their episode's concept set. Neither is inaccurate, so
   per the brief neither was edited.** EPD-0079's `in_simple_terms` covers the nutriment distinction
   and "each helps in its own way" but not C078's brief-versus-detailed pair, which IDEA-0153's
   `what_the_viewer_could_understand` now states as a second sentence; the pair is present in that
   lesson's `the_teaching`, so this is a one-sentence addition at most. EPD-0082's `in_simple_terms`
   covers entering the chain anywhere but not the second half of the idea — that within any telling
   the order still holds — which again is already in its `the_teaching`. **No lesson in the group is
   made inaccurate by the rewrite or by the corrective pass, and none requires a correction.**
7. **Repo-wide check:** `npm run check` reports 5 failures after the corrective pass and **no GRP-09
   id appears in any of them**. Three are the idea-shape checks and one is "Episodes use only kept or

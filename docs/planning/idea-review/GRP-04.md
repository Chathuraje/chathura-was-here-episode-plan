# GRP-04 "Lost" — idea finalisation

Reviewed 2026-09-19. 11 ideas (IDEA-0053 to IDEA-0063), 8 episodes (EPD-0028 to EPD-0035), 5 concepts (C008, C033, C049, C074, C086).

## 1. Philosophy findings and what changed

Every concept link was tested against its digest. All 25 links were kept except one (see C049 below); most `why` texts were rewritten so that the claim they make is the claim the digest actually supports, and so that the digest's `does_not_transfer` line that bears on that film is stated inside the link rather than left to the risks list.

**C049 — the sensitive half of the concept.** C049's digest forbids, in two places, any film use of the source's examples of "rootless" persons (people blind or deaf from birth, people of weak intellect, people living by begging), and flags the doctrinal claim itself as needing expert checking. Nothing of that kind was in the ideas and nothing of it was introduced. IDEA-0060 now states explicitly in its primary link that the film uses only the learner / beyond-learning strand and must never touch the classification of persons by the roots present at birth.

- **Link dropped:** IDEA-0062's supporting C049 link ("people start from different places; some fog is not a personal failing" [C049-P:L27–L33]). The sentence is the digest's own human reading, but the idea applies it to identifiable newcomers on camera, which is precisely the mapping the digest's `does_not_transfer` rules out. Dropped; the risk it was reaching for is now a named risk instead. IDEA-0062 is a merge, so no episode `concept_ids` change.
- **Group record flag (not edited):** GRP-04's own `concepts[]` gloss for C049 reads "Types of persons distinguished by the roots present at birth: why some find clarity harder than others." That is the strand the digest says must not be used. The group's `lesson` is safe — it uses only the learner strand — but the gloss should be narrowed by whoever owns the group record.

**C008 — the doubt boundary.** The digest restricts harmful doubt to eight religious matters, separates it from honest not-knowing [C008-P:L36], and flags as an open uncertainty how the general definition [C008-P:L34] relates to the restricted one. Four ideas (0053, 0054, 0056, 0063) rest on the general definition. All four `why` texts now say so, cite the separation, and state that the film asserts nothing about which kind of doubt it is showing. The digest's "the neutral feeling is not equanimity" is now a named risk on IDEA-0054 and IDEA-0055; "restlessness is not a productivity problem" is written into IDEA-0055's primary link.

**C033 — non-delusion is not knowledge.** IDEA-0058 turns on a fisher who can read a maze nobody else can. The digest is explicit that non-delusion is the wisdom factor within a moment of mind, not education or information. Added as a risk and reflected in the C008 link's wording. The "not root-cause analysis" and "roots classify moments of mind, not people" limits are now inside the C033 link.

**C074 — accurate, and now cited consistently.** The fetter simile in IDEA-0057 was correct; its loose "(P L544, L621–L623; M L90)" is now written as `[C074-P:L544; C074-P:L621–L623; C074-M:L90]`, the same citations the EPD-0032 lesson already validates. IDEA-0059's C074 link gained the digest's ban on using the rites-and-rituals warnings to judge or mock real religious practice — load-bearing for a film inside a family's almsgiving. IDEA-0057 gained the "not 'just let go of your attachments'" limit.

**C086 — everyone has all six.** IDEA-0059 and IDEA-0062 both sit on a digest that warns against typology and against using the sources' posture/gait/food signs to profile people on camera. Both links now carry that, and "mapping family members onto the six temperaments" is a named risk on IDEA-0059.

No source contradiction was resolved by choosing a side; no doctrine was invented; no citation was used that does not appear in the digest's own evidence.

**Other changes:** all eleven ideas were rewritten into the new shape (`premise`, `what_camera_could_observe`, `possible_arc`, `location` deleted; `situation`, `concept_merge`, `what_the_viewer_could_understand`, `selection` added; version 1 → 2). Every real place name was scrubbed from idea text: the eight episode loglines were carrying regional identifiers ("the dry zone", "the north", "the south-west coast", "the east coast", "hill-country") and now carry only the kind of setting the situation needs.

## 3. Merges

- **IDEA-0056 *Mixed Signs* → IDEA-0054 *Which Way*.** What it added: the strand of people met along the way who are themselves holding a decision that costs real money or real risk (now in `situation.who_is_involved` and `what_is_at_stake`), which gives the opening film stakes it did not have; and its guard — the digest's separation of honest not-knowing from the doubt the sources warn about — which is now written into IDEA-0054's primary C008 link and into its risks as "a considered hesitation is often wisdom". No new concept link: 0056 carried C008 + C074, which 0054 already had.
- **IDEA-0062 *Where the Mind Goes* → IDEA-0060 *Still Learning*.** What it added: the guide who asks rather than assumes — the master sometimes simply asking an apprentice what comes easily, which is the sources' own description [C086-P4:L278] — and the learner's own account of where attention goes, both now in 0060's `situation.what_happens` and its C086 link; plus the risk "presenting what the master notices in an apprentice as a diagnosis". This turns a craft film into a film about being taught as the particular person you are, answering 0060's old "craft showcase with no stake" weakness. No new concept link: 0062's C008 strand was not folded in (see §5), and its C049 link was dropped.

## 4. Holds and drops

- **HOLD — IDEA-0061 *The Weeping Pot*.** Held for two reasons: it is the standing alternative closing film if the mist film's access fails (its own `drop_if` is the narrowest in the group), and it is the only place the compendium's cracked-pot image of the taints [C074-P:L79] is carried anywhere in the slate. It is a genuinely strong idea with no slot, not a weak one.
- No drops. Nothing in the group was weak enough or duplicative enough of another group's work to justify one.

## 5. Still weak, and lessons

- **No lesson needs an update.** Each kept idea's `what_the_viewer_could_understand` was written to its episode's `lesson.in_simple_terms`, and no rewrite made any lesson inaccurate. Episode `concept_ids` are unchanged everywhere, so the depth map is untouched by this group.
- **The group is heavy on C008 and C074 and thin on C049.** C049 is carried by one kept idea only, and only by its learner strand — correctly, since the digest forbids the other strand. That is a real gap the group cannot honestly close: the concept's main content is not filmable.
- **IDEA-0060 could take a C008 link** if the folded-in strand (an apprentice's attention slipping, a mistake made because of it) becomes central in development. It was deliberately not added, because the idea does not yet need it and adding it would change EPD-0034's `concept_ids` and the shared depth map.
- **IDEA-0053 remains weather-dependent** and is the group's most ethically exposed film: a resettlement history, reopened for a few weeks, filmed. Its risks now say so plainly.
- **IDEA-0054 is still the group's thinnest situation** even after the merge — a journey with low stakes for the person at its centre. It holds the opening slot because it is the most literal, not because it is the strongest.

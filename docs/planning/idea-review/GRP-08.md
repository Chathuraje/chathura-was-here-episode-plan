# GRP-08 idea review — Endings

Reviewed 2026-09-19 by claude; report and a second corrective pass 2026-09-20. Sixteen ideas (IDEA-0133
to IDEA-0148), twelve episodes (EPD-0067 to EPD-0078), fourteen concepts (C050–C062, C092). All sixteen
ideas were rewritten into the situation/concept-merge shape; `premise`, `what_camera_could_observe`,
`possible_arc` and `location` were deleted from every one, and every real place name and regional
identifier was scrubbed from idea text and loglines. Twelve ideas are kept, four are merged, and no idea
is held or dropped.

The second pass changed six idea files (IDEA-0136, IDEA-0141, IDEA-0142, IDEA-0146, IDEA-0147 and, by the
merge, IDEA-0141's notes) and four episode files, touching only `title`, `logline` and `concept_ids`
(EPD-0071, EPD-0074, EPD-0075, EPD-0076). No `lesson`, `location`, `object`, `thread_in` or `thread_out`
was edited, no digest or group record was touched, and nothing outside GRP-08 was opened.

This is the group about death, dying, rebirth and the last moments of a life, so the digests' own
prohibitions do more work here than in any group so far. Almost every change below is a guard being moved
from an implicit assumption into the text of a link.

## 1. Philosophy findings and what changed

Every concept link was tested against its digest (`summary_en`, `human_interpretation_en`, `key_terms`,
`does_not_transfer`, `uncertainties`). Of the twenty-four original links, none was dropped, one was
re-assigned to the right concept and one was added; the group now carries twenty-five. What changed
everywhere else is what the links claim. Findings in order of seriousness:

1. **IDEA-0147 "The Tools", the group's closing film — a fishing household sat at the centre of a group
   whose digests carry three explicit bars about fishing communities, and nothing in the idea guarded
   it. Fixed.** C051 records that the Pradeepika links hunters, fishermen and those who kill cattle, goats
   and chickens to a particular hell [C051-P:L63] and states that this "must never be used on screen to
   judge real fishing, farming or butchering communities". C056 names hunting and fishing as the source's
   examples of *habitual* occupations [C056-P:L109] and repeats the bar in the same words. C058 adds that
   the source's claim that killing is always done with aversion, "even while smiling" [C058-P:L70], is a
   doctrinal claim about mind-moments and never a psychological verdict on fishers, farmers or butchers.
   All three are now written into IDEA-0147's `what_must_be_real`, with two matching named risks. They
   went there rather than into a link because IDEA-0147 links neither C051, C056 nor C058 — the exposure
   is not in this film's own doctrine but in the group around it, so the guard has to govern placement and
   edit, and the idea now says in as many words that the film may not be cut or placed against the group's
   films on the planes, the order of ripening or the courses of harm in any way that would let a viewer
   read the household through those passages. The bar that *does* belong to one of its own links went into
   it: the C057 digest's shipwreck prohibition — a thousand drowned people each meeting death through
   their own past action must never become a claim that anyone who drowned deserved it — is now in the
   C057 `why`, together with the observation that this is a film about a household whose work is done on
   water, and a matching risk covers the manner of the death. **The situation can be told without breaking
   any bar**, because nothing in the film requires the trade to be judged, explained or connected to a
   destination; no replacement is recommended and EPD-0078 was not rewired. What remains is an edit-stage
   constraint, which is a report item rather than an idea one (§5.1).
2. **IDEA-0135 "The Old Cow at the Gate", C056 — the link stated the opposite of the source, and the
   error was the one the whole group could have inherited.** The old `why` said the cattle-pen image shows
   "why what is nearest a life's end can outweigh what is strongest in it". The sources say the reverse:
   weighty action takes priority over everything, and what is done or recalled near death decides only
   where there is no weighty action [C056-P:L117]. The digest separately flags the simile's own line as
   syntactically compressed with two possible readings — the usual one, that near-death kamma ripens
   before habitual kamma, and a second in which the *weak* reserve kamma ripens first — and asks for an
   expert check [C056-P:L119]. The correction was written into IDEA-0135 and then travelled with the merge
   into IDEA-0136, whose C056 link now states the ordering only as the sources plainly give it and says in
   as many words that it is never "the last thought decides". That bar is stated three times over in the
   digests of this group (C053, C061 and C062 each carry it) and it is now carried by the one kept idea
   that could have drifted into it.
3. **IDEA-0136 "Called to the House" — the idea rested on a concept it did not link. C061 added.** Its
   concept merge stated the sources' limit on the whole practice it follows: the object at death is
   presented by the action ready to ripen and is not placed there by relatives, teachers or gods
   [C061-P:L29]. That is C061's own subject, and carrying a doctrine's limit while refusing its concept is
   not a defensible state for the one film in the slate that sits inside the dying process. The brief's
   Task 1 allows adding a link the situation genuinely carries, so C061 was added as `supporting` rather
   than the claim being reworded away. Its `why` holds the link in exactly the unresolved form the digest
   requires: the Pradeepika's "not brought by parents, relatives, teachers or gods" set beside the same
   source's list of things present at a deathbed — a Buddha image, flowers, preaching, chanting, incense,
   received through the senses as signs of the ripening deed [C061-P:L49-L55] — which the digest records
   as an open question needing an expert check before any film treats deathbed practices. The link is what
   stops the other two being read as a technique, since the digest also excludes "think positively at the
   end": the dying mind bends toward its object through unabandoned ignorance and craving [C061-M:L9], not
   through anyone's arrangement. **EPD-0075's `concept_ids` were updated to the union, C062, C056, C061**
   (Task 4); nothing else in the episode was touched. Consequences in §5.2.
4. **IDEA-0137 "The Night Ward" — the wrong concept, and the only link in the group with no citation at
   all.** Its supporting link was C061, and its `why` described "the dying mind still meeting the world
   through the senses". That is the near-death process, which is C062. The link was re-assigned and given
   the citation it never had [C062-M:L25], together with the C062 digest's own limit: sense contact at the
   bedside can become the door of the last mental process, but the texts do not say such care secures
   anything.
5. **IDEA-0145 "What Comes at Dusk", C053 — an uncertainty was being used as a selling point.** The old
   `why` called the source's identification of one class of downcast spirit with the beings who accept
   exorcists' offerings "one of the few places where the text points directly at a living Sri Lankan
   practice". The digest's own note on that line [C053-M:L15] is that it *blends a canonical category with
   Sri Lankan folk practice and needs expert comment before use*. The link is kept — it is the reason the
   film exists — but rewritten so that the hesitation is part of what the film holds rather than something
   it hides, with the same point in `concept_merge.what_it_reveals` and in the idea's `unknowns` as a check
   that must precede any approach to a family. The C053 digest's absolute bar on using the source's list of
   congenital conditions as results of weak good action is now in the link, which matters here because the
   film's subject is a seriously ill person.
6. **IDEA-0133 and IDEA-0147, C061 — both ideas were shaped exactly like the reading the digest
   forbids.** The C061 digest rules out "we live on through our legacy and in people's memories". One idea
   is a family deciding what to do with a dead man's belongings; the other is a dead man's tools in his
   successor's hands. Neither claimed the forbidden reading, but neither refused it either. Both links now
   name it and refuse it: IDEA-0133 shows the keeping as the living taking care of themselves and never
   suggests the person is present in what he left, and IDEA-0147 offers inherited work explicitly as an
   *editorial* analogy for a causal stream, with the digest's exclusion of an in-between state added so the
   film makes no claim about where he is. IDEA-0147's `what_the_viewer_could_understand` now ends "and only
   a picture".
7. **IDEA-0136, C056 — promoted from supporting to primary, and heavily guarded.** The idea's whole reason
   for existing is that the sources give a reason for sitting with the dying, so C056 could not stay an
   aside. Three guards went in with the promotion: family rituals at the deathbed must not be presented as
   guaranteeing a destination; no observer can read a calm or a difficult death as a verdict on the person
   who died (C056 and C062 both say so); and the Margaya's own point that people who have done much good
   can still be troubled by memories at the end [C056-M:L22]. With C061 now added, this is the only idea in
   the group with three links, which is right for the one film that sits at the join between how a life
   ends and what follows it.
8. **IDEA-0146 "The Night Shift", C060 — the sources' refusal of fatalism was the finding, not a risk to
   manage.** The old `why` had the right content but stopped at "skilled help can avert it". The digest
   goes much further: the Margaya calls the belief that everything comes from kamma a *wrong view* and says
   the cause of any given untimely death cannot be known [C060-M:L23-L35]. That is now the decisive part of
   the link, and it is what makes this the clearest place in the group where the texts' own position can be
   shown rather than asserted. The digest's requirement around death by a person's own act — safe reporting
   practice, no method described, never framed through kamma — is now written into the link, and the
   digest's note that wind, bile and phlegm are traditional medicine rather than diagnosis is respected by
   the idea not using them at all.
9. **IDEA-0143 "When the Rain Stops", C052 — the passage is right, and it has two prohibitions attached.**
   The source's account of a world-system's destruction does begin with rain, sowing, a growing crop and
   then rain that stops [C052-P:L156-L160]. The digest forbids two uses of it: reading it as an ecological
   prophecy, and blaming a real drought on anyone's moral failure, since the source ties the cycle to
   greed, hatred and delusion in general and never to a named community. Both are now in the link, in
   `what_must_be_real` and in the risks, and the link states that the film borrows only the shape of the
   account and makes no causal claim about this drought or these farmers.
10. **IDEA-0139 "The Boundary Walk" and IDEA-0138 "Lost and Found", C058 — the thought-crime edge.** The
    old link defined covetousness [C058-P:L224-L229] and stopped there, which leaves a film about a
    bereaved family's wanting one step from accusing people of their thoughts. The digest is explicit that
    this is not a thought-crime teaching: a flash of wanting gives only minor results and is distinguished
    from a settled wish [C058-P:L239]. That distinction is now the centre of the link, of the concept merge
    and of the episode-matching `what_the_viewer_could_understand`, and the digest's bar on moralising
    about honest and dishonest people came across with the IDEA-0138 merge.
11. **IDEA-0142 "The Opposing Good", C055 and C058 — two doctrinal claims that must not be attached to a
    real person.** C055's digest rules out both "karma points" netting out and "they deserved it": the
    source describes stronger forces overriding weaker ones, not a sum, and its examples of illness and
    poverty describe how forces interact rather than licensing judgement. C058's digest adds that
    statements that an act "certainly" leads somewhere are the source's doctrinal claims and are never
    predictions to attach to any real person, "especially in a group about grief", and that the source's
    framing of sexual misconduct and of wrong view is historical commentary. Both are in the links, and the
    second pass added a further named risk covering the situation folded in from IDEA-0141: a supplier's
    failed season or the kitchen running short must never be read as a result of anyone's past actions, or
    as the four functions illustrating themselves on real people.
12. **IDEA-0140 "The Ledger", C057 — the accounting image needed three bars, not one.** The seed-grain
    image is the digest's own [C057-P:L87] and the link was sound, but a credit book invites exactly the
    readings the digest rules out. The link now states that lapsing is expiry and not forgiveness or a
    clean slate, that there is no "instant karma", and that good and bad do not net out, and the risks add
    that no customer's poverty is framed as anything but circumstance.
13. **IDEA-0144 "As Long As It Takes", C054 and C050 — a precise teaching one step from a self-help
    promise.** The digest says "meditate and you'll go to heaven" turns a teaching about an attainment
    unlost at death into a promise; the link now says the film shows people practising and never suggests
    any of them is going anywhere. The source's traditional remark about women who are non-returners
    beginning in the lowest Brahma plane is named and not used. The digest's record that the source says
    the higher absorptions follow "the same method" without spelling out the grades [C054-P:L25-L27] is
    carried as an unresolved gap rather than filled in. On C050, the digest's first correction — that
    "higher beings feel more" reverses the source, since the freest person in its table has the fewest
    kinds of mind available [C050-P:L80] — is now the substance of the link, and the bar on the
    sense-faculty count ever becoming a ranking of human worth is stated absolutely.
14. **IDEA-0134 "The Seventh Day", C059 and C051 — the best-supported pair in the group, now bounded.**
    The digest is explicit that sharing merit is not sending things to the dead: it works through the
    relative's own gladness at a deed that has been properly described [C059-P:L239-L245]. That is the
    link, and it is unusually exact. What was missing was the C051 boundary: the source limits this
    dependence to one subclass of peta [C051-M:L21], and the digest warns directly against generalising it
    into "the dead are watching over us" or "the dead need our offerings". The link now says the film
    asserts nothing about where this particular person now is — a claim the digest says must never be
    attached to any real person, and least of all in a group about grief.
15. **IDEA-0148 "Sitting With It", C092 — the practice's own failure modes are now half the link.** The
    source directs the practitioner to charnel grounds and forests and, in the same place, warns that
    unwise recollection brings grief, glee at an enemy's death, indifference or fear [C092-P4:L80-L84].
    Both halves are in the link, which is what keeps the film from reading as either morbidity or "you only
    live once", the two readings the digest names. The digest's bar on using the timely and untimely
    categories to explain or judge a real person's death is in the link, and its note that the source's
    remarks about the body and its humours are not facts is observed.
16. **Scrub completed, and three episode loglines resynced.** IDEA-0146's logline still carried location
    reasoning and a trace of shot language — "On steep, misted hill roads, an ambulance crew *races*
    between a district hospital and villages…", which matched the episode's own regional suggestion and so
    decided something the episode is supposed to decide. It now reads "An emergency crew works through the
    night between a hospital and the households that call it, where the same call can be twenty minutes
    away or two hours, and every one is a small argument with time." On the episode side, EPD-0071 carried
    "In an eastern dry-zone village…", the same class of regional identifier GRP-04's pass removed from its
    episode loglines; it now matches IDEA-0143's scrubbed line, which keeps the one setting fact the
    situation needs, "land that depends on a seasonal tank". EPD-0076 lost "in the hills" and EPD-0074 was
    brought into line with IDEA-0146's new logline, so all twelve episode loglines and their ideas now
    agree. Only `logline` was touched in each.
17. **IDEA-0136 no longer carries two subjects.** The IDEA-0137 merge had put hospital night staff into
    `who_is_involved` alongside the monk, leaving the idea with a double subject its logline, position and
    episode could not hold. The merged substance is kept but restated: the secular register is now named as
    *this film's alternative situation for the same three concepts, not a second strand inside it*, and the
    idea says the film follows one or the other. Nothing the merge contributed was thrown away.
18. **No source contradiction was resolved, and no doctrine was invented.** Where the digests record a
    disagreement or an open question, the ideas leave it open and say so: the two possible readings of the
    old-cow line [C056-P:L119]; how "the object at death is not presented by others" fits the deathbed
    practices the same source lists, now held open inside IDEA-0136's C061 link and its `unknowns`; the
    unspelt grades for the higher absorptions [C054-P:L25-L27]; the Pradeepika's ambiguity about which
    asuras belong where [C051-P:L95-L101], which IDEA-0145's supporting link names and declines to
    adjudicate; the Margaya's internal tension between cutting-off death through kamma and untimely death
    having many non-kamma causes; and the C053 identification the whole of IDEA-0145 rests on. Every
    citation used in a GRP-08 idea, including the four added in this pass, was checked against its digest's
    own evidence and resolves.

## 3. Merges

Four ideas were merged, all four into ideas that already held their concept. None moved a concept from one
carry to two; each was folded in for depth, not for coverage.

- **IDEA-0135 "The Old Cow at the Gate" → IDEA-0136 "Called to the House."** What it added: the source's
  own image for the ordering of results, an old and weak cow that leaves the pen first because it is
  nearest the gate [C056-P:L119], *and* the correction and the expert-check flag that go with it. This is
  the merge that did the most work in the group. It turned C056 in IDEA-0136 from a supporting aside about
  a good friend at the bedside into a primary link that states the whole ordering — weighty first, then
  what is near the end, then what is habitual — and explicitly refuses "the last thought decides". An
  image-led film with no human situation was folded into the one film in the group where the ordering has a
  real bedside to sit at.
- **IDEA-0137 "The Night Ward" → IDEA-0136.** What it added: the same role performed with no religious
  standing at all — night staff doing the small physical things, a sip of water, a hand held, for patients
  whose families cannot be there — and the observation that the role the texts describe as a good friend at
  the deathbed is in practice largely filled by paid strangers. It also brought the corrected C062 link
  (§1.4). After the second pass this material is held as the kept idea's named *alternative situation* for
  the same three concepts rather than as a second strand inside one film (§1.17).
- **IDEA-0138 "Lost and Found" → IDEA-0139 "The Boundary Walk."** What it added: the source's definition of
  taking what is not given, by the plain fact of knowing something belongs to another and deciding to take
  it anyway [C058-P:L78-L86], which IDEA-0139 did not carry and which is the other half of the same course
  of action; and the counter-case that does the ethical work — strangers handing back a wallet or an
  unclaimed bag, where nothing but the decision is at stake — now in `concept_merge.what_it_reveals`. That
  counter-case is what protects the kept idea from casting one relative as the greedy one, which is its
  largest risk. C058 in IDEA-0139 went from one definition to two, with the passing-wish/settled-wish line
  between them.
- **IDEA-0141 "What the Fire Leaves" → IDEA-0142 "The Opposing Good."** What it added, after the second
  pass: both the doctrine and the situation. On doctrine, the source's own picture of the four functions as
  a farming season — seed sown, crop tended, a drought that weakens it, a fire that ends it [C055-P:L127];
  before the merge IDEA-0142 carried only the practical conclusion that someone who has done wrong should
  take up the opposing good [C055-M:L34]. On situation, which the first pass left behind and this pass
  folded in properly: the kitchen is now shown running on what arrives — rice and vegetables given by
  growers, traders and households whose own year decides what they can spare, so a bad season reaches it
  twice, as less coming in at the back and more people queuing at the front; the suppliers and the day
  labourers among those who eat there are now in `who_is_involved`; supply is now part of
  `what_is_at_stake`, since a kitchen is held open by several forces at once and can be closed by any one
  of them; and `how_it_unfolds` now gives the kitchen's year IDEA-0141's own shape, a fixed calendar with
  an unfixed outcome in which what is started has to be kept up, can be worn down by an ordinary run of bad
  weeks, and can be cut off outright by one. That is what the merge was for: the four functions can now be
  *watched* in the film's own frame instead of only stated over a volunteer's routine. The limit the merge
  cannot remove is stated in the idea: the film can watch the routine and can never see whether it has
  weakened anything. `selection.reason` on both files and the connection notes were updated to match.

## 4. Holds and drops

**There were none, and that is defensible here, but it is a judgement rather than an absence of a
choice.**

- **No drops.** Nothing in the group was weak enough or duplicative enough to justify one. In particular,
  nothing in GRP-08 is in the position GRP-07's IDEA-0117 was in — an idea whose primary link the digest
  contradicted *and* whose situation put a flagged prohibition under permanent strain. The two ideas that
  came closest to being unsound, IDEA-0135 (a link that stated the opposite of the source) and IDEA-0137 (a
  link attached to the wrong concept), were both repairable, and both repairs were worth keeping, so both
  became merges instead.
- **No holds.** A hold is for a strong idea with no slot and something to be held *for*. Each of the four
  surplus ideas here is about a concept the twelve-episode slate already carries — C056 twice over, C058
  and C055 — so there is no later group waiting for them: the concepts are all placed in GRP-08 and this is
  where they are used. Holding them would have parked material the kept ideas needed now. The honest
  counter-argument stands: IDEA-0137 and IDEA-0141 are strong enough as films that "hold" would have been
  reasonable, and if either kept idea proves too crowded in development, restoring one of them is a better
  repair than thinning the kept idea.
- **Twelve keeps for twelve episodes.** Every episode's idea was tested and none was found unsound, so no
  replacement is recommended and no episode was rewired.

## 5. Still weak, and lessons needing an update

   ideas make no edit decisions.** IDEA-0147 now says the film may not be placed or cut against the group's
   films on the planes, the order of ripening or the courses of harm in a way that lets a viewer read the
   household through those passages (§1.1). That sentence is doing work the idea stage cannot enforce: it
   has to reach the episode brief and the edit, and it constrains *other* films' narration as much as this
   one's. It should be carried forward explicitly whenever EPD-0078 is briefed, and whoever writes the
   group's narration needs to know that the three passages exist and where they sit.
2. **Adding C061 to EPD-0075 has two consequences that are now real.** First,
   `development/analysis/abhidhamma-depth-map.json` is stale on GRP-08's account: C061's
   `linked_episode_ids` gain EPD-0075 and its episode count moves from two to three, which also touches
   `summary.concept_status_counts`. I deliberately did not edit that file — it is a single global artefact,
   other groups are being finalised in parallel and would collide on the same summary block, and its check
   is a known repo-wide failure being fixed separately. It should be regenerated once all ten groups are
   done. Second, EPD-0075's `lesson` now covers C062 and C056 but not C061. The lesson is not inaccurate,
   so per the brief it was not edited; it would take a one-sentence addition saying that the texts place the
   object of the dying mind beyond anyone else's arranging.
3. **EPD-0069's lesson does not mention the identification IDEA-0145's primary link is built on** — the
   source's connection of one class of downcast spirit with the beings who accept exorcists' offerings.
   Left alone deliberately: the brief forbids editing lessons, and the digest asks for expert comment before
   that line is used at all. But it means the episode's stated lesson and its idea's primary link are not
   the same claim, and that should be a deliberate decision of Chathura's rather than an oversight.
4. **The group's third episode rests on a line the digest says needs expert comment before use.** IDEA-0145
   is honest about this in its link, its concept merge and its `unknowns`, which is the right handling — but

   tension) both bear on why its subject does what he does; the C061 link is explicitly held open until that

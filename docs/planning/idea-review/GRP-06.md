# GRP-06 "Kindness" — idea review

Reviewed 2026-09-19 against `development/groups/GRP-06.json`, the ten episodes EPD-0048 to EPD-0057, the
fourteen ideas IDEA-0093 to IDEA-0106, and the digests for C011, C012, C019, C023, C024, C027, C030, C091
and C095. All fourteen ideas were rewritten into the situation/concept-merge shape; `premise`,
`what_camera_could_observe`, `possible_arc` and `location` were deleted from every one of them.

## 1. Philosophy findings and what changed

**Unsupported or risky concept links, corrected**

- **IDEA-0098 (Four Shapes of the Same Care), C024.** The old `why` presented compassion and sympathetic
  joy as simply "shifting with who is in view" in a film about a mother and her own children. The C024
  digest says the opposite about exactly this case: the Pradeepika classes tenderness toward one's own
  family, and pride in one's own children's success, as attachment rather than as the boundless factors
  [C024-P:L81; C024-P:L91], and the digest's `does_not_transfer` states plainly that family love must not
  be presented as the model of this compassion. The link could not simply be dropped: EPD-0054's lesson
  cites C024-M:L15 and C024-M:L17, so removing C024 from the episode's `concept_ids` would break the
  lesson's own citations. **The link is kept and the `why` rewritten to hold the tension open**: C095 uses
  a mother's love for her own sons as its teaching image for the four abidings, while C024 classes love
  confined to one's own family as attachment. The idea now names this as a disagreement between the
  sources, refuses to resolve it, and adds a risk forbidding the film from telling a real family that
  their love is "really" attachment. This is the flagged claim the group must not make, and the idea now
  guards against it explicitly rather than carrying it.
- **IDEA-0103 (The Society Book), C012.** The old `why` read the society's mutual aid as "a return,
  visible only much later, on years of steady fair dealing" — which is the "you get what you give" reading
  the C012 digest rules out. Rewritten to what the digest supports: resultant minds are moments of
  experience arising from a person's own earlier action with no judge involved [C012-P:L34], and everyone,
  including the most realised, lives through the whole mixed range of them [C012-P:L52] — so every member
  is at once a giver and a receiver. An explicit prohibition was added: nothing may imply that the
  member's bad year, or anyone's poverty, is the fruit of past action (C012-P:L48, which the digest marks
  as unusable on camera).
- **IDEA-0105 (The Tea Round), C012.** The old `why` cited C012-M:L22, which is about the *functional*
  minds of arahants — acts that leave no result because the person has finished the path. That does not
  describe a volunteer, so the citation did not support the claim. Rewritten to the same honest form as
  IDEA-0103, with the same prohibition attached. Dropping C012 outright would have been cleaner, but see
  §5: episode `concept_ids` could not be changed here without editing a shared file.
- **IDEA-0095 (Setting the Load Down), C023.** The idea uses literal carried weight as the image of
  lightness. The digest gives two corrections the link was missing, and both are now carried in the `why`:
  in the six pairs "body" (kāya) means the group of accompanying mental factors, not the physical body
  [C023-P:L119], and the heaviness lightness opposes is the dullness of sloth and torpor, explicitly *not*
  the weight of any substance. A risk was added against letting a real body's tiredness be treated as a
  diagram of a mental factor, and another noting the digest's point that turning toward good still takes
  effort against a pull the other way.
- **IDEA-0096 (The Softening), C023.** Accurate, but now carries the digest's caution that mudutā is
  softness against pride and wrong view, not flexibility about one's values.
- **IDEA-0100 (The Face Taking Shape), C091.** Supported — the Margaya's image method is exactly
  head-to-feet attention to an image [C091-M:L7]. Sharpened so that the film observes the *making* of the
  object the practice uses and never attributes the practice to the maker; the digest's warnings against
  "think of your hero" and "visualisation for wellbeing" are now in the `why`. The C023 link was broadened
  from workability/proficiency alone to include faith as clarifying confidence [C023-P:L41], which is what
  EPD-0053's lesson actually cites.
- **IDEA-0106 (Between Two Rounds), C030.** The claim is sound but the digest flags part of it. The `why`
  now separates the two: compassion and gladness never sharing a moment is supported in both sources
  [C030-P:L39; C027-M:L7], while the wider rule that only one of the five occasional beautiful factors
  arises at a time comes from one source and is flagged for expert check.
- **IDEA-0099 (A Good Catch Day).** Both links hold. C024's impartiality demand applies cleanly here,
  between competitors rather than kin, and the near-enemy of muditā (worldly giddy excitement) is now
  named so the film does not mistake a celebration for a fault.
- **IDEA-0102 (Fair Measure) and IDEA-0093 (The Free Table).** Links supported as they stood; `why` text
  tightened to the digests' own wording, including C011's correction that the coin-and-note comparison is
  within one giver and never between a large giver and a small one, and C019's correction that the
  fifty-two are a roster, never all present at once.

**Flagged claims deliberately avoided**

- *Shame tied to lineage* (C023-P:L73): hiri/ottappa are not used by any idea in this group, and none was
  introduced.
- *Family pity called attachment* (C024-P:L81, C024-P:L91): named as a source disagreement in IDEA-0098,
  never asserted about a participant.
- *Poverty or disability linked to past action* (C012-P:L48, C024-P:L103, C095's beggar-as-object):
  explicitly prohibited in IDEA-0103, IDEA-0105 and IDEA-0106. IDEA-0105 also carries the C095 digest's
  line that pointing a camera at sick or poor people as objects of pity is exactly what the series rules
  out.

**Place names scrubbed.** Every `location` block is gone. "Dry zone", the road corridor, the hill-country
and estate regions, the named harbours, the coast, and the district-level suggestions were all removed.
Only kinds of setting that the situation cannot be understood without remain (a market, a hospital, an
elders' home, a weighing point), and never as proposals.

## 3. Merges

- **IDEA-0094 (Tea From Both Hands) → IDEA-0093.** Same two concepts, same human act. Folded in: the
  repeat-recipient strand — a giver and a receiver who meet daily, and day-to-day variation in what is
  given, which is precisely the Margaya's coin-and-note comparison [C011-M:L26]. IDEA-0093's
  `what_happens`, `who_is_involved` and C011 `why` all now carry it; the idea's `merged_with` lists it.
- **IDEA-0101 (Handed Back) → IDEA-0102.** Same concept (C024) and the same moral structure, and far less
  filmable, since the premise collapses if reconstructed. Folded in: the three depths of restraint —
  stopping in the moment with no prior vow [C024-P:L61], keeping an undertaking at cost, and the change
  deep enough that the wrong no longer arises — plus the one-off form of the refusal (unpaid hours spent
  returning property nobody knew was found) in `how_it_unfolds`.
- **IDEA-0104 (The Whole Team) → IDEA-0095.** Shares both concepts with IDEA-0095 and duplicates it as a
  physical-labour film; the two ideas already declared themselves mirrors. Folded in: the four marks of
  association [C019-P:L33-L47; C019-M:L3-L17] — arising together, ceasing together, one object, one base,
  each with a distinct function — and the situation of work that exists only when several people begin and
  finish it together. This is what lifts C019 in this group from a general "goodness is a team" gloss to
  its actual definition.

## 4. Holds and drops

- **Hold: IDEA-0097 (Practised Hands).** Strong and well grounded — proficiency (pāguññatā) is described
  in the source in exactly its terms: practised giving becomes easy and even sweet, first-time giving
  hesitates [C023-P:L153-L157] — with C011 as the guard-rail that the awkward version is still fully
  wholesome. It is held rather than merged because all ten slots are filled and because carrying its
  leading concept properly would mean adding C023 to EPD-0048's concept set (see §5). Held for a later
  slot, or as a second idea attached to the group's opening film if that film's concept set is ever
  widened.
- **No drops.** All four unused ideas were either merged or held; none was weak or duplicated another
  group's work.
- **No new idea created.** The spare block IDEA-0251 to IDEA-0259 is unused. Coverage does not require it:
  every group concept is linked by at least one kept idea — C011 (0093), C012 (0103, 0105), C019 (0093,
  0095, 0096, 0103), C023 (0095, 0096, 0100), C024 (0098, 0099, 0102, 0103, 0106), C027 (0102, 0105),
  C030 (0106), C091 (0100), C095 (0098, 0099, 0105, 0106) — and the two weakest carries, C019 and C024,
  were strengthened by the IDEA-0104 and IDEA-0101 folds rather than by inventing a film.

## 5. Still weak, and lessons needing an update

- **Episode `concept_ids` were deliberately left untouched, and this constrains two fixes.**
  `development/analysis/` holds the Abhidhamma depth map, whose check requires each concept's
  `linked_episode_ids` to match the episode slate exactly; it is a shared, cross-group file, so changing
  any GRP-06 episode's `concept_ids` would have meant editing another group's territory. Two changes are
  therefore recommended but **not made**:
  1. **EPD-0057 should drop C012.** After the rewrite, IDEA-0105's C012 link is honest but thin, and the
     episode's own lesson never teaches it (its `lesson.sources` cite only C027 and C095). Dropping C012
     from `concept_ids` and from the idea would lose nothing and remove a standing risk. It needs the
     depth map updated in the same pass.
  2. **EPD-0048 could gain C023.** If IDEA-0097's proficiency strand is ever folded into The Free Table
     instead of being held, C023 joins that episode and EPD-0048's lesson would need a sentence on
     practised giving becoming easy while the first, awkward attempt still counts.
- **EPD-0054's lesson needs an update (not made).** Its `the_teaching` presents the mother image and the
  Margaya's self-comparison without mentioning that the same concept's other source classes love confined
  to one's own family as attachment. The idea now holds that tension; the lesson still reads as if the
  sources agree. It should gain one sentence naming the disagreement, and should not resolve it.
- **EPD-0055's lesson is accurate but partial.** It already carries the kāya correction. After the
  IDEA-0104 fold, the episode's C019 content is broader than the lesson's single point about effort being
  ethically variable; a sentence on the four marks would bring it level. Not urgent.
- **Genuinely weak spots that remain.** IDEA-0100 depends entirely on a maker willing to talk about his
  own practice, and has no fallback if he is not. IDEA-0105 and IDEA-0106 both depend on consent that can
  be withdrawn after filming, and neither has an alternative if it is. IDEA-0099 depends on a morning
  nobody can arrange. These are research risks, not philosophy problems, and each idea's `drop_if` now
  says so plainly.
- **Uncertainties carried, not resolved.** C030's rule that only one of the five occasional beautiful
  factors arises at a time (flagged for expert check in its digest) is named as such inside IDEA-0106
  rather than asserted. C024's strong formulations about family pity and about grief as aversion, which
  the digest marks as the author's own, are likewise named rather than adopted.

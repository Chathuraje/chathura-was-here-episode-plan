# GRP-07 idea review — The Body

Reviewed 2026-09-19 by claude. Thirteen ideas (IDEA-0113 to IDEA-0125), nine episodes (EPD-0058 to
EPD-0066), twelve concepts (C063–C072, C093, C094). All thirteen ideas rewritten into the
situation/concept-merge shape; `premise`, `what_camera_could_observe`, `possible_arc` and `location`
deleted from every one, and every real place name scrubbed from idea text and loglines.

## 1. Philosophy findings and what changed

Every concept link was tested against its digest (`summary_en`, `human_interpretation_en`, `key_terms`,
`does_not_transfer`, `uncertainties`). Findings in order of seriousness:

1. **IDEA-0117 "Borrowed Parts", C067 — the primary link misreads the source, and the situation carries a
   flagged claim. Dropped.** The old `why` read a fitted prosthetic limb as a test of where "clung-to"
   (upādinna) matter begins. The C067 digest defines clung-to matter as a *psychological grip* that the
   sources apply especially to the eighteen kamma-born kinds, and its `does_not_transfer` says in as many
   words that "'clung-to' is not a claim about bodily autonomy or ownership ('you don't own your body')".
   A limb made in a workshop is not a candidate for that category on any reading of the digest, so the link
   cannot be repaired. The second problem is worse: the C064, C068, C069 and C071 digests each carry an
   explicit prohibition on using the sources' account of faculties and past action to explain any real
   person's disability ("never use it to say a person's blindness or deafness is deserved"; "must never be
   used to explain or imply anything about any real person's blindness, deafness or other disability"). A
   film placing a disabled participant inside this group's doctrinal frame would put that prohibition under
   permanent strain no matter how carefully the narration behaved. The idea is dropped, its concept links
   removed rather than rewritten, and **nothing from it is folded into a kept idea.** C067 remains covered
   by IDEA-0124, where the sources' own examples — the sense organs — are the subject.
2. **IDEA-0125 "Two Bodies, One Warmth", C072 — the `why` described a dramatic function, not a doctrine.**
   The old text said the elder's presence "quietly carries the group's closing question of what continues
   once a body ends". That is a statement about the edit, not about the source, and the digest's account of
   cessation has nothing to do with an ageing body while it is still living. Re-grounded, not dropped: the
   C072 digest's own third reading is that the end of the body's renewal is what fixes the moment of death,
   "we do not choose our time; the body's own running-down sets it". That is a claim about a living body's
   trajectory and it needs no death on screen. The link stays `supporting` with the new grounding; EPD-0065's
   `concept_ids` are therefore unchanged. If a reviewer prefers the stricter reading, the honest alternative
   is to drop C072 here and let EPD-0066 carry it alone.
3. **IDEA-0122 "Counting to Ten", C094 — the link overstated what the simile licenses.** The old `why` said
   the measurer's work *is* "mindfulness of breathing's own method of attention, transposed from breath to
   grain". The digest records the paddy measurer as the source's image *for* slow counting, and its
   `does_not_transfer` blocks four adjacent slogans at once: not relaxation, not breath control, not
   "anyone, anywhere, in five minutes", not "live in the moment". Rewritten so the film observes the original
   of an image rather than claiming a worker is meditating, with the limit of the comparison written into
   `concept_merge.what_it_reveals` and into the risks.
4. **IDEA-0113 "The Kiln and the Hand", C070 — the flour-ball image was used as though it were the
   teaching.** The digest states that the sources themselves say the image fails, because flour specks stay
   separate while a material group has no gap at all [C070-M:L5–L7], and warns that a kalāpa is not an atom,
   molecule or particle. Re-grounded on what the digest does support for this situation — that everything in
   the outside world, soil, stone, fire, water and wind, is the same basic group differing only in which
   element predominates — with the sources' own warning carried into the link, `what_must_be_real` and the
   risks. The C064 `why` also described air as what "dries and lightens"; the digest gives air as distension
   and motion, and separately flags that the book contradicts itself on whether lightness is earth element
   or a derived kind [C064-P:L42], so the lightness claim is out.
5. **IDEA-0118 "Two Kinds of Sound", C065 — an author's model stated as a fact about sound.** The old `why`
   asserted that sound "dies where it arises" and that what reaches an ear is "only sound's own moving
   trace". The digest is explicit that the chāyā/image account is the Pradeepika author's own explanatory
   model and that "a film should not claim that the texts 'anticipated science'". Kept as supporting, with
   the attribution written into the link. **Link added:** C066 (bodily and vocal intimation). The auction's
   hand signs are the digest's own example of a wave of the hand carrying meaning, working side by side with
   the shouted bid — a genuinely strong link the idea already carried in its situation and did not name. The
   digest's block on reading intimation as "body language" self-help is written into the link and the risks.
6. **IDEA-0124 "What the Eye Receives", C064 — traditional description presented as an anatomical fact.**
   The old `why` described "the eye's clear sensitivity … a minute area". The digest lists exactly these
   measurements (a louse's head, seven layers, a goat's hoof) under `does_not_transfer` as traditional
   descriptions, not anatomy. Rewritten to say so. The digest's prohibition on the "senses made by wanting"
   claim ever being used to suggest someone's sight loss is deserved is now in the link, in
   `what_must_be_real` and in the risks. **Link added:** C065, folded in from the IDEA-0115 merge.
7. **IDEA-0120 "Eleven Weeks", C071 — the most heavily flagged idea in the group.** The digest's
   `does_not_transfer` blocks four things this situation could drift into: presenting the weekly stages as
   embryology; the moisture-born category as biology; the Margaya's attribution of missing faculties to weak
   rebirth-kamma applied to any real person; and the premodern sex categories used to describe anyone. C066
   adds a fifth: the seventy-seven-day figure "must not be used in any reproductive-health or abortion
   argument" [C066-P:L125]. None of these was wrong in the old idea, but only the first two were guarded.
   All five are now written into `what_must_be_real` and the risks. The C066 link also notes the digest's
   own unresolved question about how *upacaya* and *santati* fit together, so the film cannot use the pair
   more confidently than the sources do.
8. **IDEA-0121 "Learning the Body", C093 — traditional anatomy needed an explicit bar.** The digest records
   bone, muscle and sinew counts, intestine lengths given by sex, heart-blood colour by temperament and
   thirty-two families of stomach worms as traditional commentary that "must not be presented as medical
   fact", and separately rules out both body-shaming and body-positivity readings of the practice. Both bars
   are now in `what_must_be_real` and the risks.
9. **IDEA-0114 and IDEA-0122, C068 — the "mind over matter" edge.** Neither idea claimed it, but both rest
   on mind-born matter moving and holding the body, which the digest's first `does_not_transfer` entry
   guards. Named in the risks of both, along with the bar on presenting the scholastic cluster counts and
   seventeen-mind-moment lifespans as physiology.
10. **No source contradiction was resolved.** Where the digests record a disagreement or an open question —
    ṭhiti versus jaratā for the middle phase; nine versus eight kinds absent at rebirth-linking; whether
    lightness is earth element or derived; how far to follow the breath inside the body — the ideas leave it
    open and say so rather than picking a side.

## 3. Merges

- **IDEA-0119 "Flour and Water" → IDEA-0113.** Gave the kept idea its grounded C070 link, the sources' own
  mixing image *and* the warning the sources attach to it, and the observation that a material judged
  entirely by hand before daylight is the same situation in a different trade. IDEA-0113's `what_happens`
  now says so, and explains why a firing is preferred: only a kiln supplies the change of conditions that
  C063 needs. Before the merge C070 was carried by one idea with a `why` the digest partly contradicted;
  after it, C070 is carried by two ideas with the digest's own caution attached to both.
- **IDEA-0115 "What the Hand Knows" → IDEA-0124.** Gave the kept idea a C065 link and the strongest single
  fact the group has about seeing: what reaches an eye is described as an image carried by light, a blend of
  countless small colours shifting with distance, so the true colour is never received and two people
  looking at one thing do not get the same thing. That turns IDEA-0124 from "a delicate organ can fail" into
  "a delicate organ delivers something that was never the thing itself", which is what the concept merge
  actually needed. C065 moves from one weak carry to two.

## 4. Holds and drops

- **HOLD — IDEA-0116 "The Weight of a Word."** Strong, doctrinally clean (the C066 digest names exactly its
  observation: a machine reproduces a voice and not the intimation) and well guarded on consent. It has no
  slot: the nine episodes are filled, and its real subject is what is left of a person once the body that
  meant something has stopped — closer to the question this group *hands on* than to the body films
  themselves. Held for the group that takes up what continues after a body ends. Its concept ground is not
  lost here: C066's intimation pair is now carried live by IDEA-0118.
- **DROP — IDEA-0117 "Borrowed Parts."** Reasons in finding 1. Nothing folded forward.

## 5. Still weak, and lessons needing an update

1. **`development/analysis/abhidhamma-depth-map.json` needs regeneration, and its check will fail until it
   is.** Three episodes gained a concept (EPD-0061 +C066, EPD-0062 +C065, EPD-0066 +C070), so the depth map's
   `linked_episode_ids` for C065, C066 and C070 are stale, and C065 and C070 move from one episode to two,
   which also changes `summary.concept_status_counts`. I deliberately did **not** edit that file: it is a
   single global artefact, other groups are being finalised in parallel and would collide on the same summary
   block. It should be regenerated once all ten groups are done. *(If it happens to pass when you read this,
   another agent has already regenerated it.)*
2. **Three episode lessons are now narrower than their episode's concept set.** None is inaccurate, so per
   the brief none was edited. EPD-0061's lesson covers mind-born and temperature-born sound but not bodily
   intimation (C066); EPD-0062's covers the sensitive kinds and the "internal" pair but not C065's account of
   what the eye receives; EPD-0066's covers the order of cessation but not C070's point that nineteen of the
   twenty-one kinds of material group exist only in living bodies. Each would be a one-sentence addition.
3. **EPD-0064's title, "Eleven Weeks", names the traditional seventy-seven-night figure in the headline.**
   The figure is the sources' own and the idea now guards it heavily, but a title that states a doctrinal
   count as a milestone is the single most likely place for this group to be misread as embryology. Not
   changed — it is a title decision, not a philosophy fix — but it is worth Chathura's eye.
4. **C067, C069, C071, C093 and C094 are each carried by exactly one kept idea.** That is a real thinness in
   a group of twelve concepts and nine films, and there is no honest way to fix it by invention. It is the
   reason IDEA-0115 and IDEA-0119 were merged rather than dropped: both merges moved a concept from one
   carry to two.
5. **IDEA-0113's merged situation description is the weakest join in the group.** Folding a mixing trade
   into a pottery week works at the level of the concept, but a reader will notice the film is still one
   household and one kiln. If the C070 material ever feels bolted on in the brief, the right repair is to
   restore IDEA-0119 as its own film, not to weaken IDEA-0113.
6. **Two kept ideas depend on events nobody can schedule** — a death (IDEA-0123) and a year that could end
   in a loss (IDEA-0125). Both now say plainly in `how_it_unfolds` that reality decides, and both forbid
   waiting on the event as an opportunity, but the scheduling risk is real and belongs in production planning
   rather than in the ideas.

# The footage-led slate

The current record of the 137 candidate ideas. Supersedes the ten per-group reviews now in
`idea-review/retired-2026-09-21/`, which describe the retired human-situation model.

## What an idea is now

Each film is primarily **drone and ground video of one real location**, with a voice explaining a
piece of Abhidhamma over footage that matches it. Two layers have to work at once: with the sound
off a viewer who has no interest in philosophy gets a complete piece, and with the sound on the same
footage becomes a demonstration of the concepts. The standard held throughout was that **the single
most beautiful shot a location produces should also be the clearest statement of its concept** —
not an illustration bolted onto pretty pictures.

The record carries `place` (what kind of place, why it is worth watching, what moves or changes,
when it looks best), `two_layers`, and a `sequence` of 7–9 beats each pairing `on_screen` with the
`voice` line spoken over it. The retired `situation` block is gone, as is the separate location
selection: a place is now proposed inside the idea as `suggested_location`, and Chathura's single
`review` verdict accepts or refuses the idea and its place together.

## The standing constraints

These were not stylistic preferences. Each came out of a `does_not_transfer` line in a concept
digest, and about forty ideas lost their subject entirely to them.

**No person is an exhibit.** A camera cannot show which mental factor is present, and several
digests say so outright, so no film presents an identifiable person as angry, envious, greedy,
confused, kind, composed or attained. The films that had to be rebuilt included a family sorted by
temperament, a master deciding who was ready, newcomers asked where their minds go, a reformed
offender, and a worker admired for not flinching.

**No disability, illness or dying.** C071 states that the source attributes missing faculties to
weak rebirth-kamma, and that this must never touch a real person's blindness, deafness or other
disability; C051 ties congenital blindness to a type of rebirth-consciousness with the same bar.
Two eye-surgery camps, a prosthetics workshop, an elder's fading hearing, a midwife's rounds, a
night ward, a monk at deathbeds and two emergency crews were all dropped for this.

**No bereavement and no legacy reading.** C061 says plainly that "we live on through our legacy and
in people's memories" is *not* the teaching — the continuity is a causal stream of mind, not
reputation or inheritance. Films built on opening a dead man's room, a seventh-day almsgiving, an
inheritance walk and a dead relative's recording were dropped.

**Kamma is not fate.** No film may let poverty, loss, illness or a failed harvest read as deserved.
C080 states that ageing and grief are conditioned by birth itself, not a verdict on anyone
suffering them; C090 forbids implying that good people are protected, which would mean anyone
harmed lacked goodness. One film states outright that nothing in these texts explains a flood.

**Livelihoods are not destinies.** The sources name hunters, fishermen and those who kill cattle,
goats and chickens and link them to a particular destination. C051 and C056 both forbid using that
to frame real working communities, so no film centres such a household.

**The unconditioned cannot be filmed.** C100's object is Nibbāna and no shot is it. Where the voice
in Group 10 reaches for it, the film says what it cannot show rather than letting a beautiful still
frame imply it.

**Archetypes used up.** Repetition was the other constant pressure. Weekly fairs, fish auctions and
landings, salterns, gem sites, pottery villages, tea estates, irrigation systems, free-food giving,
funeral-aid societies, railway lines, drum-making, rubber and latex, ruined monastic sites, seed
banks, palmyra jaggery, kilns, hydro and water supply, rope and ferry crossings, emergency services
and meditation retreats each became unavailable once used. Five collisions still slipped through
parallel work and were relocated afterwards: potters, latex sheds, a seed genebank, palmyra jaggery,
and four water-diversion films — one agent having written the same film twice in a single batch.

## What is not done

- **Confirmation.** 135 of 137 ideas are `pending`. Nothing downstream can be built until the slate
  is cut to 98. See `09-concept-coverage.md` before rejecting anything: 25 concepts currently rest
  on a single candidate, and each concept sits in exactly one group, so a rejection there removes
  it from the series for good. A data check fails if a full group's confirmed films miss one of its
  concepts.
- **The chronology layer.** `development/episodes/` is empty. The film order, the group blocks, the
  object-acquisition points, the `thread_in`/`thread_out` continuity, the episode lessons and the
  public numbering 2–99 all lived there and need rebuilding from the confirmed ideas. The episode
  tier of `check-data.mjs` is dormant rather than satisfied while the directory is empty.
- **The depth map.** `development/analysis/abhidhamma-depth-map.json` still cites 98 episode ids that
  no longer exist, so it measures a slate that is not there. It should be regenerated from the
  confirmed films once the chronology layer is rebuilt.
- **Location records.** `LOC-0001` and decision `REV-0006` are orphaned by the move to
  `suggested_location`. They are Chathura's own entries and were left in place rather than deleted.
- **Research.** Every proposed place is desk research. No access, permission, participant or drone
  clearance has been verified anywhere, and each record says so in its `research_note` and `unknowns`.

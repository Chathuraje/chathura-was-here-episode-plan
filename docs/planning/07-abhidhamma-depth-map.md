# Abhidhamma Depth Map

Status: draft analysis for Chathura's Chronology v1 review  
Date: 2026-09-19  
Structured record: `development/analysis/abhidhamma-depth-map.json`  
Dashboard: `/depth-map`

## 1. Purpose

This pass asks one question: does the current 98-film chronology build a deeper Abhidhamma mental model, or does it only place concepts and metaphors on screen?

The answer is **mixed: the slate contains real cumulative teaching, but that depth is unevenly distributed**. It is not merely surface-level. Groups 2, 3, 5, 6, and 7 contain genuine progressions in which later films add distinctions or applications. Group 5's cognitive-process sequence is the clearest example. However, broad classifications and the late structures of dependent origination, Paṭṭhāna, vipassanā, path/fruition, planes, and several specialised processes are too compressed to count as fully taught.

This is a separate recommendation layer. It does not approve or reject films, alter chronology, rewrite titles or loglines, change selected ideas, select locations, or import outside Buddhist knowledge to correct the project.

## 2. Method

The audit used only the repository's current:

- C001–C102 digests and their source-grounded summaries;
- GRP-01–GRP-10 concept roles, lessons, progression lists, and handoffs;
- EPD-0001–EPD-0098 loglines, linked concepts, episode lessons, and `builds_on` text;
- slate notes and current planning documents.

Concept linkage was treated as evidence of placement, not proof of teaching. Each judgment asks:

> After watching the relevant episodes, what can the viewer understand that they could not understand before?

Three learning levels were separated:

1. **Experience level:** the viewer feels or notices a teaching through an event, image, or situation.
2. **Understanding level:** the viewer can state a distinction or causal mechanism in simple terms.
3. **Structure level:** the viewer can place that mechanism inside a larger system and recall or apply it later.

Repeated metaphors do not automatically count as cumulative development. A concept is `built` only when another episode adds a contrast, step, or application. It is `integrated` only when it becomes part of a larger mechanism or a meaningful cross-group handoff.

## 3. Coverage scale

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `missing` | No meaningful encounter in the current slate. |
| `surface_only` | Present mainly as image, theme, label, or analogy; unlikely to produce a reconstructable mechanism. |
| `introduced` | A legible first definition or distinction, without enough later development. |
| `built` | Multiple steps, contrasts, or applications actively develop understanding. |
| `integrated` | Built and connected to earlier or later systems as part of a wider mental model. |
| `dangerously_compressed` | More doctrinal structure is being carried than the current narrative space can make legible. |

The status describes the current teaching sequence, not the importance or correctness of a source concept.

## 4. Overall verdict

The 102 concepts divide as follows:

| Depth status | Concepts |
|---|---:|
| Integrated | 9 |
| Built | 34 |
| Introduced | 19 |
| Surface only | 8 |
| Dangerously compressed | 32 |
| Missing | 0 |

Forty-three concepts reach `built` or `integrated`. This is enough to reject the conclusion that the slate only touches the surface. It is not enough to say that the whole deeper architecture has been taught.

The strongest system-building occurs where the chronology slows down and differentiates one mechanism over several films:

- wanting by feeling, aim, prompting, and desire-to-act;
- hate-rooted and associated unwholesome structures;
- the sensory and mind-door cognitive process;
- beautiful mental factors and the four sublime abidings;
- matter, its elements, origins, life-course, decay, and cessation.

The weakest system-building occurs where one film is asked to carry a whole taxonomy or an advanced process. This is especially visible in:

- the 121-citta map and several one-film citta classes;
- root-at-birth person types and the full defilement compendium;
- plane and rebirth-linking classifications;
- the twelve-link structure of dependent origination;
- the twenty-four Paṭṭhāna conditions;
- insight and the seven purifications;
- requisites, path/fruition process, direct-knowledge process, and attainment of cessation.

## 5. Major strengths

### Cognitive process is the benchmark

GRP-05 progresses from doors and bodily bases to object intensity, bare sensory data, turning, mind-door recall, universal factors, the seventeen-moment chart, shortened processes, no-controller sequencing, and javana. The viewer can understand that seeing is a conditioned sequence rather than one continuous act. Later death and absorption processes can build on that model.

### Mental-factor teaching accumulates

GRP-02 introduces feeling and desire-to-act. GRP-03 differentiates unwholesome factors. GRP-05 shows universal and ethically variable factors. GRP-06 contrasts them with beautiful factors. This lets the viewer understand a mind-state as an assembled event rather than an indivisible mood or a fixed personality.

### The body/matter arc has internal progression

GRP-07 moves through dependence on mind, food, and weather; breathing; elements; sense objects and bases; body parts; origins across a life; decay; and cessation at death. Several sub-classifications remain introductory, but the main material-process model is built.

### Conventional and ultimate reality form a usable opening lens

GRP-01 uses names, time, wholes and parts, space, sensory knowing, apparent continuity, and personhood. The distinction returns when the series examines bare colour, the body, continuity without a traveller, and Nibbāna. The risk is not absence but distortion: later review must preserve the difference without reducing it to “nothing is real.”

### Kamma and rebirth have a coherent central correction

GRP-08 repeatedly protects two ideas: kamma is not fate, and rebirth is continuity without a traveller. The classifications around that spine are compressed, but the spine itself is understandable.

## 6. Major compressed and problem areas

### Broad catalogues are often mistaken for mechanisms

C018, C074, C075, C077, and C085 each gather large fields. Their episodes can let a viewer know that a map exists, but naming the map is not equivalent to teaching its organisation. These records are marked compressed even when their images are effective.

### Dependent origination arrives as a retrospective load

GRP-09 rightly tries to reveal Groups 2–8 as one conditioned story. That is a strong structural intention. The current eight-film group must also carry two methods of conditional explanation, twelve links, periods, factors, connections, rounds, roots, twenty-four conditions, and a mixed compendium. The viewer can understand “because of this, that” and the stopping of a fed process. The viewer is unlikely to reconstruct the complete dependent-origination architecture.

### Paṭṭhāna is felt more than understood

Channels, pumps, leaning poles, weather, food, roofs, and extinguished kilns convey that support takes different forms. They do not yet differentiate enough of the twenty-four conditions for the viewer to hold the Paṭṭhāna logic. This mechanism is `dangerously_compressed`, not `built`.

### Group 10 contains the highest concept density

Twenty concepts sit in twelve films. The ascent from calm through insight and irreversible change to Nibbāna is emotionally coherent, but the structural load is not. Fine-material and immaterial minds, jhāna factors, meditation subjects, insight, seven purifications, requisites, noble persons, path and fruition processes, direct knowledges, powers, cessation, and Nibbāna all compete for teaching space.

### Nibbāna is framed better than the route to it

The final film's framing—unconditioned, not a feeling or a place, uncovered rather than built—completes Group 1's map well. The main risk lies immediately before it: vipassanā and path/fruition are not built to the depth of the cognitive process. The arrival can therefore be understood more clearly than the architecture of the route.

### Handoffs

The strongest handoffs deepen an existing mechanism:

- GRP-02 feeling → GRP-05 contact and process → GRP-09 dependent origination;
- GRP-05 cognitive process → GRP-08 death process → GRP-10 absorption and path processes;
- GRP-01 four ultimates → GRP-07 matter → GRP-10 Nibbāna.

Other handoffs mostly change theme unless review protects the recall:

- GRP-04's vocabulary of being stuck needs a clearer later role in dependent origination and uprooting;
- GRP-06's good action/result question needs an explicit bridge into GRP-08;
- GRP-07's material cessation needs a clean contrast with GRP-08's causal mental continuity.

## 9. Recommendation summary

These are attention flags, not episode changes.

### High priority

1. Review GRP-09 film by film. Require a stateable causal distinction after each episode; do not count a moving analogy as understanding of the twenty-four conditions.
2. Review GRP-10 as an architecture, not only an emotional ascent. Decide which advanced maps are intentionally introduced and which the slate genuinely claims to build.
3. Preserve GRP-08's no-traveller/not-fate spine while treating surrounding one-film classifications as introductions unless later films reinforce them.
4. Inspect every `dangerously_compressed` and `surface_only` concept in the dashboard before calling its source area covered.

### Medium priority

1. Test C049 and C074 in GRP-04 for understanding rather than naming.
2. Protect cross-group recalls: feeling → contact → craving; ordinary cognitive process → death/absorption/path processes; mind/factors/matter → Nibbāna.
3. Distinguish a mechanism's experiential metaphor from its structure-level explanation in review notes.

### Preserve

1. Preserve GRP-05's order; it is the best current example of cumulative mechanism teaching.
2. Preserve the progressive contrasts in GRP-02, GRP-03, GRP-06, and GRP-07.
3. Preserve GRP-01/GRP-10's four-ultimate bookend and the safeguards around Nibbāna.

## 10. Suggested use during Chronology v1 review

Review in this order for depth risk: **GRP-09 → GRP-10 → GRP-08 → GRP-04**, then use GRP-05 as a comparison standard.

For each flagged concept or mechanism:

1. Open its dashboard row and linked episodes.
2. State in one sentence what the viewer can understand after each film.
3. Identify whether the next appearance adds a distinction, application, or recall.
4. If it only repeats an image or label, retain `surface_only` or `introduced`.
5. If a large taxonomy is carried by one narrative event, retain `dangerously_compressed` unless the chronology review finds real internal steps already present.
6. Record any future attention as a recommendation; do not silently change the approved-data layer.

The practical conclusion is:

> The current structure carries several deep, teachable mechanisms, but it does not yet carry the deeper architecture evenly. The middle of the slate often builds understanding; the late synthesis and liberation architecture too often asks a small number of films to carry whole systems.

# Consolidated project rules

This document records the working rules supplied on 2026-09-18. Labels distinguish current instructions from assumptions and decisions that remain open.

## Authority labels

- **Confirmed current instruction** — use now unless Chathura changes it.
- **Provisional assumption** — safe working position, but not a locked creative decision.
- **Unresolved decision** — must be reviewed before dependent work is finalized.

## Purpose and editorial stance

**Confirmed current instruction**

- *Chathura Was Here* is a cinematic documentary project, mainly set in Sri Lanka.
- The governing sequence is **Story → Place → Experience**.
- Each film follows Chathura’s outward-looking point of view through real people, places, journeys, memories, and experiences.
- Each film must stand independently and feel immersive, not like a travel vlog or a philosophical lecture.
- Abhidhamma concepts may support the underlying human exploration, but real people and events must never be forced to illustrate doctrine or act as puzzle props.
- Never fabricate authentic participant dialogue or events.

## Content development

**Confirmed current instruction**

- Preserve original source documents and concept extracts unchanged.
- Concepts and episodes have a many-to-many relationship.
- A single idea may combine multiple concepts only where the connection is meaningful.
- Not every concept must become an episode.
- For each developed concept, preserve source meaning in a clear Sinhala explanation that retains appropriate Buddhist terms.
- Develop a separate, simple-English human interpretation without Buddhist jargon.
- Keep these evidence classes distinct: source teaching, editorial interpretation, documentary possibility, and verified real-world evidence.
- Source citations must remain traceable to exact repository files and, where available, page/line metadata.

## Series structures and numbering

**Confirmed current instruction**

- Working target: 100 films.
- Release Episode 1 is **The Beginning**.
- Release Episode 100 is **The Way Back**.
- Episode-development and screenplay generation covers Episodes 2–99 only: 98 films.
- Episodes 1 and 100 are stored separately as continuity references, not mixed into the 98-film development pool.
- Develop ten connected chronological groups whose flexible sizes total 98. A group may contain five, eight, ten, or more films; ten is not a maximum.
- Chronological groups, public release series, seasons, and filming schedules are independent structures.
- Do not assign permanent public release numbers while developing initial ideas.
- Use stable internal identifiers that do not depend on chronological position or release number.

Arithmetic invariant:

`100 total films = 2 framing films (Episodes 1 and 100) + 98 development films (Episodes 2–99)`

**Provisional assumption**

- “100 films” remains a working target until the flexible group structure and viable stories are reviewed; the system must enforce the current arithmetic without pretending the creative slate is already locked.

## Objects

**Confirmed current instruction**

- Assign Object 1 through Object 10 to chronological Groups 1 through 10 respectively.
- Each object is acquired in its group’s first chronological episode.
- Plan a subtle appearance in later episodes of that group.
- Once acquired, the object remains owned unless a real event changes that state.
- Objects from earlier groups may recur in later groups.
- Keep each object’s physical identity, images, and acquisition details unresolved for now.
- In the intended Episode 1 chronology, all ten objects have already been acquired. Record that as **planned ownership state**, not as verified acquisition evidence.
- Keep planned ownership, verified acquisition, later possession changes, and shot-by-shot visibility as separate facts.
- An object being outside the camera frame is not evidence that it has not yet been acquired.
- Ordinary ownership and continuity checks do not require every object to be visible in Episode 1 or in every shot. **Specific Episode 100 exception:** the room reveal is planned to show all ten objects together in one frame before packing. This is intended creative staging, not verified footage or proof of acquisition.
- Object rules attach to chronological groups, not seasons.

## Locations

**Confirmed current instruction**

- Chathura has exclusive control over final location selection.
- AI may describe location requirements and offer suggestions.
- Suggested locations and Chathura-selected locations must be separate fields and separate decisions.
- AI must never promote a suggestion into the selected-location field.
- An idea may exist without a chosen location.
- A location-dependent screenplay cannot advance until Chathura selects the location and the required real-world research is sufficient.
- Track whether a location’s name is revealed early, later, or never.

## Framing continuity

**Confirmed current instruction**

- Preserve the scene-level structure: **Episode 100 Part A → Episode 1 → Episode 100 Part B**.
- Episode 1 opens with crowded-location footage, cuts away, later contains the monastery experience, and ends with Chathura disappearing into the forest.
- Episode 100 opens with the same crowded-location footage but continues beyond Episode 1’s cut.
- Episode 100 Part A continues through the room, a planned reveal of all ten objects together in one frame before packing, departure, and travel toward Hunnasgiriya/Meemure.
- Part A reaches the exact planned road-footage point where Episode 1 originally resumed.
- Episode 100 revisits selected Episode 1 footage and reaches the forest disappearance.
- Episode 100 Part B continues beyond that forest ending.
- The same footage may gain different meaning after the audience’s accumulated experience.
- Support both episode-level and scene-level chronology because Episode 100 surrounds Episode 1 in story chronology.
- Preserve shared-footage references and exact edit boundaries wherever source material establishes them.
- Treat these as intended creative relationships. Actual footage IDs, timecodes, technical edit boundaries, and verified events remain unknown until supplied.
- Do not invent missing boundaries, dialogue, events, or participant behavior.

**Confirmed current instruction (Chathura, 2026-09-18)**

- The chronologically last development film (the final film of GRP-10) is released as Episode 99 and ends on the crowded-location footage shared with Episodes 1 and 100.

## Editorial form

**Confirmed current instruction**

- Begin inside the story, using footage and early voice-over.
- Reveal season/episode information, coordinates, and title only after curiosity develops.
- Do not automatically reveal a location name.
- Prioritize natural sound, observational footage, and room to breathe.
- General duration target: 5–10 minutes; ideal range 6–8 minutes; story needs may override both.
- End with “A Film by Chathura,” followed by the project logo.
- Every eventual screenplay must include scene purpose, required footage, sound, narration, estimated timing, research gaps, and continuity notes.
- Keep a pre-filming production screenplay distinct from any post-filming revision grounded in captured footage.
- Screenplay narration is written in English for now; Sinhala translation is a later step (Chathura, 2026-09-18).
- The ten chronological groups follow an emotional life-story arc whose depth reveals the book's philosophy (Chathura, 2026-09-18). See `06-master-roadmap.md` §4.

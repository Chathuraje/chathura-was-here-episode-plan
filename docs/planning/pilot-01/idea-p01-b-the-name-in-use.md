# IDEA-P01-B — The Name in Use

## Premise

Follow people as they use, teach, write, negotiate, or act upon different names for the same shared place, object, route, role, or practice. The story is not a debate over which word is philosophically “real.” It is a practical human story about what names enable: finding a way, handing down knowledge, coordinating work, welcoming someone, or deciding what deserves care. The camera stays with the actions produced by those names and with the materials that remain present regardless of what they are called.

## Central human question

What changes in people’s actions and relationships when the same part of their shared world carries different names or meanings?

## Concept connections

**Primary — C084, ප්‍රඥප්තිය.** Its distinctions among conventional wholes, names, spoken sound, and assigned meaning support a film about language in use without collapsing the world into “everything is imaginary” [C084 dossier](c084-concepts-and-names.md).

C020 and C032 are deliberately not assigned. This idea tests whether one concept can support a complete human inquiry without forcing a full pilot-concept bundle.

## Unverified possibilities to investigate

- People teaching another person how to navigate, identify, maintain, or use something through local and formal terms.
- A shared activity where different generations, trades, language communities, or institutions use differing labels.
- A real moment when a name is written, corrected, translated, remembered, questioned, or acted upon.

These are possible research directions only. No participant, naming conflict, access, practice, or event has been found or confirmed.

## What the camera could observe

- A word spoken in context, written by hand, printed, pointed to, translated, corrected, or ignored.
- People giving and following directions, teaching terms, locating materials, or coordinating action.
- The physical place/object/practice from multiple distances, alongside the different actions its labels prompt.
- Natural sound that lets pronunciation, repetition, uncertainty, and response remain audible.

## Movement and stakes

A concrete need can move the film: someone must find, teach, repair, deliver, preserve, or coordinate something, and the names they use may help or hinder that work if reality confirms it. Stakes might involve access, continuity of knowledge, responsibility, or belonging, but none is presumed. The film must discover what actually matters to participants.

## Possible change in viewer understanding

The viewer may start by treating a name as a neutral label and later see it as part of a relationship and a practice. The film must leave room for the opposite finding—that different terms cause no meaningful difficulty—or for meanings that cannot be translated neatly.

## Location requirements

- A lived-in setting where more than one naming practice is genuinely used and can be observed through action.
- Ethical access to the people who use those terms and to the relevant physical details or records.
- Conditions suitable for clear natural-sound recording of speech in context.
- No location is proposed or selected; selection remains exclusively Chathura’s.

## Research questions and evidence needed

- Which terms are actually used, by whom, in what language and situation?
- Do the terms point to the same referent, overlapping referents, or genuinely different things?
- What practical action, relationship, memory, or responsibility is affected?
- Are translations and spellings accepted by participants, and who has authority to explain them?
- What observation, interview, document, sign, map, or other record supports each naming claim?
- Could filming expose private knowledge, intensify a dispute, or privilege an official label over lived usage?

## Why this deserves a film

The independent story is people coordinating a shared life through language while dealing with a material task or need. C084 supplies a careful underlying question, but the film has reason to exist only if naming changes what people do, remember, pass on, or care for. It should work as a documentary even if no doctrinal term appears.

## Link to IDEA-P01-A

This idea contrasts with [IDEA-P01-A](idea-p01-a-the-work-between-moments.md): one watches shared language organize the outer situation; the other watches a person repeatedly adjust within a task. Together they could carry the question of how immediate experience and assigned meaning meet, but each stands alone.

## Revise or abandon if

- Research finds only interchangeable synonyms with no human consequence or observable action.
- The story depends on declaring one community’s term correct and another’s false.
- Access to relevant speakers or contexts is absent, or consent/privacy risks outweigh the value.
- Translation cannot be checked with the people concerned.
- The concept becomes a lecture while the real people and practical story remain thin.

Among the twelve types of unwholesome states of consciousness, a primary division consists of **states of mind rooted in greed or attachment**. These mental states operate in eight distinct forms through the combination of three primary factors:

1. **According to Feeling:** The state arises either accompanied by a pleasant feeling (pleasure or joy) or accompanied by a neutral feeling (neither pleasant nor painful).
2. **According to View:** The state arises either conjoined with a wrong view (an erroneous perspective) or dissociated from a wrong view.
3. **According to Prompting:** The state arises either without prompting (spontaneously, without internal or external instigation) or with prompting (through inducement or effort).

The combination of these factors gives rise to the eight specific forms of greed-rooted consciousness:
* A state accompanied by a pleasant feeling, conjoined with a wrong view, and unprompted.
* A state accompanied by a pleasant feeling, conjoined with a wrong view, and prompted.
* A state accompanied by a pleasant feeling, dissociated from a wrong view, and unprompted.
* A state accompanied by a pleasant feeling, dissociated from a wrong view, and prompted.
* A state accompanied by a neutral feeling, conjoined with a wrong view, and unprompted.
* A state accompanied by a neutral feeling, conjoined with a wrong view, and prompted.
* A state accompanied by a neutral feeling, dissociated from a wrong view, and unprompted.
* A state accompanied by a neutral feeling, dissociated from a wrong view, and prompted.

Seven specific courses of unwholesome action are carried out directly through these greed-rooted states of mind: taking what is not given (theft), sexual misconduct, false speech (lying), divisive speech (slander), idle or frivolous chatter, covetousness (the desire to possess another's property), and wrong view.

In daily human life, greed-rooted states of mind arise frequently across a wide range of common activities. They predominantly occur in consuming alcohol, gambling, taking and giving bribes, playing or listening to musical instruments like lutes, singing and listening to songs, dancing and watching dances for enjoyment, applying perfumes for sensory pleasure, wearing fine clothes and jewelry, eating delicious foods and drinking sweet beverages for gratification, viewing beautiful objects for pleasure, and playing or watching sports and games. They also largely govern activities such as making efforts to generate wealth, acquiring it, guarding acquired wealth, spending money to secure desired objects, and preparing and using possessions such as houses and vehicles.

In ordinary social and domestic life, these states arise abundantly when young men and women see, speak with, and associate with one another; when spouses see each other and engage in affectionate conversation; when providing fine clothing for wives and children; when going on leisure trips; and when nourishing, pampering, and arranging marriages for children. For an ordinary person living distanced from moral and mental cultivation, the majority of mental states arising throughout the entire day, from waking until going to sleep, are rooted in greed and attachment.

However, an external action does not in itself determine the unwholesome nature of the mind; the underlying purpose and mental disposition determine whether an act arises from greed or remains free from it:
* **Wealth and Resources:** Earning, preserving, and spending wealth out of desire and the pursuit of sensory pleasure generates greed-rooted states. However, when one earns, protects, and spends wealth—or provides clothing and food—guided by faith, loving-kindness, compassion, or practical wisdom considering utility and necessity, unwholesome states do not arise.
* **Food:** Preparing or consuming food out of craving for taste or with the desire to enhance bodily appearance produces greed-rooted states. In contrast, preparing and eating food solely to appease hunger and sustain physical life does not produce unwholesome states.
* **Clothing:** Preparing and wearing clothes with the desire to look attractive, to capture the admiration of others, or to appear superior generates greed-rooted states. However, preparing and wearing clothes with the intention of covering private parts, protecting against cold, heat, and insect bites, or avoiding the contempt of others resulting from an unkempt appearance does not produce unwholesome states. Even preparing or wearing fine and valuable clothing does not produce an unwholesome state if the sole intention is to avoid such social disrespect.
* **Treatment of Animals and Children:** Giving food to animals or young children merely for the amusement of watching them eat is driven by greed-rooted states of mind. Conversely, giving food to animals out of genuine kindness and support produces wholesome mental states. Furthermore, capturing animals upon seeing their beauty, or rearing and feeding them merely to enjoy looking at their beauty, arises from greed-rooted consciousness.

Thus, when daily activities are carried out through attachment, the craving for sensory indulgence, or the desire for status, they manifest as greed-rooted unwholesome states. When those same activities are undertaken with wisdom, benevolence, and a clear understanding of practical necessity, the mind remains free from unwholesome attachment.

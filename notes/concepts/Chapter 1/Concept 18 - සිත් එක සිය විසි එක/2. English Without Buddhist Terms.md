In this systematic analysis, consciousness and its various operations are examined with exact precision. Based on the different levels of the mind, the specific factors that accompany it, and their functional roles, the whole of consciousness is classified into one hundred and twenty-one (121) distinct states across four primary planes: the sensory plane, the refined-form plane, the formless plane, and the transcendent plane.

---

### 1. Sensory-Plane Consciousness (54 States)
This plane comprises consciousness that arises in connection with the five physical senses and ordinary mental objects. It includes twelve unwholesome states, eighteen rootless states, and twenty-four beautiful sensory states.

*   **Unwholesome States of Consciousness (12):** These are mental states that drive harmful actions:
    *   **States Rooted in Attachment (8):** These arise based on craving or clinging. They are distinguished by whether they occur with joyful feeling or neutral feeling, with false belief or without false belief, and spontaneously (unprompted) or through prompting (instigated). Combining these three factors yields eight states.
    *   **States Rooted in Aversion (2):** These arise based on conflict, anger, or ill-will. They are always accompanied by mental displeasure and reactive resistance, occurring either spontaneously or through prompting, yielding two states.
    *   **States Rooted in Delusion (2):** These arise based on fundamental confusion. Both occur with a neutral feeling: one is accompanied by skeptical doubt, and the other is accompanied by mental restlessness.

*   **Rootless States of Consciousness (18):** These states operate without the primary causal roots (attachment, aversion, delusion, or their wholesome opposites) and carry out basic sensory and cognitive processing:
    *   **Unwholesome Resultant States (7):** As passive results of past unwholesome actions, these include neutral sensory consciousness of unpleasant objects through the eye, ear, nose, and tongue; bodily consciousness experiencing physical pain; neutral receiving consciousness that accepts the sensory object; and neutral investigating consciousness that examines the object.
    *   **Rootless Wholesome Resultant States (8):** As passive results of past wholesome actions, these include neutral sensory consciousness of pleasant objects through the eye, ear, nose, and tongue; bodily consciousness experiencing physical pleasure; neutral receiving consciousness; joyful investigating consciousness; and neutral investigating consciousness.
    *   **Rootless Functional States (3):** These are non-resultant operational states that do not generate moral consequences: neutral attention-directing toward the five physical senses, neutral attention-directing toward the mind-door, and joyful consciousness producing a smile in a fully liberated being.

*   **Beautiful Sensory States of Consciousness (24):** These are wholesome, morally refined states operating within the sensory plane:
    *   **Wholesome States (8):** Arising during ethical actions such as generosity and moral discipline, these vary by whether they are accompanied by understanding (knowledge) or dissociated from it, accompanied by joy or neutral feeling, and whether they occur spontaneously or through prompting.
    *   **Wholesome Resultant States (8):** These are the eight mature resultant states corresponding directly to the eight wholesome states.
    *   **Functional States (8):** These are the eight corresponding active states performed by fully liberated beings, which perform their ethical function without accumulating karmic momentum.

---

### 2. Refined-Form Meditative Consciousness (15 States)
These are elevated states of consciousness attained through the cultivation of meditative absorptions centered on refined material forms. They progress through five distinct levels by sequentially letting go of coarser absorption factors (directed thought, sustained thought, rapture, bliss, and one-pointedness of mind):
1.  **First Absorption:** Contains directed thought, sustained thought, rapture, bliss, and one-pointedness.
2.  **Second Absorption:** Lacks directed thought; contains sustained thought, rapture, bliss, and one-pointedness.
3.  **Third Absorption:** Lacks sustained thought; contains rapture, bliss, and one-pointedness.
4.  **Fourth Absorption:** Lacks rapture; contains bliss and one-pointedness.
5.  **Fifth Absorption:** Lacks bliss; contains equanimity (neutral feeling) and one-pointedness.

These five levels of absorption manifest as five wholesome states, five resultant states (which determine rebirth in refined-form realms), and five functional states (experienced by liberated beings), totaling fifteen states.

---

### 3. Formless Meditative Consciousness (12 States)
These are deep meditative absorptions that transcend all awareness of physical form, focusing instead on purely non-material dimensions:
1.  **The Base of Boundless Space:** Directing awareness toward infinite space.
2.  **The Base of Boundless Consciousness:** Directing awareness toward infinite consciousness.
3.  **The Base of Nothingness:** Directing awareness toward absolute voidness or the absence of anything.
4.  **The Base of Neither-Perception-Nor-Non-Perception:** Directing awareness toward the subtlest threshold of perception.

Each of these four formless absorptions manifests as four wholesome states, four resultant states (determining rebirth in formless realms), and four functional states of liberated beings, totaling twelve states.

---

### 4. Transcendent Consciousness (40 States)
These are supreme states of consciousness that directly transcend worldly existence to eradicate mental defilements and realize ultimate liberation. They correspond to four successive stages of spiritual realization (the stage of entering the stream of liberation, the stage of once-returning, the stage of non-returning, and the stage of full liberation):

*   **Transcendent Wholesome States (20):** These are the transformative path states that eradicate defilements. Each of the four stages of realization is differentiated according to the five absorption levels, producing twenty path states (4 × 5 = 20).
*   **Transcendent Resultant States (20):** These are the immediate fruition states of peace and freedom that follow each path state, likewise differentiated across the five absorption levels, producing twenty fruition states (4 × 5 = 20).

Together, the path and fruition states constitute forty transcendent states of consciousness.

---

### Total Synthesis
The comprehensive classification of all consciousness is summarized as follows:
*   **Sensory-Plane States:** 54 (12 Unwholesome + 18 Rootless + 24 Beautiful)
*   **Refined-Form Meditative States:** 15 (5 Wholesome + 5 Resultant + 5 Functional)
*   **Formless Meditative States:** 12 (4 Wholesome + 4 Resultant + 4 Functional)
*   **Transcendent States:** 40 (20 Wholesome Paths + 20 Resultant Fruitions)

**Total: 121 States of Consciousness.**

This systematic structure completely maps the range of conscious experience, from the unwholesome impulses of the sensory mind to the progressive stages of meditative absorption and final transcendent liberation.

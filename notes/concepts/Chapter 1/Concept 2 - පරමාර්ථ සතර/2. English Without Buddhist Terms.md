When the world of living beings and external objects is analyzed to its finest, most fundamental level, the irreducible realities that remain are the **ultimate realities**. These are not solid material substances like stones or lumps of clay, but functional dynamic forces, energies, and bare processes. Because they are dynamic forces rather than shaped matter, they possess no spatial configurations or dimensions such as being long, short, small, large, round, or square. All existence and experience are categorized into four ultimate realities: **Consciousness**, **Mental Factors**, **Physical Phenomena**, and the **Unconditioned Reality**.

---

### 1. Consciousness (The Act of Pure Knowing)
Consciousness is the fundamental capacity of knowing or the bare act of apprehending an object. It arises in dependence upon the five physical sense faculties (the eye, ear, nose, tongue, and body) and the internal mental faculty. When awareness arises in dependence on the eye, it is called seeing; through the ear, hearing; through the nose, smelling; through the tongue, tasting; through the body, tactile sensing; and through the mind, thinking, ideation, and the arising of thoughts. The data or phenomenon that is cognized is called the object. 

Consciousness never arises without an object. Just as striking a drum produces distinct sounds with each strike, distinct instances of consciousness arise whenever objects impinge upon the sense faculties. Every act of knowing is an active process; therefore, every moment of consciousness that arises ceases immediately in that very instant. Consciousness is simply this bare act of knowing.

---

### 2. Mental Factors (Concurrent Mental Activities)
Whenever an act of consciousness arises to cognize an object, other distinct mental activities arise mixed with that act of knowing. These are the mental factors, which number fifty-two distinct types. Mental factors possess four defining characteristics in relation to consciousness:
1. They arise together with consciousness at the exact same moment.
2. They cease together with consciousness at the exact same moment.
3. They take the exact same object as consciousness.
4. They rely on the exact same physical base as consciousness.

Mental factors never arise separated from consciousness, as they lack the capacity to arise independently. Similarly, consciousness cannot arise entirely devoid of mental factors. However, consciousness does not require all fifty-two mental factors at once; a single moment of consciousness arises with a specific combination of mental factors depending on the nature of that mental event.

---

### 3. Physical Phenomena (The Material Aspect of Reality)
Physical reality comprises the basic constitutive elements of living bodies and external objects. It is distinct from consciousness and mental factors. There are twenty-eight physical phenomena, consisting of the **four primary physical elements** (solidity/extension, cohesion/fluidity, heat/cold, and motion/pressure) and the **derived physical phenomena** that depend upon them.

Out of these twenty-eight physical phenomena, only one—the visible element (color and visible form)—is directly perceptible to the eye; the remaining twenty-seven are non-visible physical phenomena. Physical reality is defined as that which undergoes deformation, disruption, and alteration when encountering opposing physical conditions, such as heat and cold (in contrast to consciousness and mental factors, which are too subtle to undergo physical collision with temperature). Physical phenomena are coarser and grosser than mental phenomena. When vast aggregations of these physical elements combine, they produce the macroscopic appearance of solid entities, such as trees, rocks, mountains, and bodies.

---

### 4. The Unconditioned Reality (The Reality of Ultimate Cessation)
The first three ultimate realities—consciousness, mental factors, and physical phenomena—are **conditioned realities**. They are generated entirely by causal conditions and cannot endure even for a moment after arising, breaking down immediately. Because they are produced by conditions and subject to change, they are impermanent and subject to birth, aging, illness, and death.

In contrast, the **Unconditioned Reality** is not generated, produced, or compounded by any causes or conditions; therefore, it is unconditioned. Because it is completely detached from the binding entanglement of craving and free from the fires of mental defilements, birth, aging, and death, it is a state of tranquil, peaceful, and ultimate well-being. It is not non-existence or a mere concept, but an objectively existing, profoundly subtle element. Being far subtler and deeper than conditioned mental realities, it is directly realized through the cultivation of transcendent path-knowledge, serving as the ultimate release from all existential suffering.

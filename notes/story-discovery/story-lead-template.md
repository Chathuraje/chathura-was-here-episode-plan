# Story Lead Template

Copy this template once for each possible real story found during research. Save completed cards as:

`notes/story-discovery/leads/<Lead ID> - <short plain description>.md`

Example filename pattern (no real lead implied): `SL-SQ05-001 - Family decision about inherited land.md`.

**Lead ID format:** `SL-<shortlist question>-<three-digit number>`, for example `SL-SQ05-001`. IDs are permanent and never reused. The number counts leads opened under that question.

**Every new lead starts as `unverified`.** A lead is not a story until research has verified it, the participants have agreed, and a separate selection decision has been made. Nothing in a lead card is screenplay material.

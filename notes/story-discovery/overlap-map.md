# Overlap Map

This file groups accepted idea cards that look similar and says how research should treat them. It is an editorial layer only. **No card has been merged, deleted, renamed or edited.** Existing merge decisions in the idea bank stay as they are; their aliases are listed below for traceability.

## Kinds of overlap used here

| Kind | Meaning | How research treats it |
|---|---|---|
| **True duplicate** | Same mechanism and same question. | The bank's merge process already handled these (14 aliases). No new true duplicates were found among the 148 accepted cards. |
| **Same mechanism, different setting** | One process described through different work, senses or places. | Research together under a lead card. Keep each card's limits. |
| **Broad and narrow** | One card states a general process; another applies it to a sensitive or specific case. | Use the broad card as lead for general research. Use the narrow card only where its setting is the subject. |
| **Different mechanisms, similar behaviour** | The same outward behaviour could come from different inner processes. | Keep separate. A researcher must not assume which mechanism is at work. |
| **Shared theme, different question** | Cards meet on a topic but ask different things. | Keep separate. They may be useful as secondary lenses for each other. |

A **lead card** is the recommended entry point when a researcher starts from a group. It is not the "best" card. Leads were chosen for openness, ethical safety and the chance of finding a real situation. Supporting IDs must always be carried with the lead.

## Groups in detail

### G01 — Unseen in familiar surroundings
**Lead:** C009-I01. **Supporting:** C040-I01 (alias C045-I01).
**Research together.** Both ask what a person passes daily without taking in. A place-based journey can test both.
**Keep the distinction.** C009-I01 says attention follows what someone is already inclined to look for. C040-I01 says it follows what is large, near or well lit. If a researcher finds an overlooked change, they must not assume which applies. A person may miss a drying stream because it is faint, or because they were never interested in it.

### G02 — Trained attention in skilled work
**Lead:** C013-I01. **Supporting:** C101-I02 (listening), C065-I01 (touch), C094-I02 (a fixed working point). **Related:** C094-I01.
**Research together.** All four describe attention trained on one object over time, through different senses. One skilled practitioner may show several.
**Keep the distinction.** C065-I01 adds that expectation shapes what the hand feels, which can mislead. C094-I02 concerns staying at one point rather than following movement. C094-I01 is about watching without steering and belongs to a different question.

### G03 — Hurry and divided attention
**Lead:** C008-I01. **Supporting:** C003-I01.
**Research together.** Both concern busy periods.
**Keep the distinction.** C003-I01 is a broad claim about attention switching between single objects. C008-I01 is narrower and more human: hurry flattens what is experienced. Research should start from the experience, not from the switching model.

### G04 — First impressions and recognition
**Lead:** C046-I01. **Supporting:** C034-I01 (aliases C010-I01, C042-I01), C036-I01. **Related:** C020-I01, C043-I01, C084-I01.
**Research together.** All three supporting and lead cards say that knowing what something is comes after first contact and relies on familiar marks. C046-I01 leads because it asks about the feeling that follows recognition, which people can report.
**Keep separate.**
- C020-I01 is *recognition versus understanding*: knowing signs without knowing causes. It is its own research question about traditional knowledge.
- C043-I01 is about the quality of attention in the moment of settling, and carries doctrinal detail about one moment.
- C084-I01 concerns labels fixed on people over time, which has social consequences the others lack.

### G05 — Disgust and the beholder
**Lead:** C009-I02. **Supporting:** C090-I01. **Related:** C093-I02 (Do not advance).
**Research together.** C009-I02 and C090-I01 both concern responses to materials others avoid changing with time and frame.
**Keep the distinction.** C009-I02 is a response that follows contact and differs between people. C090-I01 is a deliberately chosen frame. C093-I02 concerns disgust following a believed origin; it is not advanced because it reverses the source's intent and its directions touch purity beliefs.

### G06 — What holds attention
**Lead:** C033-I01. **Related:** C020-I03, C048-I01, C082-I01.
**Keep separate.** These cards share the theme of attention that will not let go, but ask different questions:
- C033-I01: what sustains a years-long focus, compared with what started it.
- C020-I03: agitation while waiting on something unresolved.
- C048-I01: a single brief contact that keeps echoing.
- C082-I01: looking at one's own feeling in a way that feeds it.
Treating these as one "obsession" idea would lose the difference between devotion, waiting, echo and self-feeding.

### G07 — Old pulls returning
**Lead:** C074-I01. **Supporting:** C013-I02.
**Research together.** Both describe an urge that returns after a long absence. The batch report noted the possibility of merging.
**Keep the distinction.** C074-I01 asks what triggers a dormant tendency and whether it becomes an act. C013-I02 asks what had been holding it away, such as a routine or a practice. A researcher needs both questions, because the answer may be the trigger, the loss of the routine, or neither.

### G08 — Satisfaction that does not close the want
**Lead:** C073-I02. **Supporting:** C081-I02. **Related:** C080-I01.
**Research together.** C073-I02 and C081-I02 both describe wanting that continues after enjoyment.
**Keep the distinction.** In C073-I02 wanting returns because satisfaction fades. In C081-I02 dwelling on the pleasure itself strengthens the pull. C080-I01 is kept separate because the wanting comes from pain, not pleasure. The batch report asked whether C080-I01 and C081-I02 should merge; this pass recommends against it.

### G09 — Hardship and what we come to want
**Lead:** C080-I01. **Supporting:** C077-I03. **Related:** C073-I01, C081-I01.
**Research together.** C080-I01 and C077-I03 meet naturally in families that moved from scarcity to plenty, where both what people want and what feels like a treat have changed.
**Keep separate.** C073-I01 concerns what people give up for relief. C081-I01 concerns relief that deepens the trouble. Both involve debt, illness or addiction and need their own ethical handling.

### G10 — Holding, possessing and releasing
**Lead:** C067-I01. **Supporting:** C100-I01. **Related:** C080-I02, C021-I02, C026-I01.
**Research together.** C067-I01 and C100-I01 both concern the grip on land, houses or heirlooms loosening: by giving up the wanting, or by recognising that the thing was not one's own.
**Keep separate.**
- *Love versus possession* and *wanting to make versus needing to own*: C021-I02 must stay separate.
- C080-I02 describes the grip tightening after obtaining, which is the reverse movement.
- C026-I01 concerns resisting others sharing, which is closer to resentment than possession.

### G11 — Conditions that make acting easy or hard
**Lead:** C003-I02. **Supporting:** C069-I01, C066-I01. **Related:** C006-I03.
**Research together.** Fatigue, practice, heat, food and bodily readiness can be observed in the same working lives.
**Keep the distinction.** C003-I02 is broad (rest and practice). C069-I01 adds mind, food and weather together. C066-I01 narrows to the step from intention to movement, which needs care in illness and disability settings. C006-I03 is kept separate: the push there comes from another person's voice or one's own persuasion, not from bodily condition. This preserves the difference between *habit and condition* and *deliberate choice*.

### G12 — Why doing good can feel hard
**Lead:** C023-I02. **Supporting:** C021-I03. **Related:** C030-I01.
**Research together.** Both concern the effort of giving or keeping commitments.
**Keep the distinction.** C023-I02 attributes difficulty to lack of practice, which people can describe and change. C021-I03 attributes it to a doctrinal claim that the untrained mind leans toward harm, which must not be presented as fact; hence C023-I02 leads. C030-I01 concerns good done without pleasant feeling. That is a separate question: *pleasant feeling versus beneficial action*.

### G13 — Balancing effort and leaving alone
**Lead:** C076-I02. **Supporting:** C087-I01 (alias C099-I01). **Related:** C013-I03 (alias C033-I02), C096-I01, C094-I01, C086-I03.
**Research together.** C076-I02 (alternate pushing, cooling and watching) and C087-I01 (over-effort just before a goal) are broad and narrow versions of one balancing process.
**Keep separate.**
- C013-I03 matches a remedy to a kind of inner obstacle.
- C096-I01 separates general knowledge of what helps from judging today's case.
- C094-I01 concerns observing without steering.
- C086-I03 fits surroundings to individuals and rests on temperament typology.

### G14 — Consolidation, conditions and readiness
**Lead:** C088-I03 (alias C099-I02). **Supporting:** C087-I03. **Related:** C089-I01.
**Research together.** C088-I03 (making a stage reliable before advancing) and C087-I03 (noting and recreating the conditions of success) meet in apprenticeships and training.
**Keep separate.** C089-I01, speed of access under sudden threat for others' sake, is a different achievement. The batch report raised a possible merge with C088-I03; this pass recommends keeping them apart, and C089-I01 is shortlisted on its own.

### G15 — Judging a long path
**Lead:** C056-I03. **Supporting:** C016-I01. **Related:** C088-I01, C100-I02.
**Research together.** The batch report identified these as opposite errors on one path: taking a striking experience for arrival (C016-I01) and taking missing results as proof the path is false (C056-I03). A researcher should hold both possibilities open.
**Keep separate.** C088-I01 concerns the limits of an achievement becoming visible after mastery. C100-I02 concerns taking honest stock after a real change.

### G16 — Struggle is not incapacity
**Lead:** C090-I02. **Supporting:** C085-I01.
**Research together.** Both separate how hard learning feels from what a person can understand.
**Keep the distinction.** C085-I01 is a broad claim about difficulty and speed. C090-I02 adds the social verdict "cannot learn" and a change of method. It leads because it names what research can observe: a label and what followed it.

### G17 — Experience, words and testimony
**Lead:** C073-I03. **Supporting:** C093-I01. **Related:** C091-I01, C020-I01.
**Research together.** Knowledge passed on by telling and by recited steps, and what only experience supplies.
**Keep separate.** C091-I01 concerns remembering a person's qualities in one's own words. C020-I01 concerns recognition without causal understanding.

### G18 — Teaching at a threshold
**Related:** C094-I03, C096-I02.
**Keep separate, research alongside.** C094-I03 concerns a teacher's verdict stopping a learner. C096-I02 concerns shaping an explanation to a specific confusion. Both are seen from the teacher's side, but one is about judgement and the other about explanation.

### G19 — Capacities without moral direction
**Lead:** C075-I01 (aliases C011-I01, C019-I01). **Related:** C004-I01.
**Keep separate.** C075-I01 concerns focus, effort and ingenuity serving a harmful aim. C004-I01 concerns whether a harm registers as harm at all to someone with full knowledge. The first is about direction; the second is about visibility.

### G20 — How harm is done by people who hold values
**Related:** C006-I01, C006-I02, C026-I02.
**Keep separate; do not assume which applies.** The same outward act can come from three mechanisms:
- acting while knowing the cost (C006-I01, desire despite understanding);
- a belief that makes the act seem fair (C006-I02);
- shame and caution absent in the moment (C026-I02).
The batch report asked whether C006-I01 and C026-I02 conflict. Read as a pair, they distinguish *knowing in general* from *seeing in the moment*. This preserves *understanding consequences versus acting on that understanding*. None of these may be used to explain a real person's act.

### G21 — Reasons for restraint
**Lead:** C023-I01. **Supporting:** C091-I02, C024-I01. **Related:** C024-I02, C097-I03, C027-I01.
**Research together.** The lead contrasts restraint from self-respect with restraint from reputation. C091-I02 adds an imagined admired witness. C024-I01 adds the contrast between the moment and a prior promise. One person's account may involve all three.
**Keep separate.**
- C024-I02: restraint when the wrong would pay for a household; livelihood changes the stakes.
- C097-I03: conduct that ends once its reward arrives.
- C027-I01: declining a specific wrong as separate from giving. This preserves *restraint versus indifference*.

### G22 — Comparison inside generosity
**Lead:** C074-I03 (alias C012-I01). **Supporting:** C011-I03. **Related:** C082-I03, C005-I01.
**Research together.** Visible communal giving where comparison may enter.
**Keep the distinction.** C074-I03 is comparison as the starting motive. C011-I03 is envy or pride arising during a long act. C082-I03 is an established virtue becoming a platform for superiority after the fact. C005-I01 is the general point that the same act can have different purposes.

### G23 — Another person's good, doubted or resented
**Lead:** C007-I02 (alias C028-I02). **Supporting:** C022-I02. **Related:** C023-I03, C097-I01.
**Research together.** Reactions to a neighbour's or relative's visible success or generosity.
**Keep separate.** C023-I03 is the mirror of C022-I02: performing a goodness one lacks, rather than doubting a goodness another has. C097-I01 concerns modesty as a form of asking. Both carry high defamation risk. The lead is Tier B, not shortlisted, because of the risk of labelling people envious.

### G24 — Long disputes and carried resentment
**Lead:** C058-I02 (shortlisted). **Supporting:** C007-I01, C095-I02. **Related:** C033-I01, C095-I01.
**Research together.** One long dispute over land, water or inheritance can show wanting becoming hostility toward an obstacle (C007-I01), irritation hardening into a held wish (C058-I02), and a grudge costing the person who holds it (C095-I02).
**Keep separate.**
- C033-I01 asks what sustains any years-long focus, including care.
- C095-I01 concerns deliberate, gradual goodwill and reconciliation. It must never become an expectation placed on people in a dispute.

### G25 — Love and letting go
**Lead:** C005-I02 (shortlisted). **Supporting:** C095-I03.
**Research together.** Parents and carers at the point where a child or person they care for becomes independent.
**Keep the distinction.** C005-I02 holds care and holding on together. C095-I03 describes stepping back as a form of care. This preserves *love versus possession* and *restraint versus indifference*. C095-I03 also carries an unresolved fidelity note (see processing report).

### G26 — Pain others cannot see
**Lead:** C032-I02. **Supporting:** C047-I02. **Related:** C077-I02.
**Research together.** Hidden suffering and how others come to know it.
**Keep the distinction.** C032-I02 is known by asking. C047-I02 is inferred and may be wrong. C077-I02 concerns people in one place whose experiences are far apart. The held card C032-I03 (*Pain in the body versus distress in the mind*) is not used. The distinction between *physical pain and mental distress* must still be respected: C032-I02 includes both and must not collapse them.

### G27 — Feeling and the goodness of care
**Related:** C030-I01, C024-I03, C030-I02.
**Keep separate.** C030-I01 separates feeling glad from the goodness of helping. C024-I03 separates being distressed from steady compassion. C030-I02 concerns compassion versus gladness for another. Merging them would collapse *pleasant feeling versus beneficial action*.

### G28 — Forms of grief

**Research together only with safeguarding.** Both describe grief's inner shape.
**Keep the distinction.** C077-I01 describes forms (burning, outpouring, depletion) without judging them. C007-I03 frames grief as resistance to what happened, from a doctrinal classification of grief as unwholesome. C077-I01 leads because it carries less risk of judging mourners.

### G29 — One cause for many
**Lead:** C080-I03. **Related:** C060-I01.
**Broad and narrow.** C080-I03 concerns choosing one cause for any event. C060-I01 applies this to an early death. For general research about how communities explain events, use C080-I03. Use C060-I01 only when the subject is a death, and only with safeguarding.

### G30 — What returns near the end

### G31 — Reflecting on death

# C086-I01 — When a good trait resembles a harmful one

**Status:** Accepted for research as a limited analogy. This is a conceptual interpretation. No real story has been verified.

## Core idea

Some helpful dispositions share their shape with harmful ones and differ mainly in direction. Devotion clings the way craving does, and discernment finds fault and keeps its distance the way hostility does.

## Human mechanism and tension

Both sources pair temperaments that look alike. Faith is "smooth" as lust is; faith searches for virtue as lust searches for pleasing things; faith will not let go of what helps, as lust will not let go of what harms. Intelligence and hatred both lack clinging affection. Intelligence looks for faults that are really there, while hatred looks for faults that are not; intelligence pushes away what is unreliable, hatred pushes away beings. My interpretation, beyond the text, is that this creates two confusions. From outside, a devoted person and a grasping one, or a sharp critic and a resentful one, can look alike. From inside, a person may take their hostility for clear judgement, or their attachment for faithfulness. The difference the source names is not intensity but whether the fault or virtue pursued is real.

## Possible everyday expression

Hypothetical research direction: roles where devotion or critical judgement is part of the work, such as temple and church volunteers, quality inspectors, reviewers or editors, asked how they tell loyalty from possessiveness, or rigour from ill will, in themselves. No person, event or outcome has been established.

## Open human question

How can someone tell whether the flaw they keep finding is really there?

## Limits and alternatives

These pairings belong to a doctrinal typology used to assign meditation subjects, not to a validated model of personality. Nobody should be labelled by it. A person may be both devoted and grasping, or both perceptive and hostile, at once. Apparent fault-finding may also reflect professional standards, past harm or justified grievance, which are not hatred.

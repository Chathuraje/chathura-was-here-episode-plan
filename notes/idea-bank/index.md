# Idea Bank — Index

Every idea card that came out of the concept notes. An ID such as `C006-I01` is a permanent source reference (Concept 6, idea 1). It is not an episode number. Accepted means the interpretation passed conceptual review. It does not mean a real story has been found or verified. See [instructions/concept-to-idea.md](../../instructions/concept-to-idea.md) for the method and [concept-register.md](concept-register.md) for per-concept coverage.

**Totals:** 172 cards: 148 accepted for research, 10 held, 14 merged. Built from snapshot `ef70024`, instruction version `concept-to-idea v1 (2026-09-17)`.

## How to use this bank

1. Start with cards marked **accepted for research**. **Held** cards need a fidelity or safety decision before use. **Merged** cards are aliases: follow `merged into` to the main card, which now includes the merged card's source trace.
2. Each card's *Possible everyday expression* is a hypothetical research direction, not a story. A research agent must find and verify real people, places and situations first. Only a researched and selected story goes on to screenplay development.
3. Keep each card's *Limits and alternatives* attached when handing it on. They say what the idea does **not** establish.
4. Idea IDs are source references. They do not map to episode numbers, and a card can support several films or none.

## Research safeguards for the whole bank

The batch reviews found source passages that must never become premises, research leads or on-screen claims about real people:

- **Karma as an explanation for someone's circumstances.** Several passages attribute disability (blindness, deafness), intersex bodies, poverty, "low birth", servitude, appearance or twins' differences to past action (for example C009, C012, C034, C049, C050, C053, C071, C075, C080, C089, C095, C101). No card uses these, and research must not either.
- **Group-stigmatising passages.** Examples are caste etymologies (C052), gendered claims (C075, C098), a racial example (C084), and polemic against other religions or modern science (C074, C091). Do not carry them.
- **Supernatural and cosmological claims.** Rebirth, realms, powers, and miracle stories involving named Sri Lankan elders (C090) are doctrinal context only. They are never documentary leads.
- **Death, grief and self-harm.** C060, C077 and C092 mention suicide or self-harm, so any research on death and grief cards needs safeguarding practice and consent protocols.
- **Typing people.** Do not classify real people by temperament signs (C086), infer inner states or motives from behaviour, or label anyone greedy, hateful, shameless or deluded.
- **Doctrine is not science.** Heart-base physiology, heart-blood colour and mind-moment counts are traditional doctrine, not neuroscience.

Suspected source-text errors and held questions are recorded in [batch-reports/](batch-reports/) for human review against the printed books. No source file was changed.

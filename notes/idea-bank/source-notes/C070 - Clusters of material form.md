# C070 — Clusters of material form

## Central meaning

Material forms never arise singly. They arise, persist and cease in clusters (කලාප) of at least eight inseparable forms, sharing four features: arising together, ceasing together, a common support, and co-existence. There are 21 clusters: by size, one octad, three nonads, nine decads, one undecad, two dodecads and one tridecad; by cause, nine kamma-born, six mind-born, four temperature-born and two nutriment-born. Only the pure octad and the sound nonad occur outside living bodies. Space and the four characteristic forms are not cluster members.

## Essential distinctions

- **Interpenetration.** Margaya lines 5–7: within a cluster, individual forms have no boundary; each is as if dissolved in, or swallowing, the others. The simile of four flours (rice, wheat, kurakkan, barley) kneaded into a ball illustrates it, with the explicit caveat that no simile fits, because flour particles remain separate while cluster forms are a gapless mixture.
- **Variety from proportion.** Margaya line 52: soil, stone, fire, water, wind, flowers, leaves, bark, roots and most of the body are pure octads. Their endless variety in colour, shape, taste, hardness and weight comes from differences in which great elements predominate.
- **Parallel with mind.** Pradeepika line 18: the four features of a cluster mirror the four features by which consciousness and its mental factors are conjoined.

## Agreement, complementary detail and disagreement

The sources agree on the 21 clusters and their causes. Pradeepika adds the parallel with mental conjunction and the mutual support of the four great elements (line 22). Margaya adds the flour simile, the "no boundary" claim and the proportion explanation. Small difference in the four features: Pradeepika's fourth feature reads "existing together with one base such as the eye" (line 18), while Margaya lists "a common support" and "existing together" (line 9). This looks like different phrasing rather than a doctrinal disagreement.

Suspected text errors: Pradeepika line 71 "ලහුතා දෙකා දසක" is presumably ලහුතාදේකාදසක. Pradeepika line 22 calls the four derived inseparables a "වර්ණ චතුෂ්කය", an unusual label. Margaya line 32 "චිත්තක කලාප" is presumably චිත්තජ කලාප.

## What cannot safely be carried into everyday language

The cluster theory is a doctrinal atomism. Its claim that a cluster is far smaller than an atom (Margaya line 7) must not be equated with particle physics or chemistry, and the element-proportion account must not be presented as material science.

## Human meaning paragraph

The concept is a technical theory of matter. The flour-ball image, of qualities so mixed that none can be located apart from the others, is vivid, but the source uses it only for material forms and itself says the simile fails. Carrying it to people, relationships or communities would be my invention, not a sourced mechanism.

# C079 — The Pali text of dependent origination

## Sources and coverage

- `Abhidhammattha Pradeepika.md`: read in full (lines 1–36). Frontmatter: Book 3, chapter 8, PDF 27 (printed 16). Page marker at line 21 (PDF 27 / printed 16). The Pali formula is at lines 23–34; line 36 labels it "the Pali text of the teaching of dependent origination".
- `Abhidharma Margaya.md`: read in full (lines 1–15). Heading `# 2. පටිච්ච සමුත්පාද පාළිය`. Numbered clauses 1–11 (lines 3–13) and the concluding sentence (line 15). No page locators.

## Central meaning

Both files give only the Pali formula of dependent origination, in eleven clauses covering twelve factors: with ignorance as condition, formations; formations → consciousness; consciousness → mind-and-matter; mind-and-matter → six sense bases; six sense bases → contact; contact → feeling; feeling → craving; craving → clinging; clinging → becoming; becoming → birth; birth → ageing, death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, grief and despair. Conclusion: "thus is the arising of this whole mass of suffering." Margaya (C078 line 13) says the learner should memorise this text first.

## Essential distinctions

None are drawn in these files. The factor definitions and explanations are in C080.

## Agreement, complementary detail and disagreement

The two texts agree word for word apart from spelling (Pradeepika `ඵස්ස පච්චයා වෙදනා`, `භවො`, `එවමෙතස්ස`; Margaya `වේදනා`, `භවෝ`, `එවමේතස්ස`) and layout (Margaya numbers the clauses). Pradeepika's excerpt note (line 12) says "eleven factors" (අඞ්ග එකොළොසේ); this counts clauses, whereas C080 speaks of twelve factors (අඞ්ග දොළොස). Not a real conflict.

## What cannot safely be carried into everyday language

The formula in bare form is a doctrinal statement spanning past, present and future lives (see C080). Without the commentary, any human reading would be invented rather than sourced.

## Human meaning paragraph

There is no explanation in these files to support a human mechanism. Any idea about feeling, wanting or holding on belongs to C080, where the text explains the links.

# C036 — Classification of objects of consciousness

## Sources and coverage

- `Abhidhammattha Pradeepika.md`: read in full (lines 1–380). Frontmatter: Book 1, chapter 3, PDF 276–295 (printed 266–285). Page markers from line 39 (PDF 277 / printed 267) to line 378 (PDF 295 / printed 285). The extract begins mid-page on PDF 276 (line 21).
- `Abhidharma Margaya.md`: read in full (lines 1–53). Heading `# 5. ආරම්මණ සංග්‍ර‍හය`, parts I (lines 3–27) and II (lines 29–53). No page locators.

## Central meaning

Every mind-state needs an object (ආරම්මණ / ආලම්බණ), "something to be thought of" (Pradeepika line 27). There is nothing the mind cannot take as object: worldly and supramundane, ultimate and conventional, present, past, future and time-free (line 29). Objects are classified six ways by door (visible, sound, smell, taste, touch, mental object), by time, and by which classes of mind can take which kinds of object (fixed/unfixed objects: sense-sphere, exalted, supramundane, concept).

## Essential distinctions

- **Sense doors take only present objects.** Eye-, ear-, nose-, tongue- and body-door minds take only a present object; past and future sights or sounds do not reach them (Pradeepika lines 43, 123–153; Margaya line 23). The mind door takes objects of all three times plus time-free nirvana and concepts (Pradeepika line 157; Margaya lines 21, 25).
- **What the eye meets is a blend.** The eye does not meet the ultimate colour of each material group, only the mixed colour of a mass; people then recognise it "by marks seen and grown used to before" and say "I saw such-and-such" (Pradeepika line 41). Sounds are likewise identified as drum, lute, human voice or thunder because they were heard before (line 71). Margaya line 9: only colour is seen, not things.
- **Three senses of "present"** (Pradeepika lines 45–59): momentary (17 mind-moments), continuity (the time of one eye-door process plus three mind-door processes, "the time taken to recognise an object", line 55) and a lifetime.
- **A present object reaches the mind door only after the five-door process** (Pradeepika line 121).
- **Objects of the process-free minds** (rebirth-linking, life-continuum, death): kamma, kamma sign, destination sign seen near death (Pradeepika lines 159–205; Margaya line 27). Doctrinal rebirth mechanics.
- **Capacity limits.** Sense-sphere wholesome states cannot take the arahant path and fruit as object "because they are led by craving" (Pradeepika line 368); states without knowledge cannot take supramundane objects (line 370).

## Agreement, complementary detail and disagreement

The sources agree on the six objects, the present-only rule for the five doors, the all-times range of the mind door, and the object counts (Margaya line 53 matches the Pradeepika tables at lines 230–366). Pradeepika adds the material explanation of colour, sound, smell, taste and touch (lines 33–95), the three kinds of present, the death-proximate objects and reasons for the capacity limits. Margaya adds that direct-knowledge (අභිඥා) states alone perform powers (line 45) and advises asking teachers about time-free objects (line 21). No genuine disagreement found.

## What cannot safely be carried into everyday language

The 17-mind-moment lifespan, the material-group (කලාප) physics of sound and smell, the death-proximate signs and rebirth objects, the planes of existence and the powers of the fifth jhāna are doctrinal and must not be presented as science or used as premises. "Present" in the continuity sense is a doctrinal unit, not a measured reaction time.

## Human meaning paragraph

The source separates two ways the mind meets the world. The senses meet only what is happening now, and even then only a raw blend of colour or sound; what we call "seeing a thing" already includes recognition built from what was seen before, and takes further mental work. Thought, by contrast, can take anything at any time: the past, the future, or ideas that belong to no time at all. The everyday reading (interpretation) is that recognition leans on memory, and that a person's senses and thoughts can be anchored in different times.

## Idea decisions

- **C036-I01 — Seeing through the marks we already know.** Accepted as limited analogy. Based on Pradeepika lines 41, 55, 71 and Margaya line 9.
- **C036-I02 — Senses stay in the present while thought travels.** Accepted as limited analogy. Based on the present-only rule for the five doors versus the all-times mind door, and the lag at line 121.
- Rejected candidate: *What returns to the mind near death* (kamma sign: implements of past deeds appearing to the dying, lines 163–187). The passage exists to explain rebirth-linking; turning it into an idea about end-of-life memory would make rebirth mechanics the premise. May be revisited only if a later concept treats dying without rebirth dependence.
- Rejected candidate: *Wanting blocks the highest view* (line 368). One sentence with no explanation; about supramundane objects only.
- Rejected: the fixed/unfixed object tables and material physics — classification without a human mechanism.

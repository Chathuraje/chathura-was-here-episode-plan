## Held questions

1. **Rejoicing needs the story (C059, Pradeepika line 249).** Rejoicing in another's good deed is said to be incomplete if the person is only told "rejoice" without hearing how the deed was done. A human reading (shared gladness depends on knowing the details) is plausible, but the passage sits inside the doctrine of merit transfer to relatives and deities (lines 239–245). No card was issued. A reviewer could decide whether a limited-analogy card is justified.
2. **Kindness learned by imitation (C059, Pradeepika lines 225, 235).** This supports the same mechanism as existing C011-I02 (Doing good by habit without asking why). No card was issued, to avoid duplication. The coordinator may wish to add these lines to C011-I02 as supporting evidence from a second concept.
3. **Unexpressed ill will is "strong" (C058, Pradeepika line 220).** Used in C058-I02 only as doctrinal context. The source gives no reason why mental-only greed and hatred are stronger than when expressed in body or speech.
4. **Auspicious times as wrong view (C058, Pradeepika lines 413–417).** Deliberately not used. Any future use must not portray Sri Lankan astrological or ritual practice as error.

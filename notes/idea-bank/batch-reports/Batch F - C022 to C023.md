## Held questions

1. **Lineage as a reason for restraint and as conceit.** Pradeepika C023 line 73 counts considering one's high lineage (කුලගෝත්‍ර) as support for shame at wrong. Pradeepika C022 line 107 counts lineage pride (ජාති මානය) as conceit. The source does not reconcile them. Any research using C023-I01 must not treat caste or lineage reasoning as admirable.
2. **"Equal" conceit and egalitarian belief.** Pradeepika C022 line 109 describes equal-conceit partly through the view that distinguishing high, low, rich and poor is unjust. This is doctrinally loaded and was deliberately not carried into any card. Should a reviewer confirm the reading of this passage?
3. **Remorse vs repair.** C022-I01 relies on the source's claim that burning regret is not virtuous. Its companion claim, that remorse can make a light wrong hell-bound (line 143), is doctrinal. Reviewers should check that the card does not read as advice against apology or accountability.
4. **Rejected but reusable candidates:** drowsiness when doing things unwillingly (C022 Pradeepika line 175); stinginess that blocks even others' giving (C022 line 133); giving to a disliked recipient (C023 lines 93, 99–103). They may support future cards on generosity or effort.

## Possible overlaps

- **C022-I02 (explaining away someone else's goodness) and C023-I03 (speaking against what you quietly do)** are mirror images: suspecting false virtue in others versus performing it oneself. They are kept separate because the mechanisms differ.
- **C023-I02 (doing good feels awkward without practice) and C006-I03 (needing a push to do what you already want)** both concern hesitation before acting. C006-I03 is about prompting within greed, and C023-I02 is about practice and familiarity in good action.
- **C022-I01 (regret keeps the wrong alive) and C023-I02** are linked by the source's claim that regret weakens the will to do good.
- **C023-I01 (self-respect or reputation) and C022-I03 (measuring yourself too low)** are both about self-measurement and standing. Any future cards built on C022 shamelessness and fearlessness (Pradeepika lines 53–69) would duplicate C023-I01 from the negative side and should be merged into it.
- C022 wrong view (දිට්ඨිය) overlaps existing **C006-I02 (the belief that makes taking seem fair)**, so no card was made. Batches covering other mental-factor concepts may encounter shame/fear of wrong, non-greed or envy again, including chapter 2 concepts on the remaining beautiful factors and the factor combinations.

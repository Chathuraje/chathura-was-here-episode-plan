## Held questions

1. **Some suffering is known only by asking** (Pradeepika 2444–2448, PDF 506–507 / printed 495–496; 450–458, PDF 375–376 / printed 364–365). Hidden pains such as earache, toothache and the inner burning of lust or hatred must be learnt by asking. What someone is thinking can only be known by asking. A monk's glance at a woman is suspected as lust, but he explains she resembled his younger sister. This is a good mechanism, not carded because it overlaps C047-I02 and C005-I01. Should C077 be added as a supporting concept to C047-I02?
2. **Old age and the loss of regard** (2588–2590, PDF 516 / printed 505). Children no longer honour and serve an aged parent as before, causing mental pain alongside physical weakness and loss of memory and effort. This is a strong tension, but it rests on one clause.
3. **Posture changes hide constant discomfort** (1391, 1397, PDF 437 / printed 426). This could be a limited analogy about small adjustments masking ongoing strain, but it rests on the doctrinal claim that all formations oppress. Held.
4. **Self-harm and suicide** appear as expressions of despair and dejection (2515, 2692, 2710). Any research drawing on C077-I01 needs safeguarding guidance.
5. **The "middling person" standard** (193). C077-I03 must not be used to rank rich or poor people. Reviewers should confirm the limits wording is sufficient.

## Suspected source text errors

- **Line 1867**: `සැත්තෑ (76)`. The word means "seventy", but the numeral and the arithmetic (89 − 10 − 3 = 76) give seventy-six. The word appears truncated (expected `සැත්තෑසය`).
- **Lines 882–890**: the transcriber's comment notes that the listed cittas that produce no matter in the formless plane add to 38, not the printed `දෙසාළිස්` (42). The four formless functional cittas are omitted. Transcribed unchanged.
- **Lines 2026–2032**: folio printed "446" for 476 (transcriber's note).
- **Lines 2895–2905**: the three functions of right view are numbered "1., 1., 2" (transcriber's note).
- **Lines 2384 vs 2386**: the simile's tree is a සපු (champak) tree at 2384 and a සල් (sal) tree at 2386. This is an internal inconsistency.
- **OCR or font artefacts**: from about line 1740 onward, a stray `ී` often replaces the repaya/rakāransha (for example `ධම්ය` for ධර්මය, `දශීනය` for දර්ශනය, `අථීය` for අර්ථය, `මාගීය` for මාර්ගය), and `ෙවෂ` appears for ද්වේෂ. The meaning is recoverable, and nothing was changed.
- **Counting conventions, not errors**: the dhamma base is 69 items at 1409 (52 mental factors + 16 subtle matter + nibbāna) but 20 at 1865 (3 mental aggregates + 16 + 1).

## Source disagreements recorded (not errors)

- **Purpose of the base and element teachings.** Margaya 49: the bases are for those holding mental functions as self. Pradeepika 3029: they are for those confused about matter. Margaya 60 and Pradeepika 1853: the elements are for those with strong self-notion in consciousness. Pradeepika 3031: they are for those confused about both mind and matter.
- **Formation suffering and change suffering.** Margaya 83 and 85 describe the effort of obtaining and maintaining a human life, and the decay and fear of destruction. Pradeepika 2440–2442 describes pleasant feeling turning to pain, and all formations oppressed by arising and passing.

## Possible overlaps

- **C077-I01** relates to **C007-I03** (*Grief as pushing against what happened*). C007-I03 is about resistance within grief. C077-I01 is about distinct forms of grief (inner, outpouring, depletion). Related, not merged. It also sits near **C024-I03** (distress at another's suffering).
- **C077-I02** relates to **C047-I02** (*Sensing another's pain you cannot see*). C047-I02 is about how another's feeling is sensed. C077-I02 is about closeness defined by likeness of experience rather than place.
- **C077-I03** is loosely related to **C073-I02** (*Getting what you want and wanting it again*). Both touch the resetting of satisfaction, but C073-I02 is about renewed wanting and C077-I03 about the baseline that sets what feels pleasant.
- Rejected candidates would have duplicated **C066-I03** (signs of ageing), **C013-I01** and **C075-I02** (supported concentration, turning an object over) and **C001-I01** (body parts that do not know one another).

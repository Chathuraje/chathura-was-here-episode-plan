## Held questions

1. **C028-I01 (doubt excludes deciding).** Can the structural point that doubt and decision cannot co-occur be carried into everyday indecision? The C008 source note says Pradeepika restricts unwholesome doubt to religious and doctrinal doubt. If it cannot be carried over, consider merging the structural point into C021-I01.
2. **C032-I03 (bodily pain versus mental distress).** Is there a source (C007, C074–C077 or chapter 9) that states the relationship between bodily pain and distress? Can it be presented without implying that people in pain are at fault?
3. **Compassion and neutral feeling.** Pradeepika C031 line 97 (PDF 208 / printed 197) says states with equanimity cannot take happy or suffering beings as object, so compassion and appreciative joy are absent in the fifth absorption. Yet both sources include compassion and appreciative joy in neutral-feeling sense-sphere wholesome and functional states (C030 Pradeepika tables lines 63, 100; C030 Margaya lines 5, 9). Is the rule limited to absorption, or is there an inconsistency?
4. **Doing good without joy.** C011 Margaya ranks joyful good states above neutral ones "in general", while C030 treats them as parallel. C030-I01 notes this. A reviewer should confirm that the card does not understate the ranking.
5. **Sensing before judging (C029).** This was rejected here because the sequence belongs to chapter 4. It appears to be covered already by C009-I02, C010-I01, C034-I01 and C042-I01.

## Held questions

1. **Where does careless or careful attention sit?** Pradeepika (C043 lines 77–83) places it in the determining moment. Margaya (C041 line 7; C042 line 34; C045 line 34) treats determining as judging "as investigated" and ties the variable to impulsion and disposition. C043-I01 avoids pinning the mechanism to a specific moment. A doctrinal reviewer should confirm.
2. **"No kamma" without impulsion** (C045 Pradeepika line 61). This could support an idea about responsibility for what is only half noticed. It was not carded because it is a karmic-result claim. Is a limited analogy acceptable?
3. **Good and bad objects set by ordinary people's judgement** (C045 Margaya line 30). This is a possible idea about social standards of taste versus individual reaction. It was not carded because it serves a doctrinal result classification.
4. **C045-I01 merge.** The merged card keeps a distinct angle: how reliable later recall of half-registered things is ("seen and not seen, as in a dream"). If C040-I01 is researched, that angle should be carried over. Otherwise C045-I01 could be revived as a separate card.

Row (1) shows three ending `භ` where the text says four. Row (5) shows two `වො` where the text says three. Row (6) shows one `වො` where the text says two.

## Held questions

1. **Sorrow measured by attachment** (C080 Pradeepika lines 1603–1605, PDF 121 / printed 110). Grief on separation from what the mind was stuck to ("like a deer struck by a spear") and sorrow when a body taken as "me, mine" changes. A plausible mechanism, but close to C007-I03 and C005-I02, and the text treats sorrow and lament as products of delusion (line 1601). Not carded, to avoid judging mourners. Could be revisited if a later concept offers a gentler framing.
2. **Lamentation as repeatedly naming the dead and their virtues** (Pradeepika lines 268–274, 1583). A clear description of a mourning practice, but no mechanism beyond description. Could serve as supporting context for a future grief card.
3. **Deathbed offerings by relatives** (Pradeepika lines 817–821). Relatives bring flowers, music, incense, honey and cloth to the dying person's senses. Duplicates C062-I01; recommend listing C080 as a supporting concept there if the coordinator wishes.
4. **Rebirth as blind, deaf or neuter for weak wholesome kamma** (Pradeepika line 741) and twins differing "because of kamma" (Margaya line 117). Same caution as Batch S question 1: any future card touching disability, intersex people or twins must not draw on these passages.
5. **"Ordinary people are like madmen / blind fools"** (Pradeepika lines 590, 1525). Sermon rhetoric; research agents should not quote these as characterisations of anyone.

## Possible overlaps

- **C080-I01 — When discomfort turns into wanting comfort** is near C073-I01 (When relief is worth more than pleasure) and C073-I02 (Getting what you want and wanting it again). Mechanisms differ: I01 is pain generating a new want; C073-I01 is valuing release over enjoyment; C073-I02 is satisfaction fading and renewing desire. Listed as related, not merged.
- **C080-I02 — Wanting tightens once the thing is in hand** is near C005-I02 (Love that is also holding on) and C067-I01 (Giving up the wanting, not the thing). I02 is the escalation from hoping to gripping after possession. Related, not merged.
- **C080-I03 — Naming one cause for what many causes made** overlaps C060-I01 (Asking why a life ended too soon), which already contrasts one decisive reason with many causes. I03 is broader (the selection built into any causal statement, and naming a cause's cause), but the coordinator may prefer to merge or hold it. Flagged as the main overlap risk in this batch.

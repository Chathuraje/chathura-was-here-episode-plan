## Held questions

1. **Merit-sharing for the dead** (C051, Margaya line 21). One ghost type is said to live on merit given by relatives. The Sri Lankan practice of giving on behalf of dead relatives may be a strong human theme, but this source only states the doctrine from the ghost's side. Is there a concept that treats merit-sharing (පින් අනුමෝදනාව / පත්තිදාන) directly and could support a card?
2. **C052 origin narrative scope.** Three cards come from one mythic passage (Pradeepika C052 lines 142 and 176–192). The coordinator should confirm that limited analogies from this narrative are acceptable in the bank, given that it is cosmology.
3. **Conceit and appearance** (C052 lines 182–184). The good-looking despising the ill-looking is a clear human pattern, but the text gives it a magical consequence. Should it be held for a later concept on conceit (මානය)?
4. **Stigmatising content** (C053 Margaya lines 9–15; Pradeepika C053 line 48; C052 lines 194–204). These passages attribute disability, mental illness, intersex and variant sex characteristics, servitude, and a varṇa group's name to weak merit or bad conduct. The source notes record them as unsafe to carry. The coordinator may want a bank-wide rule for such passages.

## Possible overlaps

- **C052-I03 and C054.** C054 Margaya line 33 repeats the lifespan-cycle passage. It is merged in as a supporting source, not a separate card. C051 Pradeepika line 142 (the human plane valued for opportunity, not comfort) is context only.
- **C052-I03 and C060-I01 / C066-I03.** These also touch mortality and ageing. The mechanism differs: C052-I03 concerns a community losing restraint as the memory of loss fades across generations, not individual awareness of death.
- **C052-I01 and C026-I01 (Stinginess as resenting that others share).** Both involve sharing. C026-I01 is an individual's resentment; C052-I01 is a collective shift from common use to division. Different mechanisms.
- **C052-I01 and C052-I02.** These are consecutive steps in one narrative, but distinct questions (resource division vs delegated authority). Kept separate and cross-referenced.
- **C053 deferred candidate (near-death objects)** is already covered by C061-I01 (Old deeds feeling new again near death). No card created.
- **C052 rejected candidates** on curiosity-driven craving and appearance-based contempt overlap C006 (desire) and C005-I01 (appearance and motive).

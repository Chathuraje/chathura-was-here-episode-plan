## Held questions

1. **Timescale.** Both C034 cards read a sub-second cognitive process (C034 Pradeepika line 37: billions of mind-moments per finger-snap) as an everyday sequence. Reviewers should confirm the limited-analogy label is enough.
2. **Two-way door (C035).** Margaya (lines 5, 9) says objects enter and the mind goes out to the object; Pradeepika (line 27) says exit is the ceasing of the mind-state. Could “taking something in is also reaching out to it” support a card if a later concept (for example the cognitive-process chapter) develops it? Not carded now.
3. **Registration after impulsion** (C034 Pradeepika line 185, runner simile). Possibly relevant when the cognitive-process concepts are processed; rejected here as too momentary to stretch.
4. **Sensory impairment.** C034 Pradeepika line 47 ties congenital blindness and deafness to weak past wholesome action. Any future idea touching senses or disability must exclude this explicitly.

- C034 Pradeepika line 29: the list reads “සායන, සායන”; the first should be ඝායන (smelling). Also no comma between “දස්සන සවණ”.
- C034 Pradeepika line 109: heading `#### සායන කෘත්‍යය (6)` for smelling should be ඝායන; line 121 (7) correctly uses සායන for tasting. The table at line 207 uses ඝායන correctly.
- C034 Pradeepika line 185: “එකක් පමණක් නුපදනේය” (only one does not arise) — context requires “උපදනේය” (only one arises) when there is no room for two.
- C034 Pradeepika line 175: says the seven impulsion states are “සහෙතුක” (rooted), but the 55 impulsion states include the rootless smile-producing state (හසිතුප්පාද, line 181); likely a generalisation rather than a scanning error.
- C034 Margaya line 49: “සම්ප්‍ර‍ත්‍යේෂණ කෘ කෘත්‍යය” duplicated syllable. Line 31 “ව්‍යවස්තාපන” vs line 53 “ව්‍යවස්ථාපන” spelling variation.

## Held questions

1. **C081, preparation as part of an act** (Pradeepika lines 215–223, PDF 128–129 / printed 117–118). The source separates the intentions of someone preparing a gift for a month or a year from the intention at the moment of handing it over. It also names the wish for a future pleasant rebirth as craving within the act. There is a possible human idea about long preparation versus the moment of giving, but it is entangled with a rebirth premise and overlaps C011-I03, C012-I01 and C074-I03. Held.
2. **C081, no agent yet action matters** (lines 541–545, PDF 146–147 / printed 135–136). The wrong reading of "no controller" becomes the view that action does nothing. Recommend adding this as a supporting reference to C040-I02 rather than a new card.
3. **C081, specific causes versus fatalism** (line 547, PDF 147 / printed 136: milk to curd, and the wrong reading as a doctrine of fixed outcomes). Possible supporting reference for C060-I02.
4. **C084 as supporting evidence for C001-I01.** Margaya line 7 (cloth is only threads) is the same mechanism as C001-I01's table analysis. The coordinator may wish to add C084 as a supporting concept to C001-I01. This agent did not edit C001 files.
5. **C084, "white man" example** (Margaya line 29). Colour is used as the "real quality" in a grammatical example. Any future card must not use this concept to discuss race or skin colour. A standing research note is recommended.
6. **C083, repetition condition (āsevana).** Do not use it as evidence for everyday habit formation. The source restricts it to successive javana moments within one process.

## Suspected source text errors or oddities

- **C083 Pradeepika line 357 (PDF 553 / printed 542).** The six ways clinging conditions the immediately following kamma-becoming are listed as proximity, contiguity, proximity-decisive-support, **අත්ථි** (presence), disappearance and repetition. That group elsewhere has **නත්ථි** (absence) with disappearance (compare lines 51–53 and 375–377). Likely අත්ථි for නත්ථි. Not corrected.
- **C083 Pradeepika line 395 (PDF 554 / printed 543).** It reads "නාමරූප – ප්‍රඥප්ති නාමයට", which appears to say mind-and-matter condition *concept-mind*. Margaya line 25 says concepts, mind and matter all condition *mind* in two ways. The Pradeepika line is probably a punctuation or ordering slip (ප්‍රඥප්ති නාමරූප – නාමයට). Not corrected.
- **C083 Pradeepika line 79.** `(2. කර්ම ප්‍රත්‍යය` has a missing closing parenthesis. Lines 385–387 have two-column numbering (1)(3)(5) / (2)(4) from the print layout.
- **C084 Pradeepika line 27 versus lines 29–41.** The heading says "eight concepts" but seven are listed. This is already flagged in the extract's own comment (line 43).
- **C084 Pradeepika lines 47–71.** Uses **පරමාණු වශයෙන්** ("as atoms") where the sense and Margaya (lines 25–35) require **පරමාර්ථ වශයෙන්** ("ultimately"). This is either a consistent transcription error or the author's idiosyncratic usage. Not corrected.
- **C084 Pradeepika line 67.** `වක්ෂුර්විඥනය` for `චක්ෂුර්විඥානය` (ව/ච confusion).
- **C084, classification disagreement.** Pradeepika puts earth, mountains, rivers and seas under *collection* and chariots under *shape* (lines 29–31). Margaya puts earth, mountains and trees under *shape* and chariots, tables and beds under *collection* (lines 13–15). This is recorded as a genuine disagreement.
- **C084 Margaya line 21.** `දයෙක්` for `දෙයක්`.
- **C081 Pradeepika lines 104, 116, 126 and 136.** `**විධ**` where `ද්විවිධ` (twofold) is expected, apparently a dropped prefix, as the sequence runs one-, two-, threefold.
- **C081 Pradeepika lines 223 and 249.** `භිකාන්තිය` is probably `නිකාන්තිය` (nikanti, longing).
- **C081 Pradeepika, other letter confusions.** Line 58 `මාරෙයි` for `මැරෙයි`. Line 62 `අතීත ධ්වය` for `අතීතාධ්වය`. Line 400 `කකීශ` for `කර්කශ`. There are frequent න/ත confusions (e.g. line 66 `ගින්තෙන්`, line 72 `පමුණුවන්තාවූ`). Meaning is recoverable, and nothing was changed.
- **C081, scope of the result round.** Margaya line 57 includes rebirth-becoming, birth and ageing-death in the result round. Pradeepika line 313 lists only the five present-result factors. This is a difference of scope, not an error.

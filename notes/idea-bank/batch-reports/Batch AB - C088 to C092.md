## Held questions

1. **C090, miracle narratives about named canonical and Sri Lankan elders** (Dhammadinna at Tissamahavihara, Cūḷasamudda, Tissadatta, Mahinda; Book 4 lines 314, 326, 421–423). These must never be treated as historical events or as research leads for "real" miracles. Recommend a register note.
2. **C090-I02 and learning difficulty.** The Cūḷapanthaka story could be misused to romanticise or diagnose learners. The card warns against this. The coordinator may wish to add research guidance on consent and on not labelling people with learning disabilities.
3. **C092, sensitive material.** Book 4 line 67 lists suicide among untimely deaths, and the whole concept invites death reflection. Cards C092-I01 to I03 carry warnings. Research with bereaved, terminally ill or at-risk people needs a safeguarding protocol before any fieldwork.
4. **C088, "near enemy" framing.** Book 4 repeatedly calls the preceding state a near enemy (lines 44, 54, 208, 220, 240). A possible card on "the old habit that stays close to a new one" was not written because the text gives no mechanism beyond the label. It could be revisited with C086 or C076 material.
5. **C092, sluggish versus keen horizon.** The Buddha's quoted saying (lines 187–191) calls four or five mouthfuls "sluggish". The author's summary sentence at the end of line 191 then describes the practice with "four or five mouthfuls". C092-I03 relies on the quoted distinction, not the summary. It should be confirmed against the Pali or the Visuddhimagga.

## Possible overlaps

- **C088-I03 — Mastering a stage at will before moving on** and **C089-I01 — Having a skill but not reaching it in time** (parallel batch) both draw on absorption mastery. I03 concerns consolidating control (enter, hold, exit, review) before advancing. C089-I01 concerns speed of access under threat. Kept separate and listed as related. The coordinator may prefer to merge them if the research agent treats "readiness" as one theme.
- **C088-I01 — A hard-won calm looks coarse once mastered** is near **C016-I01** (mistaking a striking experience for arrival) and **C014-I02** (moving beyond while still hanging from it). Different mechanisms: flaws revealed by familiarity and review; premature sense of arrival; dependence on a surpassed stage.
- **C088-I02 — When delight itself unsettles a quieter contentment** is near **C068-I01** (not every joy turns into laughter) and **C073-I01** (relief versus pleasure). Listed as related.
- **C090-I01 — Seeing the repulsive kindly and the lovely soberly** is near **C013-I03** (answering each obstacle with its opposite). C013-I03 concerns counter-states to hindrances. C090-I01 concerns deliberately reframing disgust and attraction.
- **C090-I02 — Unable to memorise, yet able to understand** is near **C085-I01** (a hard road does not mean slow understanding). Related, not merged: C085-I01 separates difficulty from speed, while C090-I02 concerns one failed method becoming a verdict on the person.
- **C092-I03 — Living as though only a breath remains** is near **C052-I03** (lessons learned in disaster fade in plenty), which concerns how seeing death restrains people and how comfort erodes that restraint. C092-I03 concerns the assumed time horizon.
- **Rejected as duplicates:** C088 snake and enemy similes (C014-I01), C088 pavilion simile (C014-I02), C090 four bases of power (C075-I02), C090 Devadatta's transformation (C019-I01).
- Within the batch, C092-I01 and C092-I02 both concern how death is brought home. They are kept separate because I01 is about relationship-dependent reactions and I02 about removing an imagined exemption through comparison with the great.

## Held questions

1. **C037-I01 — Needing another's voice for the first step.** Margaya C037 line 39 says that, apart from Buddhas and Paccekabuddhas, no one can produce the stream-entry path without hearing the teaching from another, while later paths need no help. Can this narrow soteriological claim, stated inside an argument about formless beings, be carried into ordinary learning as a limited analogy? Needs a coordinator or reviewer fidelity decision.
2. **C036 death-proximate objects** (Pradeepika C036 lines 163–187; Margaya C040 line 17). Rejected here as rebirth mechanics. If a later concept on death-proximate processes offers a non-rebirth human reading (what surfaces in memory when facing death), revisit there rather than here.
3. **C040 two criteria for object strength.** Margaya C040 line 5 (size, light, nearness) and line 7 (mind-moments elapsed before impact) are different explanations that the source does not reconcile. C040-I01 relies mainly on line 5 and flags line 7.
